- a complete and unconditional **renunciation of all exclusive economic, control,
  custodial, and enforcement privileges** related to S.V.E.;

No ownership, veto, licensing monopoly, or special enforcement rights are reserved
to the author.

---

S.V.E., including its licenses, protocols, economic mechanisms, and enforcement logic,
is hereby **dedicated to Humanity as a whole**, in perpetuity.

This dedication is:
- universal,
- non-revocable,
- non-transferable,

S.V.E. exists as a **public ethical and economic commons**.

---

than any other verified S.V.E. Counsel, Custodian, Auditor, or Representative.

- this License,
