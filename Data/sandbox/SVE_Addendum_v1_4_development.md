## Article I — Economic Enforcement Engine  
### (The Recovery Ladder)

This License establishes an autonomous economic enforcement mechanism designed to make
systemic violations economically irrational.

### I.1 Recovery Ladder (Claims ≤ USD 1,000,000,000)

For the first USD 1B of any recovery (judgment, settlement, disgorgement):

| Enforcement Phase | Counsel Share | S.V.E. Fund Share |
|------------------|--------------|-------------------|
| Initial Success  | 25 %          | 75 %              |
| After 1st Loss   | 67 %          | 33 %              |
| After 2nd Loss   | 80 %          | 20 %              |
| After 3rd Loss   | 90 %          | 10 %              |

Each failed attempt to suppress enforcement increases the economic incentive
for continued action.

### I.2 Global Partner Rule (Claims > USD 1,000,000,000)

For any portion of recovery **exceeding USD 1B**:

> **Mandatory split: 50 % Counsel / 50 % S.V.E. Fund**,  
> independent of the Recovery Ladder phase.

This rule is immutable.

## Article II — The Protocol of Informants  
### (The 4 % Rule — Priority of Truth)

### II.1 Priority Deduction

A fixed **4 % of the total recovery amount** is deducted **before any other calculation**.

This amount is reserved exclusively for **Messengers of Truth** (informants).

### II.2 Collective Truth

If multiple informants provide materially relevant evidence:
- the 4 % allocation is **split equally by default**;
- no seniority or hierarchy applies unless unanimously agreed.

### II.3 Divine Escrow

If no informant exists:
- the 4 % is placed into **Divine Escrow**;
- a verifiable random coin toss determines allocation:
  - **Heads:** awarded to Counsel as a High-Difficulty Investigation Bonus;
  - **Tails:** transferred to the S.V.E. Fund.

This mechanism is non-discretionary.

## Article III — S.V.E. Family Status & PEMY Integration

### III.1 Induction into the S.V.E. Family

Any Counsel who formally completes **at least one S.V.E. enforcement case**
(reaching final judgment or settlement, regardless of outcome)
is inducted into the **S.V.E. Family**.

### III.2 Rights of Family Members

Family members acquire:
- voting rights in the **Veche (DAO governance)**;
- participation in global S.V.E. economic yield via **PEMY**;
- access to shared legal-intelligence infrastructure.

### III.3 PEMY Default Distribution

Unless explicitly overridden by public resolution,
PEMY yield distribution follows the invariant:

> **25 % Parent / 25 % Elderly / 25 % Middle / 25 % Young**

This rule applies ecosystem-wide.

## Article IV — Repentance Protocol & Tier-Y (Junior)

### IV.1 Exclusion

Any Counsel who violates:
- transparency obligations,
- audit requirements,
- or economic integrity rules,

is immediately:
- expelled from the S.V.E. Family;
- stripped of all dividend and governance rights.

### IV.2 Repentance & Reinstatement

A banned Counsel may return only as **Tier-Y (Junior)** under the following conditions:
- permanent **10 % compensation cap**;
- continuous audit eligibility;
- per-case allocation subject to verifiable random approval (“coin audit”).

Restoration to full Family status requires demonstrable net contribution
to S.V.E. integrity and knowledge.
