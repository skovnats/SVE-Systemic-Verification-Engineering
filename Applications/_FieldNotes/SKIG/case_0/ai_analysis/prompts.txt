# ✅ **1) AI Prompt (for each individual model)**

**Purpose:** produce one structured table + analytical commentary.
**Context:** conflict decision-makers often face asymmetric personal risks; “skin in the game” clarifies incentives and moral distance.

---

## **📌 AI Prompt (copy–paste)**

**You are one independent analytical model. Your task is to produce:
(1) a structured table and
(2) a short analytical explanation.**

**Context:**
We are evaluating the “incentive distance” between political decision-makers and the real human cost of war.
This is a purely analytical exercise (no judgement, no advocacy).
Quantitative values are *estimates*, based on publicly known information, patterns, plausibility, and comparative reasoning.

---

### **TASK 1 — Build the table**

Create a table with the following columns:

1. **Name**
2. **Start of Political Power (year)**
3. **End of Political Power (year)**
4. **Skin in the Game (0–100)** — personal exposure to war consequences
5. **Children – Skin in the Game (0–100)**
6. **Relatives – Skin in the Game (0–100)**
7. **Direct Role in the Conflict** *(peace-maker / escalator / ambiguous / neutral)*
8. **Number of Children of Draft Age**
9. **Days Personally Spent in High-Risk Zones**
10. **Direct Exposure to War Consequences (0–100)**
11. **Democratic Mandate Transparency** *(directly elected / indirectly elected / low-transparency mandate)*
12. **Combat Experience (yes/no + brief note)**
13. **Experience of Extreme Hardship** *(hunger, cold, poverty — 0–100 probability/strength)*
14. **Economic Vulnerability / Lived Poverty (0–100)**
15. **Connections to Global Power Networks** *(0–100: investment groups, elite institutions, corporate influence)*
16. **Estimated Responsibility Radius** *(0–100 self-calculated: how many people their decisions realistically affect)*
17. **Notes (2–3 lines max)**

Include the following individuals (if insufficient information, write “insufficient data” but still estimate):

* Angela Merkel
* Olaf Scholz
* Friedrich Merz
* Boris Johnson
* Emmanuel Macron
* Petro Poroshenko
* Ursula von der Leyen
* Heads of major military-industrial corporations (Lockheed Martin, Rheinmetall, BAE Systems, etc.)
* Add **5–10 additional globally relevant figures** if needed.

---

### **TASK 2 — Provide a short analytical explanation**

After the table, write **a short analysis (max 12 sentences)**:

* What patterns do you see?
* How asymmetric is the “risk distribution”?
* Are political decisions insulated from personal consequences?
* Which variables correlate the most with escalation vs. de-escalation?
* Which individuals have the lowest vs. highest incentive alignment with ordinary civilians?

Keep the tone neutral, analytical, non-political.

---

## **OUTPUT FORMAT**

1. **TABLE (markdown)**
2. **SHORT ANALYSIS (bullet points + 1 summary paragraph)**

# ✅ **2) Meta-AI Prompt (final synthesis)**

**Purpose:** combine all AI tables + analyses and produce one consolidated “master table” and high-level synthesis.

---

## **📌 Meta-AI Prompt (copy–paste)**

**You are Meta-AI.
You do NOT generate your own estimates.
You ONLY integrate and reconcile the outputs from multiple independent AI models.**

**Input:**
You receive multiple tables and analyses created by different AI systems.

**Your tasks:**

### **TASK 1 — Aggregate Tables**

* Merge all tables into one unified *Master Table*.
* When values differ, compute:

  * **min**, **max**, **mean**, **consensus category**,
  * and note “variance level” (low/medium/high).
* If some AIs disagree strongly, explicitly highlight the conflict.

### **TASK 2 — Meta-Analysis**

Produce a synthesis answering:

1. **Cross-model consensus** — where all AIs agree (patterns, clusters).
2. **Cross-model divergence** — where AIs differ significantly (ambiguities, contested data).
3. **Top systemic insights** — what the combined data suggests about:

   * incentive misalignment,
   * risk distribution,
   * decision-maker insulation,
   * correlation between personal exposure and political choices.

### **TASK 3 — Generate a “Risk–Ethics Asymmetry Index”**

For each individual, produce a final index (0–100):

* how asymmetric their personal risk is compared to the risk imposed on the public.

Explain in 3 sentences how the index is computed.

### **TASK 4 — Provide a Final Meta-Conclusion**

A short, neutral, non-political summary (max 10 sentences):

* What the entire multi-AI process reveals
* What the deepest structural insight is
* What remains uncertain or unknowable.

---

## **OUTPUT FORMAT**

1. **MASTER TABLE**
2. **META-ANALYSIS (bullets)**
3. **RISK–ETHICS ASYMMETRY INDEX**
4. **FINAL META-CONCLUSION**
