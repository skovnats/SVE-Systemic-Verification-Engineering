# Analysis of Results: The Categorical Cliff of Asymmetry

## Overview
The core finding: decision-making power vs. personal risk is **not linearly correlated**.  
Instead, it forms **distinct categorical clusters**, with a sharp “cliff” where authority separates from consequence—except for rare anomalies.

---

## 1. Statistical Evidence

### Methodology
- 5 AI systems used as expert panel:
  - Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, Qwen
- 14 global actors rated on a **0–100 SITG scale**

### Key Results
- **Correlation Test**
  - Pearson: `r = -0.04` → not significant  
  - ⇒ No smooth inverse relationship

- **Group Differences**
  - One-way ANOVA: `F = 19.3, p < 0.001`  
  - ⇒ Strong separation between categories

- **Effect Size**
  - Cohen’s d: `3.2 – 4.6`  
  - ⇒ Near-zero overlap between:
    - Tier 1: *Exposed*
    - Tier 3: *Insulated*

- **Variance Insight**
  - High agreement:
    - Tier 1: Zelenskyy, Netanyahu
    - Tier 4: Defense CEOs
  - High disagreement:
    - Tier 2/3: Putin, Western leaders  
  - ⇒ Signals **bias embedded in AI training data**

## 2. Convexity: Defense Profits vs. Conflict Severity

### Model Comparison
| Model        | R² Range     |
|--------------|-------------|
| Linear       | 0.47 – 0.55 |
| Quadratic    | 0.74 – 0.82 |

### Interpretation
- Returns are **convex**, not linear
- Profit **accelerates** at high conflict intensity (Severity 80–100)

### Mechanism
- Panic-driven procurement
- Stockpile depletion
- Long-term commitments (e.g., multi-decade programs)
  - Not incremental → structural escalation

---

## 3. Executive Downside Protection

### Structure: “Heads I Win, Tails I Still Win”

#### Upside
- 55–76% compensation in equity
- Captures convex gains from escalation

#### Downside Floor
- Guaranteed exit packages:
  - ~$15M – $55M
- Includes:
  - Severance
  - Accelerated vesting

### Implication
- **Asymmetric payoff structure**
- Incentives:
  - Benefit from conflict escalation
  - Avoid consequences of failure

### System Effect
- **Principal–Agent Problem**
  - Gains = convex (profits)
  - Losses = capped (personal insulation)
- ⇒ Bias toward **prolonged conflict**

---

## 4. The Zelensky Singularity

- Only actor where:
  - Decision-making power
  - Personal physical/existential risk  
  → are **co-located**

### Metric
- RAI ≈ 12

### Interpretation
- Represents the **exception**
- Highlights the rule:
  - Modern systems optimize for **decision-makers detached from consequences**

---

## Key Takeaway

Modern conflict systems exhibit:
- **Categorical separation** of power and risk
- **Convex profit incentives**
- **Asymmetric executive protection**

Result:
> Structural bias toward escalation with minimal accountability for decision-makers
