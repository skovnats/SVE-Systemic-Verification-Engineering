# Dark Place With Perhaps Lighted Candle

Drafts, raw notes, fragments, and early-stage explorations.

Unfiltered, analytically heavy, theologically layered, and at times artistically expressive.
This material captures thinking **before form**, **before refinement**, and **before full safety constraints**.

This is **not a final position**, **not a recommendation**, and **not an action plan**.

1. Descriptive, not prescriptive
2. Metaphorical / analytical, not literal
3. Explicitly excluded from conclusions and actions

---

## ⚠️ Warning

Content may be emotionally intense, spiritually confronting, or cognitively demanding.

**Not intended for general audiences.**
Read only with discernment, maturity, and critical distance.

---

## Author’s Note

There are cognitive and moral territories most people should **never enter**
—and should never need to know exist.

At times, a small number of people approach these areas deliberately,
not out of fascination, ideology, or aggression,
but to understand **how destructive systems operate**,
so others do not have to encounter them directly.

This folder exists as a **quarantine space** for such exploration.

Think of it as **infrastructure maintenance**, not construction:
some work exists to prevent failure, not to produce outcomes.

---

## Scope and Limits

❌ No calls to action

✅ Analysis, metaphor, thought experiments
