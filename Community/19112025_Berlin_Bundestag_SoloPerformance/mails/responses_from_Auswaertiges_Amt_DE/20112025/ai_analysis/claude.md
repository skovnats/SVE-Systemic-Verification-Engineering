- **If they answer substantively:** 
  - May expose policy contradictions
  - May create legal precedent
  - May require inter-departmental coordination
  - May need ministerial approval
  - May generate follow-up questions requiring more work

- **If they don't answer:**
  - One person is dissatisfied
  - May generate bad PR if escalated (low probability)
  - No internal process requirements
  - No legal exposure

**Result:** Strong institutional incentive to provide **process** without **substance**.
