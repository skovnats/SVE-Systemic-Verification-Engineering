It is important to clarify the foundation of my worldview,
because it explains why my questions focus not on geopolitics,
but on **living human beings**.

### **I do not think in categories of “states”.**

States rise, fall, merge, collapse, and transform:

* **FRG (Bundesrepublik Deutschland)** and **GDR (Deutsche Demokratische Republik)** existed as two distinct states — and then the GDR ceased to exist through absorption.
* **USSR** existed — and then ceased to exist.
* **Russian Empire**, **Austro-Hungarian Empire**, **Ottoman Empire**, **Yugoslavia**, **Czechoslovakia** — all dissolved.
* **Ukraine** has undergone **multiple constitutional ruptures, revolutions, and shifts of legitimacy** (1991 → 2004 → 2014 → wartime emergency configuration).

History teaches a simple truth:

**States are temporary human constructs.
Human life is not.**

A state exists to serve the human being.
Not the other way around.

The dignity of the person is absolute.
Geopolitical narratives are secondary and transient.

This is why I did not ask about geopolitics.
I asked about:

* the right not to be forced into war,
* the protection of civilians from coercion,
* human dignity,
* and transparent historical accountability.

These transcend state borders.
