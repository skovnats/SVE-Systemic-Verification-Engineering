# ✅ **I. PROMPT for AI Models (Primary Analysis)**

Analyze the following political communication artifact.

INPUT:
• Screenshot from the German Federal Foreign Office.
• Headline: “Justice and Humanity.”
• Text: “Returning abducted Ukrainian children to their families. Addressing the indoctrination and militarization of Ukrainian children, and calling for Russia’s accountability.”

TASKS:
1. Identify the explicit message.
2. Identify the implicit message(s).
3. Identify the emotional framing.
4. Identify the moral framing.
5. Identify the political framing.
6. Identify rhetorical devices used to shape public perception.
7. Identify what is *not* being said that is structurally relevant.
8. Identify which audience segments this message is targeted at.
9. Identify possible strategic purposes behind this communication.
10. Identify contradictions or tensions when compared to universal human-rights principles (gender equality, mobility rights, right to refuse participation in war).

Your output must be:
– concise,
– analytical,
– free of moral judgement,
– strictly based on the structure of the communication itself.

---

# ✅ **II. PROMPT for META-AI (Second-Layer Analysis)**

You are the meta-analyst.

INPUT:
Below are multiple independent AI analyses of the same political narrative artifact.
Your task is to synthesize them into a unified, structured understanding.

TASKS:
1. Identify points of agreement across models.
2. Identify points of divergence.
3. Extract deeper patterns that individual models missed.
4. Identify systemic signals about:
   – narrative construction,
   – power asymmetry,
   – strategic communication goals,
   – moral positioning vs. operational behavior.
5. Identify how the message functions within:
   – international diplomacy,
   – domestic political legitimacy,
   – public psychological framing.
6. Identify contradictions between the message and:
   – universal human-rights principles,
   – institutional behavior in other contexts.
7. Produce a distilled synthesis:
   – 10–15 bullet points,
   – neutral,
   – suitable for weekly archival documentation.

Do not add opinions. Work only with structural logic and patterns present in the input.

---

# ✅ **III. PROMPT for FINAL SYNTHESIS (for weekly report)**


**Prompt:**

Produce a final 6–10 point synthesis of the meta-analysis for archival purposes.

Requirements:
– neutral tone,
– no emotional or accusatory language,
– no speculation about intentions,
– describe only observable narrative structures,
– highlight structural tensions between stated values (“Justice and Humanity”) and known human-rights universals,
– output must be suitable for long-term public documentation.

Keep it concise and strictly analytical.
