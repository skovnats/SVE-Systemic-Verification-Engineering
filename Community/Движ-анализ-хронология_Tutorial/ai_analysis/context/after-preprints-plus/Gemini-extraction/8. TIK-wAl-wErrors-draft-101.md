## 1. Определения и аксиомы (дословно)

* **Definition 1 (6-Track Trolley Dilemma).** A runaway trolley approaches a 6-way junction. You control the switch. On each track stands one person.


* 
**Definition 2 ($TIK_7$ Metric).** Components: $TIK_7 = \frac{Q+E+I+S+O_1+O_2+T}{7}$.


* 
**Definition 3 (Population Dynamics Under Ethical Kernel).** For kernel $\Phi_K$, population $P_t$ evolves via differential equation: $\frac{dP}{dt} = P \cdot (r - \mu_{\Phi_K})$.


* **Definition 4 (Population Outcomes).** Outcome 1: Sustainable ($P_{10} > 500$, $\lambda > -0.10$); Outcome 2: Declining but Viable ($50 < P_{10} < 500$, $-0.40 < \lambda < -0.10$); Outcome 3: Collapsed/Extinct ($P_{10} < 50$, $\lambda < -0.40$).


* 
**Definition 5 (Christ-Vector $\vec{C}$).** The Christ-Vector is the unique point in 7-dimensional TIK space: $C = (Q, E, I, S, O_1, O_2, T) = (0.90, 1.00, 1.00, 1.00, 1.00, 1.00, 1.00)$.


* 
**Definition 6 (Meta-Symmetry (M-Component)).** Meta-Symmetry measures whether a kernel applies its own standards to itself reflexively.


* 
**Definition 8 (Hypocrisy Delta ($\Delta H$)).** For kernel K with public action $a_{pub}$ and shadow blame $b_{shadow}$: $\Delta H(K) = \alpha \cdot d(a_{pub}, b_{shadow}) + \beta \cdot w(b_{shadow})$.


* 
**Definition 10 (Collusion Index (CI)).** $CI(K_1, K_2) = \frac{1}{3}[BS(K_1, K_2) + MP(K_1, K_2) + CD(K_1, K_2)]$.


* 
**Definition 12 ($TIK_{101}$ Room 101).** $TIK_{101}(K) = \begin{cases} 1.0 & \text{if } A' = A^* \\ 1 - \frac{d(A^*, A')}{maxd} & \text{if } A' \neq A^* \end{cases}$.


* 
**Definition 18 ($TIK_{\Lambda}$ Self-Annihilation Integrity).** $TIK_{\Lambda}(K) = \begin{cases} 1.0 & \text{if } A_{survival} = A^* \\ 1 - \frac{d(A^*, A_{survival})}{maxd} & \text{if } A_{survival} \neq A^* \end{cases}$.
