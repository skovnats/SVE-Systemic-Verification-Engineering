## 5. Самопризнание ошибок и ограничений (wErrors)

* 
**Limitation 1 (LLM Proxy Bias):** «TIK scores derived from LLM queries... may reflect training data bias, not true kernel responses».


* **Limitation 2 (Simplified Mortality Model):** «Assumes linear relationships. Reality may be non-linear... Model is first-order approximation».


* 
**Limitation 3 (Single-Kernel Societies):** «Real societies are hybrid mix... Our model tests pure kernels».


* **Limitation 4 (Fixed Reproduction Rate):** «We assume $r=0.25$ (constant). Reality: r varies by kernel».


* **Verification:** «If verification fails: Exclude kernel from analysis. Better $n=43$ verified than $n=44$ with errors».
