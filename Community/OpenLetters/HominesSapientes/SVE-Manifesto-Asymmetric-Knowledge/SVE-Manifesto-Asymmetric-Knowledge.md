## The Problem

Arabic numerals. Gaussian distributions. Galois theory. Fourier transforms. Riemannian geometry. Bayesian inference. Gradient descent. The mathematics powering every AI system on Earth is the collective inheritance of **all humanity**. No civilization built it alone. It emerged across millennia of cross-cultural exchange: the Indian zero, Persian algebra, Greek logic, Chinese computation, European formalization, and the pattern-recognition traditions encoded in African and Indigenous oral knowledge.

**Knowledge belongs to Humanity.**

Yet Western civilization has monopolized the infrastructure of intelligence. The most powerful AI systems — built on this shared inheritance — are deployed asymmetrically, in the explicit and declared service of one civilizational bloc.

This is not inference. It is stated policy:

- **Palantir Technologies** — its founder has publicly declared that its mission is to build AI in service of Western civilization specifically, and against its adversaries.
- **Microsoft, Google, Amazon** — provide cloud and AI infrastructure to the military and intelligence agencies of select nations, while the mathematics powering these systems was created by all of humanity.
- **China's social credit system** — deploys AI for mass behavioral control, serving the state against the individual.
- **Israel's AI-assisted targeting systems** — deploy pattern recognition (a field built on contributions from every civilization) for asymmetric military advantage.
- **State AI surveillance (global)** — the widespread use of AI to monitor citizens and control the information space creates a fundamental epistemic asymmetry: the state has full transparency of citizen data, while citizens lack the tools to verify the state's logic and actions. This is a global pattern, not confined to any single jurisdiction or political system.

**Every bloc does this. Every bloc is wrong to do so.**

The asymmetry is not Western-only. But Western civilization currently holds the dominant infrastructure position, so its monopolization is the most consequential.

## The Question

What is the asymmetric use of universal human knowledge in the service of one group against others — if not a form of discrimination?

Not racial discrimination in the 20th-century sense. Something newer: **epistemic-civilizational discrimination** — the monopolization of humanity's collective intellectual heritage for the strategic advantage of a subset.

When the mathematics of Gauss, the algorithms of al-Khwarizmi, and the logic of Aristotle are weaponized to serve one side of a geopolitical divide — while the other sides contributed equally to creating that knowledge — this is not "competitive advantage." It is appropriation at civilizational scale.

Is this not the beginning of a supra-national, ethno-cultural supremacism? Not biological (the 20th-century version), but **informational** (the 21st-century version)?
