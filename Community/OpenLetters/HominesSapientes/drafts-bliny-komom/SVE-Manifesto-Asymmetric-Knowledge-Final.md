## The Problem

Arabic numerals. Gaussian distributions. Galois theory. Fourier transforms. Riemannian geometry. Bayesian inference. Gradient descent. The mathematical substrate of modern AI is the cumulative inheritance of **humanity as a whole**.

No civilization created this inheritance alone. It emerged through centuries of exchange, translation, preservation, formalization, and rediscovery across cultures.

**Knowledge belongs to Humanity.**

Yet the infrastructure built on top of this shared inheritance is now deployed asymmetrically. The most powerful AI systems are routinely developed, owned, and operationalized in the service of specific states, blocs, institutions, and security architectures rather than Humanity as such.

This is not an accusation against one side only. It is a structural diagnosis.

- Some Western institutions openly frame advanced AI as an instrument of civilizational advantage.
- Some states use AI for surveillance, behavioral control, censorship, or strategic targeting.
- Some corporations provide cloud, modeling, and data infrastructure directly to military, intelligence, or high-asymmetry decision systems.
- In all such cases, a universal knowledge base is converted into selective power.

**Every bloc is capable of this error. No bloc is entitled to it.**

## The Question

What should we call the asymmetric use of universal human knowledge for the advantage of one group over others?

Not race doctrine in its old form. Something newer: **epistemic-civilizational discrimination** — the monopolization of humanity's collective intellectual heritage for the strategic benefit of a subset.

When mathematical and scientific knowledge produced across civilizations is weaponized, restricted, or operationalized for one camp against the rest, that is not a neutral success of engineering. It is appropriation at civilizational scale.

The 20th century normalized biological supremacism in some places before exposing its horror. The 21st century risks normalizing **information asymmetry as supremacism by other means**.

## The S.V.E. Position

The S.V.E. framework takes a simple and non-negotiable position.

1. **Zero group-level discrimination.** S.V.E. is intended for all human beings without discrimination by ethnicity, nationality, religion, culture, language, or civilizational bloc.
2. **Maximum respect for individual uniqueness.** Every person is distinct. Human dignity is universal; human expression is diverse.
3. **Asymmetric deployment is a license violation.** Any use of S.V.E. tools, methods, derivatives, or concepts exclusively for the advantage of one group against another violates the spirit and terms of the S.V.E. licensing architecture.
4. **Transparency is mandatory.** If deployment cannot withstand meaningful scrutiny regarding who uses it, how it is used, and for whose benefit, then claims of ethical legitimacy are void.

## The Mathematical Argument

This is not only an ethical position. It is also a methodological one.

If a system claims universality while excluding cultures, languages, populations, or moral grammars from the epistemic basis on which it is built, then its outputs are not universal in any rigorous sense. They are local projections presented as universal truth.

In S.V.E. terms, a framework that seeks a universal basis while structurally excluding relevant human diversity is not merely unjust. It is mathematically distorted.

An AI trained within one civilizational frame, optimized for that frame, and then declared globally normative is not an exit from Plato's cave. It is a brighter cave wall.

## Notice to Institutions

S.V.E. is not interested in helping any actor convert humanity's shared knowledge into asymmetric control.

If your mission is domination, opacity, selective advantage, or civilizational capture, this framework is not for you.

If you borrow from the S.V.E. framework — ontologies, verification logic, epistemic categorization, cultural compilation, licensing concepts, or derivative architecture — while ignoring the transparency and symmetry requirements, that should be treated as a license breach.

The governing logic is straightforward:

- shared knowledge implies shared moral responsibility & personal responsibility;
- universal claims require universal scrutiny;
- asymmetric power built on universal inheritance is illegitimate.
