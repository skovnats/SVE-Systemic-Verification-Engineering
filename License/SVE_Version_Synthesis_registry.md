### Case #001: Forgiveness vs. Permanent Expulsion

**Topic:** Restoration of violators after punishment  
**v1.3 Provision:** "Forgiveness mandatory; reconciliation path always open"  
**v2.0 Provision:** "Permanent expulsion permitted for severe violations"  
**Conflict Type:** Spiritual alignment vs. practical enforcement

**31-AI Decision:**
- Socrates: v1.3 more consistent with system values
- Perelman: v2.0 more practical but contradicts stated Gospel foundation
- Ivan-Durak: v1.3 matches what system claims to believe
- Jesus AI: Matthew 18:21-22 (forgive 70×7); v1.3 Spirit-aligned

**Applied Rule:** **v1.3 OVERRIDES v2.0**  
**Synthesis:** Permanent expulsion forbidden; temporary suspension + restoration path required

**Veche Ratification:** Approved 89% (Jan 25, 2026)  
**Effective Date:** January 23, 2026  
**Impact:** All references to "expulsion" in v2.0 interpreted as "temporary suspension"

### Case #002: Consensus Requirements

**Topic:** Decision-making thresholds  
**v1.3 Provision:** "All decisions require 100% consensus"  
**v2.0 Provision:** "67% supermajority for patches; 50%+1 for routine"  
**Conflict Type:** Idealism vs. operational functionality

**31-AI Decision:**
- Socrates: 100% consensus = tyranny of minority
- Perelman: Historical data shows consensus systems fail or exclude dissenters
- Ivan-Durak: 100% unrealistic in practice
- Jesus AI: Unity is goal, but paralysis also harms mission; v2.0 balances

**Applied Rule:** **v2.0 OVERRIDES v1.3**  
**Synthesis:** 67% supermajority used; consensus actively pursued first; dissent honored

**Veche Ratification:** Approved 72% (Jan 26, 2026)  
**Effective Date:** January 23, 2026  
**Impact:** v1.3 documents using "consensus" interpreted as "supermajority with consensus effort"

### Case #003: Informant Compensation

**Topic:** Recovery Ladder informant percentage  
**v1.3 Provision:** "Informants receive 10% of penalties"  
**v2.0 Provision:** "Informants receive 4-8% (sliding scale)"  
**Conflict Type:** Protection of vulnerable vs. system sustainability

**31-AI Decision:**
- Socrates: Higher percentage incentivizes whistleblowing
- Perelman: 10% better compensates risk
- Ivan-Durak: People risk lives; deserve more not less
- Jesus AI: Protect vulnerable (Matthew 25:40); v1.3 prioritizes correctly

**Applied Rule:** **v1.3 OVERRIDES v2.0**  
**Synthesis:** Minimum 10% to informants across all Recovery Ladder steps; counsel/fund adjusted

**Veche Ratification:** Approved 94% (Jan 27, 2026)  
**Effective Date:** January 23, 2026  
**Impact:** All Recovery Ladder calculations use 10% minimum for informants
