## §2 — Compensation (Escalating Share Model)

Attorney share **increases** with violator's resistance:

| Phase | When | Attorney % | SVE % |
|-------|------|-----------|-------|
| **Pre-litigation** | Violator corrects after notice | 25% | 75% |
| **Filing** | Formal complaint filed | 40% | 60% |
| **Discovery** | Past discovery phase | 55% | 45% |
| **Trial** | Goes to trial | 70% | 30% |
| **Appeal** | Violator appeals | 90% | 10% |

**Rationale:** Truth-seekers settle fast. Resistors reveal guilt through obstruction.

**Example:** $10M penalty at appeal → Attorney: $9M / SVE: $1M.

**When victims exist:**
- 100% of penalty recovery goes to victims (Appendix F §2)
- Attorney share (25–90%) is paid **additionally** by the violator — SVE takes nothing
- Violator pays: penalty + attorney share

**When no victims** (e.g., pure IP theft): standard allocation per Appendix B §4.

### Special: Pharma Cases (Enhanced Incentive)

Base share: 25–90% (escalating), **plus bonuses:**
- Patient deaths caused → +10%
- Intentional suppression → +20%
- Systemic (multiple drugs/trials) → +30%
- **Attorney share capped at 95%** (SVE always retains minimum 5%)

**Why:** Patient lives > money.

### Special: Science Cases (Witch Hunt Prevention)

*Academic/research violations only:*
- **Win** → Standard share (25–90%)
- **Lose** → You pay all costs (court fees, defendant's legal fees)

**Exception:** If defendant is pharma/biotech (not an individual academic) → no personal liability on loss.

**Why:** Prevents frivolous suits against struggling researchers. Only pursue if evidence is solid.
