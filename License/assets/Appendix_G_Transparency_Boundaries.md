## §1 — The Sacred Boundary (Personal Life is Inviolable)

**Individual and family life is sacred — SVE cannot demand personal information.**

> *"When you pray, go into your room and shut the door."* — Matthew 6:6

**Privacy zones (absolute protection):**
- Personal beliefs, thoughts, prayers
- Family relationships, marriage intimacy
- Medical history (unless directly affects job performance)
- Financial details (unless managing others' money)
- Home life and personal habits
- Children's information (always protected)
- Private communications (unless fraud is specifically suspected)

**SVE cannot demand transparency in these zones** — even with a coin flip.

**Exception:** If you voluntarily share (e.g., public testimony), that is your choice. No one can force disclosure.

## §2 — The Responsibility Threshold (When Privacy Yields to Accountability)

### Rule: Responsibility for Others = Accountability to Others

**If you manage only your own life (and/or within your family):**
- Privacy remains absolute
- Your decisions affect only you/your family → your business

**If your decisions critically affect other adults' lives:**
- Privacy in that domain yields to accountability
- Refusing transparency = grounds for demotion or removal
- Those whose lives depend on you have the right to verify trustworthiness

### What Counts as "Critical Responsibility"?

All four criteria must be met:

| Factor | Requirement | Example |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| **Scale** | ≥5 adults whose lives depend on your decisions | Managing 5+ employees |
| **Impact** | Decisions affect survival, safety, or major wellbeing | Budget, safety protocols, medical treatment |
| **Power asymmetry** | They cannot easily exit or override your decisions | Employees, patients, subordinates |
| **Duration** | Ongoing responsibility (not one-time) | Manager (continuous) vs. consultant (temporary) |

**Examples:**
```
✓ CEO (100 employees)      → Critical responsibility (transparent)
✓ Doctor (patients)        → Critical responsibility (transparent)
✓ Safety inspector         → Critical responsibility (transparent)
✓ Parent (minor children)  → Critical BUT family privacy applies (not SVE)
✗ Freelancer (own work)    → No critical responsibility (private)
✗ Team member (not leader) → No critical responsibility (private)
✗ Retired person           → No critical responsibility (private)
```

**Key distinction:**
- **Children:** Family privacy applies — parents accountable to God, not SVE (Deuteronomy 6:6–7)
- **Adults:** If you have power over adults → they have the right to verify you

### Types of Decisions Requiring Transparency

| Decision Type | Transparent? | Reason |
|---------------|-------------|--------|
| **Financial** (managing others' money) | ✓ Yes | Choices affect their survival |
| **Safety** (protocols, equipment) | ✓ Yes | Negligence could kill |
| **Medical** (treatment, diagnosis) | ✓ Yes | Lives depend on your accuracy |
| **Legal** (representing others) | ✓ Yes | Mistakes harm their rights |
| **Resource allocation** (who gets what) | ✓ Yes | Bias creates injustice |
| **Performance evaluation** (hire/fire) | ✓ Yes | Dishonesty ruins careers |
| **Strategic** (company direction) | Conditional | Only if affects survival |
| **Personal preferences** | ✗ No | Trivial, not critical |

**Rule:** If lying about this decision could seriously harm those depending on you → transparency required.

## §3 — Audit Mechanisms (How Transparency Works)

### What Transparency Means

**NOT:**
- Publishing home address, family photos, or private conversations
- Invasive surveillance or 24/7 monitoring
- Access to medical records unrelated to the role

**YES:**
- Lie detector test (before critical decisions)
- AI audit (scan work communications for fraud patterns)
- Financial transparency (if managing others' money)
- Decision rationale (explain why you chose X)
- Conflict of interest disclosure (who benefits from your decision)

### The Choice: Accept Audit or Step Down

```
Two options only:
1. Accept transparency (lie detector, AI audit, disclosure)
2. Decline → step down to a role with no critical responsibility

Cannot decline AND keep the position.
```

**Example:** CEO refuses lie detector before major layoff decision → board offers advisor role (no decision power). CEO chooses: accept audit OR step down.

### Demotion Path (If Audit Refused)

1. **Refusal** → person declines transparency mechanism
2. **Assessment** → evaluate lower positions available (no critical responsibility)
3. **Offer** → propose demotion (same organization, lower role, less power)
4. **Decision** → person accepts demotion OR exits

**Coin involvement:** If unclear whether demotion is appropriate, or if multiple lower positions exist → coin decides.

**Not punishment** — demotion matches responsibility level to transparency willingness. Compensation adjusted to new role market rate (per Appendix B).

### Audit Frequency

| Role Level | Frequency | Trigger |
|------------|-----------|---------|
| **Team lead** | Quarterly | Before major decisions only |
| **Manager** | Monthly | Routine + before critical decisions |
| **Director** | Bi-weekly | Routine + spot checks |
| **VP/C-Suite** | Weekly | Routine + real-time option for critical |

**Not retroactive** — covers current and future decisions, not entire past (unless fraud is specifically suspected).

### Audit Scope

```
✓ Decision rationale (why you chose X)
✓ Conflict of interest (do you benefit personally?)
✓ Information accuracy (are facts true?)
✓ Compliance with rules (following protocols?)

✗ Personal beliefs
✗ Family life (spouse, children, home)
✗ Medical history (unless affects job performance)
✗ Personal finances unrelated to role
```

**Audit must be proportional** — no fishing expeditions.

### Lie Detector Protocols

1. **Consent** — person agrees or steps down (cannot force)
2. **Scope** — questions limited to work decisions only
3. **Professional** — licensed examiner, not organization employee
4. **Recording** — full session recorded, IPFS-hashed, reviewable
5. **Interpretation** — results reviewed by independent third party
6. **Privacy** — results confidential unless fraud found

Cannot be used for personal, belief, or hypothetical questions.

### AI Audit Details

**Scope:** Work communications only (email, Slack, work documents). NOT personal email, private messages, or family group chats.

**Process:**
1. AI scans → flags suspicious patterns
2. Human reviewer checks flagged items only
3. Fraud confirmed → evidence compiled; false alarm → discarded (not stored)

Cannot be used to monitor personal life, track family, or build dossiers for future use.

### Who Decides if Threshold Met?

1. **Self-assessment** → person evaluates own role
2. **Stakeholder input** → those affected vote (simple majority)
3. **Coin decision** → if disagreement → coin resolves

## §3A — Symmetric Audit Requirement (Radical Symmetry in Practice)

```
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHOEVER INITIATES AUDIT → UNDERGOES AUDIT SIMULTANEOUSLY
NO SELECTIVE, ASYMMETRIC AUDITS ALLOWED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

> *"Why do you see the speck in your brother's eye, but do not notice the log in your own eye?"* — Matthew 7:3

### Minimum 2, Possibly 3 Audited

Every audit includes:
1. **Initiator** (person demanding audit) → MUST be audited
2. **Target** (person being investigated) → audited
3. **Shadow Interest** (if target names a puppet master with credible evidence) → MAY be added

### Why Symmetry is Absolute

- Prevents weaponization (cannot attack without exposing self)
- Reveals puppet masters (hidden manipulators dragged into light)
- Discourages false accusations (must pass audit yourself)
- No exceptions — even whistleblowers, even victims, even founders

**If you're clean enough to judge others, you're clean enough to prove it.**

### The Shadow Interest Rule

If target suspects a puppet master:

1. Target names 1 person and provides credible evidence (emails, financial ties, coordination)
2. Evidence assessed by Elder Brothers or Veche
3. If credible → all 3 audited simultaneously

**Protection against false shadow claims:** If shadow person is cleared and no evidence existed → target pays Anti-Judas penalty (3×). Cannot weaponize shadow rule to drag random people in.

### Chain Depth

After a 3rd person is added: flip coin — Heads = add next person; Tails = stop. Auto-stop at 5 people (investigate deeper chain separately).

### Simultaneous, Not Sequential

All audits happen on the same day, in separate rooms, with the same protocol. Prevents coordination, document destruction, and prepared/cleaned versions.

### If Initiator Refuses Symmetric Audit

Audit request is **dropped**. Cannot proceed if initiator refuses symmetry. Persistent refusal = bad faith abuse of process → potential demotion or removal.

### Cost Allocation

| Outcome | Who Pays |
|---------|----------|
| Initiator clean, target guilty | Target pays 100% (initiator reimbursed) |
| Initiator guilty, target clean | Initiator pays 100% (target reimbursed) |
| Both clean | Organization pays (legitimate inquiry) |
| Both guilty | 50/50 |
| Shadow guilty, others clean | Shadow pays 100% (others reimbursed) |

Coin decides if cost allocation is disputed.

### Whistleblower Exception

Whistleblower is still audited (symmetry applies), but:
- Costs covered by organization (employee pays nothing)
- Scope limited to the specific accusation only
- Results confidential unless fraud is found

If whistleblower refuses even this limited audit → report not processed; anonymous reporting remains available (but carries less weight).

### Anonymous Tips vs. Formal Audits

```
ANONYMOUS TIP:
├─ Investigated quietly
├─ If evidence credible → escalates to formal audit
└─ If escalated → tipper must reveal identity + undergo symmetric audit

FORMAL AUDIT:
├─ Identity known from start
└─ Symmetric audit applies immediately
```

### Regulatory Audits (Government)

Government demands org audit → organization may invoke symmetry ("government agency audited too, per §3A"). If government already publishes budgets, decisions, and conflicts → symmetry is satisfied. If government refuses and law mandates compliance → organization's choice: comply noting the SVE violation, refuse (civil disobedience), or exit jurisdiction.

### Audit Duration

```
Maximum: 90 days (extendable twice by Veche 67% vote → max 270 days total)

Days 1–30:  Evidence gathering, interviews, documents
Days 31–60: Analysis, lie detector, AI audit
Days 61–90: Report writing, review, publication
```

If deadline missed: audit void; costs borne by initiator; must restart if still warranted.

**Exceptions for complex cases** (≥5 people, international scope, mass fraud) → Veche may grant longer timeline upfront.

### Examples

**Example 1: Clean Initiator, Guilty Target**
```
Board Member A suspects CEO embezzlement → symmetric audit.
Result: A clean, CEO guilty ($2M embezzled).
Outcome: CEO removed, A reimbursed, CEO pays penalties.
```

**Example 2: Puppet Master Revealed**
```
Board Member A accuses CEO. CEO names Board Member B as puppet master
(provides emails showing coordination).
3-way audit: A + CEO + B.
Result: A naive (used), B guilty (conspiracy), CEO clean.
```

## §4 — Exceptions & Edge Cases

### A. Emergency Situations

Crisis declared by 3+ stakeholders or Elder Brother → emergency audit authorized (limited scope, max 90 days). After crisis: confirm fraud (enforcement per Appendix F) OR clear person (privacy restored) OR extend via Veche vote (67%).

### B. Voluntary Transparency

Person may choose to be more transparent than required. **Protection:** Cannot be used against them later — voluntary disclosure is revocable.

### C. Marriage / Partnership Objection

Audit scope limited to work decisions only. If spouse still objects → person must choose: accept work audit (responsibility to employees) or step down (prioritize spouse's wishes). Cannot use family privacy to shield work fraud.

### D. Children (Always Protected)

Parenting decisions = family privacy (absolute). Exception: suspected child abuse is a separate legal matter outside SVE jurisdiction.

### E. Gradual Responsibility

| Role | People | Transparency Required |
|------|--------|-----------------------|
| **Individual contributor** | Self | None |
| **Team lead** | 5–10 | Lie detector for budget decisions |
| **Manager** | 10–50 | + AI audit of work communications |
| **Director** | 50–200 | + Financial disclosure |
| **VP** | 200–1,000 | + Quarterly deep audits |
| **CEO** | 1,000+ | + Real-time monitoring option |

Person can decline higher role if transparency requirement is uncomfortable.

### F. False Accusation Protection

Audit requires credible evidence or legitimate authority. Malicious demand with no evidence → denied. If false accusation proven → Anti-Judas Protocol (Appendix F §6): 3× penalty to false accuser.

### G. Whistleblower Protection (Retaliation)

If leader retaliates against whistleblower: immediate Appendix F one-bit violation; whistleblower compensated (lost wages + damages); leader removed.

### H. Audit Quality Standards

- Lie detector: licensed examiner (not organization employee)
- AI audit: verified tools (not custom/biased algorithms)
- Financial review: CPA or equivalent
- Auditor must be independent (no financial or family ties to either party); conflict of interest must be disclosed; coin decides if acceptable

### I. Appeal Process After Audit

30-day appeal window after results published. Must provide new evidence (not just disagreement). Veche vote (67%) or coin decides if re-audit warranted. Same symmetric rules apply if re-audit ordered.

### J. Audit Results Publication

- **Public:** Verdict (clean or guilty) + evidence summary
- **Confidential:** Personal details, family, medical, unrelated finances
- **Available to Veche on request:** Full report

### K. Cross-Border Coordination

Primary jurisdiction per Appendix F §5 (Germany → Switzerland → Estonia). Other jurisdictions cooperate via mutual legal assistance. If cooperation is impossible → audit limited to accessible jurisdiction.
