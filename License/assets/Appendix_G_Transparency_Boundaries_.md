## §1 - THE SACRED BOUNDARY (Personal Life is Inviolable)

### Core Principle

**Individual and family life is sacred — cannot demand personal information.**

**Biblical foundation:**
> *"When you pray, go into your room and shut the door and pray to your Father who is in secret."* — Matthew 6:6

> *"What God has joined together, let no man separate."* — Matthew 19:6

**Privacy zones (absolute protection):**
- Personal beliefs, thoughts, prayers
- Family relationships, marriage intimacy
- Medical history (unless affects job performance)
- Financial details (unless managing others' money)
- Home life, personal habits
- Children's information (always protected)
- Private communications (unless fraud suspected)

**SVE cannot demand transparency in these zones** — even with coin flip.

**Exception:** If you voluntarily share (e.g., public testimony), that's your choice. But no one can force disclosure.

## §2 - THE RESPONSIBILITY THRESHOLD (When Privacy Yields to Accountability)

### Rule: Responsibility for Others = Accountability to Others

**If you manage ONLY your own life (and/or within your family):**
- Privacy remains absolute
- No one can demand transparency in SVE context
- Your decisions affect only you/your family → Your business

**If your decisions CRITICALLY affect other adults' lives:**
- Privacy in that domain yields to accountability
- Refusing transparency = grounds for demotion or removal
- Those whose lives depend on you have right to verify trustworthiness

### What Counts as "Critical Responsibility"?

**Threshold criteria (ALL must be met):**

| Factor | Requirement | Example |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| **Scale** | ≥5 adults whose lives depend on your decisions | Managing 5+ employees, or 1 person's life fully |
| **Impact** | Decisions affect survival, safety, or major wellbeing | Budget, safety protocols, medical treatment |
| **Power asymmetry** | They cannot easily exit or override your decisions | Employees (need job), patients (need care), subordinates |
| **Duration** | Ongoing responsibility (not one-time) | Manager (continuous), vs consultant (temporary) |

**Examples:**

```
✓ CEO managing 100 employees → Critical responsibility (transparent)
✓ Doctor treating patients → Critical responsibility (transparent)
✓ Safety inspector (lives depend) → Critical responsibility (transparent)
✓ Parent with minor children → Critical BUT family privacy applies (no transparency)
✗ Freelancer managing own work → No critical responsibility (private)
✗ Team member (not leader) → No critical responsibility (private)
✗ Retired person → No critical responsibility (private)
```

**Key distinction:**
- **Children**: Family privacy applies (parents accountable to God, not SVE audits)
- **Adults**: If you have power over adults → They have right to verify you

### Types of Decisions Requiring Transparency

**Not all decisions, only critical ones:**

| Decision Type | Transparent? | Reason |
|---------------|--------------|--------|
| **Financial** (managing others' money) | ✓ Yes | Your choices affect their survival |
| **Safety** (protocols, equipment) | ✓ Yes | Your negligence could kill |
| **Medical** (treatment, diagnosis) | ✓ Yes | Lives depend on your accuracy |
| **Legal** (representing others) | ✓ Yes | Your mistakes harm their rights |
| **Resource allocation** (who gets what) | ✓ Yes | Your bias creates injustice |
| **Performance evaluation** (hire/fire) | ✓ Yes | Your dishonesty ruins careers |
| **Strategic** (company direction) | ✗ No | Unless affects survival (then yes) |
| **Personal preferences** (coffee brand) | ✗ No | Trivial, not critical |

**Rule:** If lying about this decision could seriously harm those depending on you → Transparency required.

## §3 - AUDIT MECHANISMS (How Transparency Works)

### What Transparency Means

**NOT:**
- Publishing home address, family photos, private conversations
- Invasive surveillance, 24/7 monitoring
- Access to medical records unrelated to job

**YES:**
- Lie detector test (before critical decisions)
- AI audit (scan communications for fraud patterns)
- Financial transparency (if managing others' money)
- Decision rationale (explain why you chose X)
- Conflict of interest disclosure (who benefits from your decision)

### The Choice: Accept Audit or Step Down

**If you hold critical responsibility position:**

```
Two options:
1. Accept transparency (lie detector, AI audit, disclosure)
2. Decline → Step down to lower position (no critical responsibility)

Cannot decline AND keep position.
```

**Example:**
- CEO refuses lie detector before major layoff decision
- Board: "Accept audit or step down to advisor role (no decision power)"
- CEO chooses: Accept audit OR step down
- Cannot refuse audit AND remain CEO

**Rationale:** Those whose lives depend on you have right to verify trustworthiness.

## §3A - SYMMETRIC AUDIT REQUIREMENT (Radical Symmetry in Practice)

### Core Rule: Auditor Must Be Audited

```
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WHOEVER INITIATES AUDIT → UNDERGOES AUDIT SIMULTANEOUSLY

NO SELECTIVE, ASYMMETRIC AUDITS ALLOWED

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

**Biblical foundation:**
> *"Why do you see the speck in your brother's eye, but do not notice the log in your own eye?"* — Matthew 7:3

> *"With the judgment you pronounce you will be judged, and with the measure you use it will be measured to you."* — Matthew 7:2

### Minimum 2, Possibly 3 Audited

**Every audit includes:**

1. **Initiator** (person/representative demanding audit) → MUST be audited
2. **Target** (person being accused/investigated) → Obviously audited
3. **Shadow Interest** (if target points to puppet master) → MAY be audited

**Example:**

```
Board Member A demands CEO be audited for fraud.

Symmetric Audit:
├─ Board Member A → Audited (lie detector, finances, conflicts)
├─ CEO → Audited (same scope)
└─ If CEO says "Board Member B told A to do this" 
    → Board Member B also audited (3rd person)
```

### Why Symmetry is Absolute

**Cannot demand transparency without providing it:**
- Prevents weaponization (can't attack without exposing self)
- Reveals puppet masters (hidden manipulators dragged into light)
- Enforces Core Principle #2 (Radical Symmetry)
- Discourages false accusations (must pass audit yourself)
- Levels playing field (powerful can't hide behind proxies)

**No exceptions:**
- Even if initiator is "lower level" → Still audited
- Even if initiator is whistleblower → Still audited (proves good faith)
- Even if initiator is victim → Still audited (verifies credibility)
- Even if "just asking questions" → If formal audit requested, symmetry applies

**Rationale:** If you're clean enough to judge others, you're clean enough to prove it.

### The "Shadow Interest" Rule

**If target suspects puppet master:**

**Process:**
1. Target being audited can name 1 person: "X is behind this, acting through initiator"
2. Must provide evidence (emails, conversations, financial ties showing coordination)
3. If evidence credible (Elder Brothers or Veche assess) → X added to audit
4. All 3 audited simultaneously: Initiator + Target + Shadow

**Example:**

```
Scenario: Board Member A demands CEO audit
CEO: "Board Member B is using A as proxy to attack me 
      because I rejected B's corrupt contract. 
      Here are emails showing B coordinated this."

If evidence credible:
├─ Board Member A → Audited
├─ CEO → Audited  
└─ Board Member B → Audited (shadow interest)

If all 3 clean: Resolve via other means
If A/B colluding: Conspiracy exposed
If CEO lying: CEO guilty of false accusation (Anti-Judas)
```

**Protection against false shadow claims:**
- Need credible evidence (not just accusation)
- If shadow person cleared + no evidence → Target pays Anti-Judas penalty (3x)
- Cannot weaponize shadow rule to drag random people into audit

### Chain Depth (How Deep to Go?)

**What if shadow has shadow?**

```
A demands B audit
B points to C (shadow)
C points to D (shadow's shadow)
D points to E...

How deep?
```

**Rule: Coin decides maximum depth**

**Process:**
1. After 3rd person added, ask: "Should we investigate deeper?"
2. Flip coin: Heads = Yes (add 4th person), Tails = No (stop at 3)
3. Repeat for each additional level
4. If chain exceeds 5 people → Automatic stop (too diffuse, investigate separately)

**Rationale:** Prevents infinite chains, but allows uncovering real conspiracies.

### Simultaneous, Not Sequential

**All audits happen AT SAME TIME:**

```
✓ Correct: All 2-3 people audited same day, same timeframe
✗ Wrong: Audit A first, then B later (allows cover-up)
```

**Why simultaneous:**
- Prevents coordination (can't warn each other)
- Prevents document destruction (no advance notice)
- Reveals real-time state (not prepared/cleaned version)
- Ensures symmetry (same conditions for all)

**Logistics:**
- Separate rooms (cannot communicate during audit)
- Same audit protocol (same questions, same rigor)
- Same timeline (both get lie detector, both get AI scan, both get financial review)
- Results compared (look for collusion or contradiction)

### If Initiator Refuses Symmetric Audit

**Consequences:**

```
Board Member A demands CEO audit.
CEO: "Fine, but you get audited too per §3A."
Board Member A: "No! I'm the auditor, I don't need to be audited!"

Result: Audit request DROPPED.
           Cannot proceed if initiator refuses symmetry.
           
If A persists: A guilty of bad faith abuse of process.
               Potential demotion or removal from board.
```

**Rationale:** If you won't submit to transparency, you cannot demand it from others.

**No "too important to audit" exception:** Even founder, even owner, even chairman → Symmetry is absolute.

### Cost Allocation

**Who pays for symmetric audit?**

**Default: Split equally**

```
2-person audit: Each pays 50%
3-person audit: Each pays 33.3%
```

**If audit reveals guilt:**

```
Initiator clean, Target guilty:
└─ Target pays 100% (initiator reimbursed)

Initiator guilty, Target clean:
└─ Initiator pays 100% (target reimbursed)

Both clean:
└─ Organization pays (was legitimate inquiry)

Both guilty:
└─ Both pay 50% each (both at fault)

Shadow guilty, others clean:
└─ Shadow pays 100% (others reimbursed)
```

**Coin decides:** If dispute over "who pays" → Coin determines allocation.

### Whistleblower Exception (Limited)

**Special case: Vulnerable whistleblower**

```
Low-level employee reports CEO fraud.
CEO: "Fine, but employee gets audited too per §3A."
Employee: "I can't afford audit, and I'm risking my job reporting this."
```

**Protection:**
1. Whistleblower still audited (symmetry applies)
2. BUT: Costs covered by organization (employee pays nothing)
3. AND: Audit scope limited (only relevant to accusation, not entire life)
4. AND: Results confidential unless fraud found (protects employee if clean)

**Why still audit whistleblower:**
- Verifies good faith (not malicious attack)
- Prevents false accusations (must be clean to accuse)
- Maintains symmetry (no one exempt)

**But with protection:**
- Cost-free (vulnerable person not financially punished for reporting)
- Limited scope (proportional to their role/accusation)
- Confidential (results not used against them if clean)

**If whistleblower refuses even this limited audit:**
- Report not processed (symmetry required)
- Can report anonymously instead (but less weight without verification)

### External Auditors Must Be Symmetric

**If external firm conducts audit:**

```
External audit firm must be SVE-compliant themselves

Requirements:
├─ Firm must have transparent operations
├─ Firm must submit to periodic SVE audits
└─ Cannot use opaque auditor to check transparent org

Why: Cannot demand transparency while providing secrecy
```

**If auditor refuses SVE compliance:**
- Organization can refuse their audit (no legitimacy)
- Must find SVE-compliant auditor instead

**Rationale:** Auditor claiming "trust us, we're professional" while hiding own operations = hypocrisy.

### Anonymous Tips vs Formal Audits

**Two paths for reporting:**

```
ANONYMOUS TIP:
├─ Investigated quietly (no formal audit yet)
├─ If evidence credible → Escalates to formal audit
└─ If escalates → Tipper must reveal identity + undergo symmetric audit

Cannot: Hide behind anonymity AND demand formal audit

FORMAL AUDIT REQUEST:
├─ Identity known from start
├─ Symmetric audit applies immediately
└─ Full process per §3A
```

**Protection for anonymous whistleblowers:**
- Identity protected during investigation phase
- Only revealed if evidence sufficient for formal audit
- If evidence insufficient → Remains anonymous, no consequences

**Rationale:** Anonymous tips allowed (protects vulnerable), but formal audits require symmetry (prevents weaponization).

### Regulatory Audits (Government Symmetry)

**If government/regulator demands organizational audit:**

```
Per Radical Symmetry: Government must ALSO be audited

Process:
├─ Govt demands org audit
├─ Org: "Fine, but govt agency gets audited too per §3A"
├─ If govt refuses → Org can refuse (unless law mandates)
└─ If law mandates + govt refuses symmetry → Civil disobedience case

Exception: If govt already transparent (publishes budgets, decisions, conflicts)
           → Symmetry already satisfied
```

**Reality:** Most governments will refuse symmetric audit.

**Organization's choice:**
1. Submit to asymmetric audit (comply with law, but note violation of SVE principles)
2. Refuse + civil disobedience (risk legal penalties, but maintain integrity)
3. Exit jurisdiction (operate where symmetry possible)

**SVE position:** Governments should be MOST transparent (they have most power). If they refuse symmetry, they're illegitimate auditors.

### Time Limits

**Audit duration:**

```
Maximum: 90 days from initiation to conclusion

Timeline:
├─ Days 1-30: Evidence gathering, interviews, document review
├─ Days 31-60: Analysis, lie detector, AI audit
├─ Days 61-90: Report writing, review, publication
└─ Day 91+: Must conclude or request extension

Extension:
├─ Requires Veche vote (67% to extend)
├─ Maximum 2 extensions (90 days each)
└─ Total maximum: 270 days (9 months)
```

**Why time limit:**
- Prevents indefinite investigation as harassment
- Forces efficient process
- Protects accused from prolonged uncertainty

**If deadline missed:**
- Audit automatically void (cannot be used)
- Must restart from beginning (if still warranted)
- Costs borne by initiator (penalty for delay)

**Exception: Complex cases**
- If ≥5 people audited (chain depth)
- If international scope (multiple jurisdictions)
- If massive fraud (years of documents)
→ Veche can grant longer timeline upfront

## §4 - EXCEPTIONS & EDGE CASES

### A. Emergency Situations

**Can temporarily demand transparency:**
- Active crisis (fire, disaster, imminent harm)
- Suspected fraud (evidence of deception affecting others)
- Whistleblower report (credible accusation requiring investigation)

**Process:**
1. Crisis declared (by 3+ stakeholders or Elder Brother)
2. Emergency audit authorized (limited scope, time-bound)
3. After crisis: Audit ends, privacy restored
4. If fraud found: Standard enforcement (Appendix F)

**Duration:** Maximum 90 days emergency audit, then must either:
- Confirm fraud (enforcement) OR
- Clear person (privacy restored) OR
- Extend via Veche vote (67%)

### B. Voluntary Transparency

**Person can CHOOSE to be more transparent than required:**
- Publish personal finances (even if not managing others' money)
- Share medical history publicly (even if irrelevant to job)
- Open home to inspection (even if private)

**Why?** Build trust, lead by example, demonstrate integrity.

**Protection:** Cannot be used against them later ("you shared before, must share now"). Voluntary = revocable.

### C. Marriage / Partnership Objection

**If spouse/partner objects to transparency:**

**Scenario:** CEO's spouse says "Lie detector violates our family privacy."

**Resolution:**
1. CEO explains: "This is for my work role, not family life"
2. Audit limited to work decisions only (not personal/family)
3. If spouse still objects → CEO must choose:
   - Accept audit (prioritize responsibility to employees)
   - Step down (prioritize spouse's wishes)

**Coin decides:** If couple deadlocks on whether work audit violates family privacy → Coin determines if audit is limited enough.

**Cannot use spouse objection to avoid accountability** — that's exploitation of family privacy to shield work fraud.

### D. Children (Always Protected)

**Parent decisions about children = family privacy (absolute).**

**Even if parent is CEO, doctor, etc.:**
- Cannot audit parenting decisions
- Cannot demand children's information
- Cannot surveil family life

**Exception:** If child abuse suspected (separate legal matter, not SVE jurisdiction).

**Rationale:** Parents accountable to God for children, not to SVE (Deuteronomy 6:6-7).

### E. Gradual Responsibility (Team Lead → CEO)

**Transparency requirement scales with responsibility:**

| Role | Responsibility | Transparency Required |
|------|----------------|----------------------|
| **Individual contributor** | Self only | None |
| **Team lead** (5-10 people) | Minor | Lie detector for budget decisions |
| **Manager** (10-50 people) | Moderate | + AI audit of communications |
| **Director** (50-200 people) | Significant | + Financial disclosure |
| **VP** (200-1000 people) | Major | + Quarterly deep audits |
| **CEO** (1000+ people) | Critical | + Real-time monitoring option |

**Principle:** More lives depend on you = more transparency required.

**Person can decline higher role** if transparency requirement uncomfortable.

### F. False Accusation Protection

**Cannot weaponize audit demands:**

```
Malicious actor: "I demand CEO submit to lie detector!"
Organization: "On what grounds?"
Malicious actor: "I just don't trust them."
→ Denied (no evidence, bad faith)

vs

Whistleblower: "I have evidence CEO misreported safety data."
Organization: "Submit evidence for review."
Whistleblower: [Provides documents showing discrepancies]
→ Emergency audit authorized (credible basis)
```

**Standard:** Need credible evidence or legitimate authority to demand audit.

**Cannot demand audit to harass** — that's abuse of transparency framework.

**Protection:** If false accusation proven (malicious, no basis) → Anti-Judas Protocol (Appendix F) applies (3x penalty to false accuser).

### G. Whistleblower Protection

**If subordinate reports leader's dishonesty:**

**Process:**
1. Report submitted (confidential to DVN or Elder Brothers)
2. Evidence reviewed (is accusation credible?)
3. If credible → Emergency audit authorized
4. Whistleblower identity protected (cannot retaliate)

**If leader retaliates:**
- Immediate violation (Appendix F one-bit rule)
- Whistleblower compensated (lost wages + damages)
- Leader removed (cannot hold responsibility position)

**Rationale:** Those under power have right to expose abuse without fear.

### H. Cross-Border Differences

**Cultural variations in privacy expectations:**

**Example:**
- Western cultures: Financial privacy expected
- Some cultures: Family finances shared openly

**SVE adapts:** If cultural norm is MORE transparent → No conflict. If cultural norm is LESS transparent → SVE requirement applies (critical responsibility = accountability regardless of culture).

**Cannot use "cultural difference" to hide fraud.**

## §5 - IMPLEMENTATION GUIDELINES

### Who Decides if Threshold Met?

**Three-step process:**

1. **Self-assessment** → Person evaluates own role (critical responsibility?)
2. **Stakeholder input** → Those affected by decisions vote (simple majority: is transparency needed?)
3. **Coin decision** → If disagreement (person says no, stakeholders say yes) → Coin decides

**Coin question:** "Does [Person]'s role meet critical responsibility threshold (≥5 adults, impact survival/safety, power asymmetry, ongoing)?"
- Heads = Yes (transparency required)
- Tails = No (privacy maintained)

### Audit Frequency

**Not constant surveillance — periodic verification:**

| Role Level | Audit Frequency | Trigger |
|------------|-----------------|---------|
| **Team lead** | Quarterly | Before major decisions only |
| **Manager** | Monthly | Routine + before critical decisions |
| **Director** | Bi-weekly | Routine + spot checks |
| **VP/C-Suite** | Weekly | Routine + real-time option for critical |

**"Real-time option"** means stakeholders can request emergency audit if suspicious behavior detected.

**Not retroactive:** Audit covers current/future decisions, not entire past (unless fraud suspected).

### What Audit Covers

**Limited scope (not everything):**

```
✓ Decision rationale (why you chose X)
✓ Conflict of interest (do you benefit personally?)
✓ Information accuracy (are facts true?)
✓ Compliance with rules (following protocols?)

✗ Personal beliefs (what you pray about)
✗ Family life (spouse, children, home)
✗ Medical history (unless affects job performance)
✗ Unrelated finances (personal investments if not conflict of interest)
```

**Audit must be proportional** — no fishing expeditions, only investigate relevant areas.

### Lie Detector Protocols

**If lie detector used:**

1. **Consent** → Person agrees or steps down (cannot force)
2. **Scope** → Questions limited to work decisions (no personal questions)
3. **Professional** → Licensed examiner, not organization employee
4. **Recording** → Full session recorded, IPFS hash, reviewable
5. **Interpretation** → Results reviewed by 3rd party (not just organization)
6. **Privacy** → Results confidential (only revealed if fraud found)

**Cannot use lie detector for:**
- Personal questions ("Do you love your spouse?")
- Belief questions ("Do you believe in God?")
- Hypotheticals ("Would you ever steal?")
- Fishing ("Tell us every mistake you ever made")

**Only for:** Specific work decisions under investigation.

### AI Audit Details

**If AI scans communications:**

**Scope:** Work communications only (email, Slack, work documents)

**NOT:** Personal email, private messages, family group chats

**Method:**
- Pattern detection (fraud indicators, concealment language)
- Anomaly detection (unusual behavior for this person)
- Cross-reference (do claims match evidence?)

**Privacy:** AI doesn't show human reviewer unless red flags detected.

**Process:**
1. AI scans → Flags suspicious patterns
2. Human reviewer checks flagged items only
3. If fraud confirmed → Evidence compiled
4. If false alarm → Discarded (not stored)

**Cannot use AI to:**
- Monitor personal life
- Spy on family
- Track unrelated activity
- Build dossier for future use

## §6 - BIBLICAL FOUNDATION

### Privacy as Sacred

> *"When you pray, go into your room and shut the door."* — Matthew 6:6

**Personal relationship with God is private.** No one can demand access to inner life.

### Accountability for Leaders

> *"An overseer must be above reproach."* — 1 Timothy 3:2

**Those in positions of authority held to higher standard.** Not because they're worse, but because more people depend on them.

### Proportional Responsibility

> *"To whom much is given, much will be required."* — Luke 12:48

**Greater responsibility = greater accountability.** This is not punishment, it's proportionality.

### Protection of Family

## §8 - REMAINING CONSIDERATIONS

### What May Still Need Refinement:

#### A. **Audit Quality Standards**
**Question:** What qualifies as "sufficient" audit?
**Proposal:** 
- Professional lie detector (licensed examiner, not amateur)
- AI audit by verified tools (not custom/biased algorithms)
- Financial review by CPA or equivalent
- Must meet minimum standards (to be defined by DVN)

#### B. **Conflict of Interest for Auditors**
**Question:** Can auditor have relationship with either party?
**Proposal:**
- Must be independent (no financial/family ties to either side)
- If conflict exists → Must disclose + Coin decides if acceptable
- Violation → Audit void + auditor banned from future SVE work

#### C. **Multi-Jurisdiction Coordination**
**Question:** What if audit spans multiple countries?
**Proposal:**
- Primary jurisdiction (per Appendix F §5: Germany → Switzerland → Estonia)
- Other jurisdictions cooperate via mutual legal assistance
- If cooperation impossible → Audit limited to accessible jurisdiction

#### D. **Appeal Process After Audit**
**Question:** Can audit results be challenged?
**Proposal:**
- 30-day appeal window after results published
- Must provide new evidence (not just "I disagree")
- Veche vote (67%) or Coin decides if re-audit warranted
- If re-audit ordered → Same symmetric rules apply

#### E. **Audit Results Publication**
**Question:** How much published? What stays confidential?
**Proposal:**
- Verdict public (clean or guilty)
- Evidence summary public (what was found)
- Personal details confidential (family, medical, unrelated finances)
- Full report available to Veche upon request (transparency within community)

These refinements can be added via future patches as experience reveals needs.
