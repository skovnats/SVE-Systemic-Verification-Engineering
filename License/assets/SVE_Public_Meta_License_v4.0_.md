## Legal Foundations (Prior Art)

Nothing in the SVE Meta-License is invented from scratch. Every mechanism has precedent in existing law, technology licensing, or international jurisprudence. This section maps each SVE innovation to its real-world ancestor — both to establish legitimacy and to mark where SVE goes further.

### Latent Activation → Clickwrap / Copyleft Precedents

The principle that "touching = accepting" is not new. Software licensing has operated on this basis for decades.

**GPL (1989–present):** Using GPL-licensed code in your project means your project inherits the license. No signature required. The act of use *is* the acceptance. SVE applies the same logic: use SVE methodology → SVE terms activate.

**Clickwrap case law:** Courts have upheld that clicking "I agree" constitutes binding acceptance (ProCD v. Zeidenberg, 1996). SVE goes one step further — there is no button. Usage itself is the act. This is closer to how GPL operates than to clickwrap, and GPL has survived decades of legal challenge.

**Where SVE goes further:** GPL governs code. SVE governs *methodology and its outcomes*. The activation extends not just to software but to any application of SVE verification principles.

### Precedent-Based Interpretation → Common Law Tradition

SVE-Cases function identically to judicial precedents in common law systems (UK, US, Australia). Each case interprets existing principles for a specific situation, and future cases build on prior ones.

**Where SVE goes further:** Traditional precedent is set by courts. SVE precedent is set by custodians + the 4+1 AI Oracle, and ratified by community. This is closer to how international arbitration panels work — authoritative but outside state courts.

### Living Document Doctrine → ECHR "Living Instrument"

The European Court of Human Rights interprets the 1950 Convention as a "living instrument" — adapting its meaning to present-day conditions without rewriting the text. SVE's fractal interpretation layer works the same way: Core Principles are fixed, but their *application* evolves.

**Where SVE goes further:** ECHR relies on human judges. SVE adds algorithmic consistency (4+1 Oracle) to reduce interpretive drift across eras and cultures.

### Share-Alike Integrity → Copyleft Lineage

SVE's requirement that all derivatives inherit the license mirrors copyleft (GPL, CC BY-SA). This is the most battle-tested mechanism in open licensing — 35+ years of legal precedent.

**Where SVE goes further:** Copyleft protects code freedom. SVE protects *ethical integrity*. A derivative that is technically open-source but ethically harmful still violates SVE (see Case-01).

### Enforcement Without State → Lex Mercatoria

*Lex mercatoria* (merchant law) has governed international trade for centuries without relying on any single state's legal system. Traders enforced rules through reputation, exclusion, and community arbitration. SVE operates on the same principle: enforcement through community consensus, public accountability, and economic incentives — functional regardless of jurisdiction.

### Superintelligence as Juridical Threat → The Horizon Case

This is where SVE has *no* prior art to cite — and acknowledges it honestly.

A sufficiently advanced AI will be able to read any license and find loopholes that no human lawyer would see. This is not speculative — current LLMs already assist in contract analysis. The trajectory is clear: adversarial AI will become a tool for circumventing legal frameworks, including SVE.

SVE's defense is structural, not textual:

1. **Spirit over Letter (Layer 2)** means loopholes in wording are irrelevant if the outcome violates Core Principles. An AI that finds a textual gap still cannot justify harm.
2. **4+1 Oracle as counter-AI** — the same technology that finds loopholes can be deployed to *detect* them. SVE's Oracle is designed to be upgraded as AI capabilities advance.
3. **Precedent accumulation (Layer 4)** — every AI-discovered loophole, once identified, becomes a new SVE-Case that permanently closes it. The immune system learns from every attack.
4. **The Ratchet Principle** — even if a superintelligent AI proposes an interpretation that technically satisfies every written rule, the cumulative protection clause ensures that the *strongest* reading across all versions prevails.

This is an open frontier. SVE does not claim to have solved the superintelligence alignment problem. It claims to have built an architecture that *can keep adapting* to it — which is more than any static license offers.
