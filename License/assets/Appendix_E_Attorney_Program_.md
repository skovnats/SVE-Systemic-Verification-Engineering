## §2 - COMPENSATION (Escalating Share Model)

**When victims exist:**
- Recovery distribution: 100% to victims (Appendix F §2)
- Attorney share: 25-90% (paid by violator, separate from victim recovery)
- SVE Foundation: 0% (we don't profit from victim suffering)

**When no victims (e.g., IP theft without harm):**
- Standard allocation: Per Appendix B §4

### Attorney Share Increases With Resistance

| Phase | When | Attorney % | SVE % |
|-------|------|-----------|-------|
| **Pre-litigation** | Violator corrects after notice | 25% | 75% |
| **Filing** | Formal complaint filed | 40% | 60% |
| **Discovery** | Past discovery phase | 55% | 45% |
| **Trial** | Goes to trial | 70% | 30% |
| **Appeal** | Violator appeals | 90% | 10% |

**Rationale:** Truth-seekers settle fast (cheap). Resistors reveal guilt through obstruction (expensive).

**Example:** $10M penalty at appeal → Attorney gets $9M, SVE gets $1M.

### Special: Pharma Cases (Enhanced Incentive)

**Base share:** 25-90% (escalating)

**PLUS Bonus:**
- Patient deaths caused → +10% bonus
- Intentional suppression → +20% bonus  
- Systemic (multiple drugs) → +30% bonus
- **Maximum total:** 95% to attorney, 5% to SVE

**Why:** Patient lives > money. Heavy rewards for pharma enforcement.

### Special: Science Cases (Witch Hunt Prevention)

**Academic/research violations:**
- If you win → Standard share (25-90%)
- If you lose → **YOU pay all costs** (court fees, defendant's legal fees)

**Exception:** If defendant is pharma/biotech company (not individual academic) → No personal liability.

**Why:** Prevents frivolous suits against struggling researchers. Only pursue if evidence solid.
