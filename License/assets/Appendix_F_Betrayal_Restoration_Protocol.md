## §1 — The One-Bit Rule (Zero Tolerance)

**Even 1 bit of dishonesty = relationship terminated.**

**"One-bit violation" means:**
- Hiding any data point (even one number in a dataset)
- Claiming transparency while concealing any process step
- Using SVE brand while violating any principle
- Reporting 99.9% truth while hiding 0.1%

> *"Whoever is faithful in very little is also faithful in much, and whoever is dishonest in very little is also dishonest in much."* — Luke 16:10

**Why zero tolerance:**
- Trust is binary: 100% trustworthy or not at all
- "Mostly honest" = dishonest (the hidden part poisons the whole)
- Allowing "small" violations creates precedent for incremental corruption

**Immediate consequences upon discovery:**
1. **Immediate termination** — All SVE rights revoked (no warning, no cure period)
2. **Public disclosure** — Violation published (Hall of Shame)
3. **Victim compensation** — See §2
4. **Permanent ban** — Cannot use SVE again unless repentance path completed (see §4)

**No exceptions.** Not for "good reasons," emergencies, or competitive pressure. Truth or nothing.

## §2 — Victim Compensation Protocol

### Trigger

If SVE brand was used or implied (through partnership, franchise, licensing, or endorsement) AND a violation occurred → full compensation to victims.

### Compensation Formula

```
Compensation = (All money from partnership)
             + (Additional damages: 1%–101% of partnership revenue)
             + (Legal costs)
             + (Opportunity costs)
```

**"All money from partnership"** means every amount received from day 1 to discovery date — including profits, fees, commissions, and revenue share. **No deductions allowed.**

**Additional damages:**

| Harm Level | Additional % | Example |
|------------|--------------|---------|
| **Minor** | 1–10% | Late disclosure, honest mistake covered up briefly |
| **Moderate** | 11–50% | Systematic concealment, multiple violations |
| **Severe** | 51–100% | Intentional fraud, patient harm, public danger |
| **Existential** | 101% | Deaths, mass harm, Mark-of-Beast scenarios |

**Decided by:** Veche vote (67%) or arbitration panel.

### Distribution

**Court cases (SVE lawyers involved):**
- Victim compensation: 100% of penalty
- Attorney fees: 25–90% of penalty (per Appendix E §2), paid additionally by violator
- SVE Foundation: 0%

**Example:** $10M penalty + $7M attorney share (70% at trial) → violator pays $17M total; victims receive $10M, attorneys $7M, SVE $0.

**Direct settlements (no court):**
- Victims: 90%
- SVE Foundation: 10% (administrative costs only)

**Why SVE takes 0% in court cases:** Money belongs to those harmed. Foundation exists to serve victims, not profit from their suffering.

### "All Money Back" Rule

If partnership used SVE brand/mark, victim has the right to demand **full refund of all payments made** — regardless of "value received."

**Rationale:** Brand trust was violated. Customer paid for "SVE-verified" (truth), received "SVE-labeled" (lie). That is fraud. **No "but we delivered value" defense.**

## §3 — The Coin (Conflict Resolution & Divine Judgment)

### When Coin Decides

The Coin (lot/sortition) resolves conflicts where:
- Both parties claim truth but facts are unclear
- Dispute involves judgment calls (not a clear violation)
- Repentance path decisions (see §4)
- Restoration after betrayal (return to normal rates?)

> *"The lot is cast into the lap, but its every decision is from the Lord."* — Proverbs 16:33

### How It Works

**Physical coin flip only** (not digital):
1. Witness present (video-recorded, IPFS-hashed)
2. Question stated clearly before the flip
3. Heads = Yes / Tails = No (agreed beforehand)
4. Result is **final and binding** — no appeals, no do-overs

**Why physical:** Physical quantum randomness removes human bias, bribery, and politics. Human judgment removed. God decides.

### Coin Does NOT Decide

- Clear facts (proven dishonesty = ban, no coin)
- Mathematical / logical facts
- Core Principles interpretation (that is the 31-AI Oracle's role)
- Safety decisions requiring immediate human action

**Rule:** If humans can determine truth/falsehood definitively, no coin needed. Coin decides only when humans deadlock on judgment.

## §4 — Repentance Path (Restoration After Betrayal)

### Eligibility

**Who can repent:** Anyone who violated SVE rules.

**Who cannot:**
- Those who caused deaths through intentional suppression (e.g., pharma hiding trial deaths)
- Those who committed "Mark of Beast" level violations (mass harm, systemic evil)
- Those who refuse to acknowledge wrongdoing (denial = no repentance)

### The Path

**Step 1: Public Confession (Day 1)**
- Full written confession: what was hidden, why, who was harmed
- Published on GitHub + IPFS (permanent record)
- No minimization, no excuses, no blame-shifting
- Statement: *"I betrayed trust. I accept consequences. I seek restoration."*

**Step 2: Victim Restitution (Months 1–12)**
- All money returned to victims (per §2)
- Additional damages paid (per §2)
- Public apology to each victim (if they consent to contact)
- Victim forgiveness is not required (but welcomed if offered)

**Step 3: Reduced-Rate Period**

The coin decides **each contract**: normal rate or reduced rate (25% of normal).
The person cannot refuse contracts — refusal terminates the path permanently.

| Duration | Culpability Level | Restoration Bonus |
|----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **11 years** | Sincere ignorance / honest mistake | +50% margin bonus |
| **22 years** | Negligence (should have known) | +70% margin bonus |
| **33 years** | Recklessness (knew risk, proceeded) | +90% margin bonus |
| **444 months (37 years)** | Intentional malice (planned deception) | Reduced rates only — no normal rate possibility |

*Duration is chosen at the start of the path and cannot be shortened (only extended by new violations).*

**Step 4: Completion & Restoration**

After completing the chosen duration:
1. **Full rights restored** — all previous SVE privileges return
2. **Margin bonus applied** (per table above; capped at 90% of total value)
3. **"Restored" badge** — public recognition (fell, repented, restored)

**Path terminates early (ban becomes permanent) if:**
- Second violation during repentance period
- Refusal of contract because coin gave reduced rate
- Documented continuation of dishonest behavior
- Attempted coin manipulation

**Repentance is offered once.**

## §6 — Case-by-Case Judgment (The Knight's Move)

No rigid rules for every scenario. Each case is judged individually, guided by three principles:

1. *"Let him who is without sin cast the first stone"* — John 8:7 (humility, mercy, awareness of our own failings)
2. *"The Prodigal Son Returns"* — Luke 15:11–32 (goal = restoration, not punishment)
3. *"Judge not, that you be not judged"* — Matthew 7:1–2 (the standard applied to others applies to us)

**The Coin is the supreme decider when humans cannot agree.**

### Culpability Gradations (Author's Guidance — Coin Can Override)

| Level | Description | Proposed Path |
|-------|-------------|---------------|
| **Sincere ignorance** | Didn't know rule existed; honest mistake | 11 years; coin decides rate per contract |
| **Negligence** | Should have known; careless, not intentional | 22 years; coin decides rate per contract |
| **Recklessness** | Knew risk, proceeded anyway | 33 years; coin decides rate per contract |
| **Intentional malice** | Planned concealment; calculated exploitation | 444 months; reduced rates only |
| **Premeditated evil** | Systemic harm (pharma deaths, mass casualties) | No repentance path — permanent ban |

> *"I recommend showing MERCY in cases of sincere ignorance, and MAXIMUM HARSHNESS in cases of intentional, malicious action. But even my recommendation is not final. The Coin decides. I am human. Coin is divine."*
> — Dr. Artiom Kovnatsky

### Standard Process for Any Unclear Case

1. Present scenario (facts, harm, culpability, mitigating factors)
2. Community input (Veche discussion, victim testimony, evidence review)
3. Author guidance if available (mercy or harshness recommended)
4. Formulate a clear binary question
5. Flip coin (physical, witnessed, video-recorded, IPFS-hashed)
6. Accept result — no appeals, no do-overs
7. Implement faithfully

### Key Edge Cases

**Coin manipulation prevention:** Minimum 3 witnesses required; affidavits signed; blockchain-timestamped.

**Corporate vs. individual:** Whistleblowers protected; executives held accountable; case-by-case culpability assessment.

**Second-generation consequences:** Children do not inherit ban (Ezekiel 18:20) and do not inherit restored status.

**Aggregate coin bias:** If >80% of flips yield reduced rates over 100+ cases, investigate coin integrity.

**Organizational repentance:** Leadership change required; transformation timeline applies. Employees who were whistleblowers or unaware are not penalized.

**Anonymous violations:** SVE pursues forensic investigation (Appendix A). Once identified, standard protocol applies. Anonymity does not prevent exposure.

**Victims who decline compensation:** Victim may donate to SVE or redirect funds — their choice, their compensation.
