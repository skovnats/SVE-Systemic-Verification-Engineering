## §1 - THE ONE-BIT RULE (Zero Tolerance)

### Principle

**Even 1 bit of dishonesty = relationship terminated.**

**"One-bit violation"** means:
- Hiding ANY data point (even one number in a dataset)
- Claiming transparency while concealing ANY process step
- Using SVE brand while violating ANY principle
- Reporting 99.9% truth while hiding 0.1%

**Biblical foundation:**
> *"Whoever is faithful in very little is also faithful in much, and whoever is dishonest in very little is also dishonest in much."* — Luke 16:10

### Why Zero Tolerance?

**Trust is binary:**
- You're either trustworthy (100%) or you're not (0%)
- "Mostly honest" = dishonest (the hidden part poisons the whole)
- Small lies reveal character willing to tell large lies

**Systemic reason:**
- Allowing "small" violations creates precedent
- System degrades incrementally ("just this once" → corruption)
- Zero tolerance = bright line (no gray area to exploit)

### Immediate Consequences

Upon discovery of ANY dishonesty:

1. **Immediate termination** — All SVE rights revoked (no warning, no cure period)
2. **Public disclosure** — Violation published (Hall of Shame)
3. **Victim compensation** — See §2
4. **Permanent ban** — Cannot use SVE again (unless repentance path completed, see §4)

**No exceptions.** Not for "good reasons," not for "emergencies," not for "competitive pressure."

**Truth or nothing.**

## §2 - VICTIM COMPENSATION PROTOCOL

### Trigger

If SVE brand was used or implied (through partnership, franchise, licensing, or endorsement) AND violation occurred → Full compensation to victims.

### Compensation Formula

```
Compensation = (All money from partnership)
             + (Additional damages: 1% - 101% of partnership revenue)
             + (Legal costs)
             + (Opportunity costs)
```

**"All money from partnership"** means:
- Every dollar/euro/currency unit received
- From day 1 of partnership to discovery date
- Including profits, fees, commissions, revenue share
- **NO deductions allowed** (not for costs, not for "legitimate" work, nothing)

**Additional damages (1-101%):**
| Harm Level | Additional % | Example |
|------------|--------------|---------|
| **Minor** | 1-10% | Late disclosure, honest mistake covered up briefly |
| **Moderate** | 11-50% | Systematic concealment, multiple violations |
| **Severe** | 51-100% | Intentional fraud, patient harm, public danger |
| **Existential** | 101% | Deaths, mass harm, Mark-of-Beast scenarios |

**Decided by:** Veche vote (67%) or arbitration panel.

### Distribution

**Court cases (if SVE lawyers involved):**
```
Victim compensation:    100% of penalty
Attorney fees:          25-90% of penalty (per Appendix E §23)
SVE Foundation:         0%

Total recovery needed:  Penalty + Attorney share (both paid by violator)
```

**Example:**
- $10M penalty owed to victims
- $7M attorney share (70% at trial phase)
- Violator pays: $17M total
- Victims receive: $10M (100%)
- Attorneys receive: $7M (their share)
- SVE Foundation: $0 (we take nothing when victims exist)

**Why SVE takes 0%:** Money belongs to those harmed. Foundation exists to serve victims, not profit from their suffering.

**Direct settlements (no court):**
```
Victims:           90%
SVE Foundation:    10% (administrative costs only)
```

### "All Money Back" Rule

**If partnership used SVE brand/mark:**

Victim has right to demand **full refund of all payments made** — regardless of "value received."

**Example:**
- Customer paid $100k for "SVE-verified consulting"
- Later discovered consultant lied about transparency
- Customer gets $100k back (even if consulting was partially useful)

**Rationale:** Brand trust was violated. Customer paid for "SVE-verified" (truth), received "SVE-labeled" (lie). That's fraud.

**No "but we delivered value" defense.** Trust was the product. Trust was not delivered.

## §3 - THE COIN (Conflict Resolution & Divine Judgment)

### When Coin Decides

**The Coin (lot/sortition) resolves conflicts where:**
- Both parties claim truth but facts unclear
- Dispute involves judgment calls (not clear violation)
- Repentance path decisions (see §4)
- Restoration after betrayal (return to normal rates?)

**Biblical foundation:**
> *"The lot is cast into the lap, but its every decision is from the Lord."* — Proverbs 16:33

### How Coin Works

**Physical coin flip** (not digital):
1. Witness present (video recorded, IPFS hash)
2. Question stated clearly ("Does Person X get normal rate this contract?")
3. Heads = Yes, Tails = No (or reversed, agreed beforehand)
4. Flip recorded, blockchain timestamp
5. Result is **final and binding** (no appeals, no do-overs)

**No manipulation possible:**
- Physical quantum randomness (Heisenberg uncertainty)
- God decides outcome (we trust His sovereignty)
- Human judgment removed (prevents bias, corruption)

### Coin Jurisdiction

**Coin decides in these scenarios:**
1. **Critical moments** (life-or-death decisions, irreversible choices)
2. **Repentance path** (does person get normal rate or reduced? see §4)
3. **Restoration timing** (has person completed repentance period?)
4. **Forgiveness disputes** (victim wants mercy, community wants justice)

**Coin does NOT decide:**
- Clear facts (1+1=2, no coin needed)
- Clear violations (dishonesty proven = banned, no coin)
- Interpretation of principles (that's 31-AI Oracle's job)

**Coin decides when humans deadlock on judgment calls.**

## §4 - REPENTANCE PATH (Restoration After Betrayal)

### Eligibility

**Who can repent:** Anyone who violated SVE rules (one-bit or larger).

**Who cannot:** Those who:
- Caused deaths through intentional suppression (pharma hiding trial deaths)
- Committed "Mark of Beast" level violations (Revelation 13:16-17 scenarios)
- Refused to acknowledge wrongdoing (denial = no repentance)

**Why excluded:** Some acts are unforgivable in this life (though God may forgive in eternity). We don't enable those who killed through lies.

### The Path

**Step 1: Public Confession (Day 1)**
- Full written confession (what was hidden, why, who harmed)
- Published on GitHub, IPFS (permanent record)
- No minimization, no excuses, no blame-shifting
- Statement: "I betrayed trust. I accept consequences. I seek restoration."

**Step 2: Victim Restitution (Months 1-12)**
- All money returned to victims (per §2)
- Additional damages paid (per §2)
- Public apology to each victim (if they consent to contact)
- Victim forgiveness is NOT required (but welcomed if offered)

**Step 3: Reduced-Rate Period (Years 1-11/22/33)**
- Return to SVE work **at reduced compensation**
- **Coin decides EACH contract:** Normal rate (25-90%) OR reduced rate (10-25% of normal)
- Example: Normal rate = 40%, Reduced = 10% (which is 25% of 40%)
- Coin flipped before EACH engagement (not once, every single time)
- This continues for **11, 22, or 33 years** (person chooses duration at start)

**Duration choices:**
| Duration | Meaning | Restoration |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| **11 years** | Moderate repentance | Normal rates return + 50% margin bonus |
| **22 years** | Deep repentance | Normal rates return + 70% margin bonus |
| **33 years** | Full repentance (Christ's age) | Normal rates return + 90% margin bonus |

**Coin outcomes during reduced-rate period:**
- **Heads** = Normal rate this contract (e.g., 40%)
- **Tails** = Reduced rate this contract (e.g., 10%)
- Person never knows in advance which they'll get
- Cannot refuse contracts (if refuse = path terminated, ban permanent)

**Step 4: Completion & Restoration**

After completing chosen duration (11/22/33 years):

**Restoration package:**
1. **Full rights restored** — All previous SVE privileges return
2. **Margin bonus** — Compensation increases:
   - 11 years → +50% (if was 40%, now 60%)
   - 22 years → +70% (if was 40%, now 68%)
   - 33 years → +90% (if was 40%, now 76%)
3. **"Restored" badge** — Public recognition (not "forgiven and forgotten" — more like "fell, repented, restored")
4. **Maximum margin cap** — Cannot exceed 90% of total value (prevents exploitation)

**Why bonus?** 
- Proven character through suffering (Romans 5:3-4)
- Community invests in restoration (should benefit from success)
- Demonstrates rehabilitation works (encourages others to repent)

### Termination of Repentance Path

**Path ends early (ban becomes permanent) if:**
- Second violation during repentance period (zero tolerance returns)
- Refuses contract because coin gave reduced rate (shows insincerity)
- Public complaints indicate lack of genuine change (fruits test)
- Attempts to manipulate coin (provable bad faith)

**No second chances after path termination.** Repentance is offered once.

### Additional Considerations (Not Previously Listed)

**K. Cultural Context**
- Practice acceptable in one culture, violation in another
- Example: Gift-giving (bribery vs hospitality)
- Solution: Intent matters more than form; coin decides if unclear

**L. Good Faith Attempts**
- Tried to comply but failed (documented effort)
- vs Didn't try at all (negligence)
- Coin decides if good faith sufficient to reduce penalty

**M. Proportionality**
- Harm scale should affect timeline
- $1000 fraud ≠ $100M fraud (even if both dishonest)
- Coin can scale timeline up/down based on harm magnitude

**N. Precedent Value**
- First case sets example (may be harsher to deter)
- Later cases can be more lenient (precedent established)
- Coin decides if this is precedent-setting case

**O. Organizational Repentance**
- Corporation violated → Corporate path
- Proposal: Leadership change required + transformation timeline (like 44-year path but punitive)
- If leadership unchanged → No repentance path (organization can't repent without personnel change)
- Individual employees not punished if they were whistleblowers or unaware

**P. Disability / Illness**
- Mental illness, intellectual disability affect culpability
- Path paused if person becomes unable to work (serious illness)
- Coin decides: Is pause legitimate or manipulation?

**Q. Redemption vs Justice**
- Victim wants mercy, community wants justice (or vice versa)
- Coin decides which voice to honor this time
- Both are valid; coin shows God's priority in this case

**R. Anonymous Violations**
- Violation discovered, perpetrator unknown
- SVE can pursue forensic investigation (Appendix A — truth leaks)
- Once identified: Standard protocol applies
- Cannot hide behind anonymity forever (time + investigation = exposure)
