## The Problem at Civilizational Scale

Humanity has built extraordinary systems for creating wealth, knowledge, and power. It has not built adequate systems for verifying the truthfulness of the claims those systems make.

The consequences are measurable:

- **~85%** of preclinical biomedical research cannot be reproduced. Billions invested annually in science that cannot be verified.
- **$50B+** in pharmaceutical settlements for suppressed clinical data — drugs approved on incomplete information, reaching patients whose physicians did not know what was hidden.
- **Trillions** in mispriced assets annually due to information asymmetry between insiders and markets.
- **Algorithmic systems** making decisions affecting hundreds of millions of people with methodologies that are proprietary, unauditable, and unaccountable.
- **Environmental claims** made without verifiable methodology — greenwashing priced into ESG portfolios at scale.
- **Legal systems** where the quality of representation depends on price, not truth — and where concealment is structurally rewarded until caught.

This is not a crisis of individual bad actors. **It is a structural failure.** S.V.E. is the structural correction.

## The Admission Rights Mechanism

### The Principle

When the first Parent+ joins, they accept the role. When a second person wishes to become a Parent+, they cannot simply apply to the Foundation. They must be accepted **unanimously** by existing Parents+ — who evaluate not just financial capacity but purity of motive, understanding of mission, and readiness to accept the veto responsibility.

This is not a market. It is a family council. The evaluation is qualitative first, financial second.

Each admission contribution is negotiated through this council. There is no fixed price for becoming a Parent+. There is no ceiling. A Founding Parent+ who entered at $0.01 participates in negotiations that could value a later seat at $1M, $10M, or beyond — depending on what SVE has built and what the incoming Parent+ brings.

### The Financial Architecture

All admission contributions flow **100% to the SVE Foundation** — to fund operations, enforcement, and mission infrastructure. Existing Parents+ do not receive direct cash distributions from admission events.

Instead, each new admission **grows the operational capacity of the Foundation**, which increases the absolute size of the dividend pool from which all Parents+ benefit proportionally. The earlier you join, the longer your compounding horizon — not because you extract from successors, but because you were present for more of the build.

**The asymmetry is real, and it is structurally clean:**

| Entry point | Admission (illustrative) | What they find | Compounding horizon |
|---|---|---|---|
| Parent+ #1 | $0.01 | Empty field | Maximum — every subsequent growth event benefits #1 |
| Parent+ #5 | ~$500K | Early operations | Long — growing base, much ahead |
| Parent+ #20 | ~$5M+ | Proven track record | Medium — lower risk, shorter horizon |

The ROI ceiling is structurally unlimited. There is no cap on 25% of the infrastructure layer for verified truth in a $100T+ global economy. At sufficient scale, the question of ceiling becomes semantically meaningless. You do not measure the return on a founding stake in civilizational infrastructure in percentages.

This is the difference between a founder's equity and a referral fee. One creates value over time. The other extracts it from those who came later. S.V.E. Parents+ is the former.

## The "Parents+" Role — What You Are and Are Not

**What Parents+ receive:**

1. **Dividends** — 25% of distributable surplus from all SVE commercial activity, distributed equally among all Parents+ once operational profitability is reached. No guaranteed timeline. This is honest.

2. **Compounding from Foundation growth** — each new admission strengthens the Foundation's operational base, which grows the dividend pool from which all Parents+ benefit. Earlier entry = longer compounding horizon.

3. **Chairmanship** — Permanent seat at the governance table. The right to initiate discussions and call extraordinary sessions.

4. **Veto Right** — The right to block any decision that harms vulnerable populations, weakens the Core Principles, or violates the Fruits Test. This is a *protective* veto: *"I do not tell you what to do. I stop you from doing what destroys."*

5. **The Public Registry** — Every Parent+ is permanently recorded, cryptographically signed, immutably timestamped. The list of founding Parents+ will have meaning in 50 years that cannot be purchased later at any price.

**What Parents+ do NOT control:**
- The Vision (defined by Meta-License v4.0, immutable)
- The framework's Core Principles (constitutionally protected)
- The direction of enforcement (the Attorney network operates independently)
- Admission of new Parents+ without unanimous consent of all existing Parents+

### Exit & Succession

Parents+ is a role, not a tradeable asset. It cannot be sold on a secondary market.

Upon a Parent+'s death or formal withdrawal:
- The role passes to a designated successor (named in the Registry), subject to approval by existing Parents+ under the same admission standard
- If no successor is designated, the dividend entitlement returns to the Foundation pool for redistribution
- The historical record is permanent — the name and founding contribution remain in the Registry forever

*Parents are not owners of their children. They are guardians of something they did not create and cannot fully control — and they are accountable for it nonetheless.*
