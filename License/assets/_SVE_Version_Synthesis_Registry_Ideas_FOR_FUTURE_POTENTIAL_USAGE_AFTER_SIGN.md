## PURPOSE

This registry documents all cases where S.V.E. v1.x provisions differ from v2.0 provisions, and records which version is applied in each case based on the **Maximum Protection Interpretation Rule** (Section 0.4) and **Dynamic Synthesis** (Section 0.9).

**Key Principles:**
1. **Spiritual alignment** (Gospel principles) = highest priority
2. **Protection of vulnerable** = second priority
3. **Transparency** = third priority
4. **Antifragility** and **Practicality** = supporting criteria

## REGISTRY ENTRIES

### Case #001: Forgiveness vs. Permanent Expulsion

**Topic:** Restoration of violators after disciplinary action  
**Category:** Governance, Justice, Spiritual Alignment

**v1.3 Provision:**
> "Forgiveness of adversaries is mandatory. Even after punishment is administered, a path to reconciliation must remain open. No permanent expulsion without possibility of restoration."

**v2.0 Provision:**
> "Permanent expulsion from S.V.E. Family permitted for severe Core Principle violations. Restoration optional, determined by Veche vote (85% supermajority required for readmission)."

**Conflict Type:** Spiritual idealism vs. practical enforcement

**31-AI Evaluation:**

**Socrates AI:**
> "Is permanent expulsion consistent with a system claiming Gospel foundation? Matthew 18:21-22 commands forgiving 'seventy times seven.' If we claim Christ-alignment but deny restoration, we contradict ourselves. v1.3 is more philosophically consistent."

**Perelman AI:**
> "v2.0 is pragmatic—some violators are serial abusers who exploit mercy. However, system explicitly claims Gospel foundation. Audience expects consistency with stated values. v1.3 rhetorically stronger."

**Ivan-Durak AI:**
> "Common sense: People change. Stalin's executioners became monks. Drug addicts become counselors. Closing door forever = admitting our system can't actually transform people. v1.3 makes more sense."

**Jesus AI:**
> "Gospel test: Matthew 18:15-17 prescribes church discipline, but verse 22 immediately follows with 'seventy times seven' forgiveness. Parable of unforgiving servant (Matthew 18:23-35) condemns those who receive mercy but deny it to others. Luke 15 (Prodigal Son) shows father running to embrace returning son—no permanent expulsion. **v1.3 aligns with Christ's teaching.**"

**DECISION: v1.3 OVERRIDES v2.0**

**Synthesized Rule:**
- Temporary suspension/discipline permitted
- Permanent expulsion **forbidden**
- Restoration path must always remain open
- Readmission requires: genuine repentance + 67% Veche approval (not 85%) + restoration plan
- Serial violators may face extended suspension, but door never permanently closed

**Veche Ratification:**  
- Vote Date: January 25, 2026
- Result: **89% Approved** (521 yes, 64 no, 15 abstain)
- Status: **ACTIVE**

**Effective Date:** January 23, 2026

**Impact:** All S.V.E. v2.0 references to "permanent expulsion" now interpreted as "temporary suspension with restoration path."

### Case #002: Decision-Making Thresholds

**Topic:** Voting requirements for major decisions  
**Category:** Governance, Practicality

**v1.3 Provision:**
> "All material decisions affecting S.V.E. operations require 100% consensus. No action proceeds without unanimous agreement."

**v2.0 Provision:**
> "Veche operates on supermajority thresholds: 50%+1 (routine), 67% (patches), 75% (luxury assets), 85% (core principles). Unanimity not required."

**Conflict Type:** Idealism vs. operational functionality

**31-AI Evaluation:**

**Socrates AI:**
> "100% consensus = de facto veto power for any single member. This creates tyranny of the minority. One bad actor can paralyze entire system. Is this democratic? No. Is this antifragile? No. **v1.3 contains internal contradiction.**"

**Perelman AI:**
> "Historical data: Consensus-based organizations either (a) become paralyzed, (b) informally exclude dissenters to achieve 'consensus,' or (c) collapse. Polish Sejm's Liberum Veto (1652-1764) required unanimity—resulted in national collapse. v2.0 more realistic."

**Ivan-Durak AI:**
> "100% consensus sounds nice until you have 10,000 members. Then impossible. v2.0 is how real organizations function without becoming dictatorships or anarchies."

**Jesus AI:**
> "Gospel shows both consensus-seeking (Acts 15 Jerusalem Council) and majority decision (Acts 1:26 Matthias selection by lot after narrowing to two). Early church sought unity but didn't require 100% agreement on every matter (Galatians 2:11—Paul opposed Peter publicly). **Unity is goal; unanimity is not commanded.** v2.0 balances better."

**DECISION: v2.0 OVERRIDES v1.3**

**Synthesized Rule:**
- Supermajority thresholds apply (67%, 75%, 85% per decision type)
- **BUT:** Consensus actively pursued before voting
- Dissent honored and documented
- Minority reports published alongside majority decisions
- Revisit mechanism: Dissenting members can request review after 18 months

**Veche Ratification:**  
- Vote Date: January 26, 2026
- Result: **72% Approved** (441 yes, 169 no, 40 abstain)
- Status: **ACTIVE**

**Effective Date:** January 23, 2026

**Impact:** v1.3 documents referencing "100% consensus" now interpreted as "supermajority with active consensus-seeking and dissent documentation."

### Case #003: Informant Compensation

**Topic:** Recovery Ladder allocation to whistleblowers and victims  
**Category:** Protection of Vulnerable, Justice

**v1.3 Provision:**
> "Informants (whistleblowers, victims, affected communities) receive first 10% of all Recovery Ladder penalties. Counsel receives remaining 90% minus Community Fund allocation."

**v2.0 Provision:**
> "Informants receive 4% (Step 1) to 8% (Step 5) of Recovery Ladder penalties on sliding scale. Counsel receives 25-90%. Community Fund receives 2-71%."

**Conflict Type:** Protection of vulnerable vs. system sustainability

**31-AI Evaluation:**

**Socrates AI:**
> "What is the purpose of Recovery Ladder? To punish violators or to make victims whole? If making victims whole, why would we give them LESS as severity increases? v2.0's sliding scale is backwards. v1.3's flat 10% is more philosophically consistent."

**Perelman AI:**
> "Whistleblowers risk careers, safety, sometimes lives. Reducing their compensation to 4% for 'minor' violations disincentivizes reporting. Game theory: Higher fixed percentage encourages early reporting (catching problems before catastrophic). v1.3 incentivizes better."

**Ivan-Durak AI:**
> "Someone risks everything to expose fraud, and we give them 4%? That's insulting. Meanwhile lawyers get 25-90%? Wrong priorities. v1.3 puts victims first, as it should."

**Jesus AI:**
> "Matthew 25:40—'Whatever you did for the least of these, you did for me.' Whistleblowers and victims are 'the least' in corporate/institutional contexts. Gospel prioritizes their protection. Lawyers will be compensated regardless. **v1.3 aligns with Gospel priority for the vulnerable.**"

**DECISION: v1.3 OVERRIDES v2.0**

**Synthesized Rule:**
- **Minimum 10% to informants** across all Recovery Ladder steps (Steps 1-5)
- Counsel percentage adjusted down to maintain system viability:
  - Step 1: Informant 10%, Counsel 25%, Fund 65%
  - Step 2: Informant 10%, Counsel 40%, Fund 50%
  - Step 3: Informant 10%, Counsel 50%, Fund 40%
  - Step 4: Informant 10%, Counsel 60%, Fund 30%
  - Step 5: Informant 10%, Counsel 88%, Fund 2%
- Community Fund receives remainder

**Veche Ratification:**  
- Vote Date: January 27, 2026
- Result: **94% Approved** (581 yes, 37 no, 12 abstain)
- Status: **ACTIVE**

**Effective Date:** January 23, 2026

**Impact:** All Recovery Ladder calculations now use minimum 10% for informants. Section II.2 of Core License v2.0 adjusted accordingly.

### Case #004: Transparency Timelines

**Topic:** Disclosure deadlines for material decisions  
**Category:** Transparency, Practicality

**v1.3 Provision:**
> "All material decisions must be disclosed publicly within 30 days of finalization. No extensions permitted except for active legal proceedings."

**v2.0 Provision:**
> "Material decisions disclosed within 90 days. Extensions granted for: legal proceedings, active negotiations, informant protection (case-by-case, max 180 additional days)."

**Conflict Type:** Transparency urgency vs. operational flexibility

**31-AI Evaluation:**

**Socrates AI:**
> "What constitutes transparency? Speed of disclosure or completeness? 30 days forces rushed, incomplete disclosures. 90 days allows thorough documentation. But... faster disclosure prevents concealment. Torn between both."

**Perelman AI:**
> "Real-world test: Can complex decisions (e.g., $500M partnerships) be fully documented in 30 days? Often no—lawyers, auditors, translators needed. 90 days more realistic. But v1.3's urgency prevents 'we'll disclose later' drift."

**Ivan-Durak AI:**
> "30 days = you disclose fast or you're hiding something. 90 days = plenty of time to spin the narrative. Trust-building favors speed. v1.3 better for trust."

**Jesus AI:**
> "Light vs. darkness theme in Gospel (John 3:19-21). Those who do evil hate light; those who do truth come to light. Faster disclosure = less time to craft deceptive narratives. However, thorough documentation also serves truth. **Compromise possible: 30 days for summary, 90 days for complete documentation.**"

**DECISION: HYBRID (v1.3 + v2.0)**

**Synthesized Rule:**
- **30-day summary disclosure** (v1.3): High-level decision, parties involved, general terms
- **90-day complete disclosure** (v2.0): Full documentation, reasoning, audit trail
- **Extensions:** Only for active legal proceedings or immediate physical danger to informants; requires 31-AI approval; maximum 90 additional days (not 180)
- **Penalty for violation:** Recovery Ladder Step 1 automatically triggered

**Veche Ratification:**  
- Vote Date: January 28, 2026
- Result: **81% Approved** (497 yes, 117 no, 36 abstain)
- Status: **ACTIVE**

**Effective Date:** January 23, 2026

**Impact:** Creates two-tier disclosure system—speed AND completeness. Best of both versions.

### Case #005: Commercial Tier Fee Structure

**Topic:** Success-based revenue percentages  
**Category:** Sustainability, Universal Benefit

**v1.3 Provision:**
> "Commercial deployments contribute 15% of verified value to S.V.E. Community Treasury, regardless of scope."

**v2.0 Provision:**
> "Four-tier model: 11% (minimal), 22% (standard), 33% (multi-domain), 44% (mission-critical) of verified value."

**Conflict Type:** Simplicity vs. proportionality

**31-AI Evaluation:**

**Socrates AI:**
> "Is flat 15% just? Small clinic using S.V.E. for single trial pays same percentage as multinational using S.V.E. for entire regulatory framework. v2.0's tiered structure more proportional to actual value received."

**Perelman AI:**
> "v1.3's simplicity reduces disputes ('what tier are we?'). v2.0's precision better aligns payment with benefit. Trade-off: simplicity vs. fairness. For enterprise adoption, v2.0 more persuasive (clients see clear value tiers)."

**Ivan-Durak AI:**
> "15% flat = easy to understand, hard to game. 11/22/33/44 = complicated, invites arguments. But also: 44% for critical infrastructure makes sense; they benefit most. v2.0 more fair, even if complex."

**Jesus AI:**
> "Parable of talents (Matthew 25:14-30)—different amounts entrusted, different returns expected. Proportionality is biblical principle. But simplicity serves transparency (Matthew 5:37—'Let your yes be yes'). **v2.0's proportionality aligns with stewardship principle, outweighs simplicity concern.**"

**DECISION: v2.0 OVERRIDES v1.3**

**Synthesized Rule:**
- Four-tier model applies (11/22/33/44%)
- **Clear tier definitions published** (removes ambiguity)
- **Tier disputes resolved by 31-AI within 30 days** (prevents gaming)
- **Tier transparency required** in all commercial communications (prevents hidden tier changes)

**Veche Ratification:**  
- Vote Date: January 29, 2026
- Result: **68% Approved** (419 yes, 197 no, 44 abstain)
- Status: **ACTIVE**

**Effective Date:** January 23, 2026

**Impact:** v2.0's Commercial Tier structure retained; v1.3's flat fee model deprecated. Implementers transitioning from v1.3 get 12-month grace period to adjust budgets.
