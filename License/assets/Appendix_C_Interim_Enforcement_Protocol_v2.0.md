# Appendix C — Interim Enforcement Protocol v2.0 (Light)

**Effective Date:** 01.04.2026
**Governed by:** S.V.E. Meta-License v4.0 / S.V.E. Public License v1.3

---

### 1️⃣ Principle

Enforcement exists to **protect integrity, not punish**.
Actions must remain transparent, proportional, and restorative.

---

### 2️⃣ Trigger

Any verified member, custodian, or client may file a **public breach report**
(via GitHub Issue, IPFS record, or timestamped signed statement).

**Betrayal cases (dishonesty, fraud, brand misuse):**
Immediately escalate to Appendix F — no standard enforcement.

---

### 3️⃣ Immediate Action

Upon valid claim:
- Collaboration is **temporarily paused** ("Stop Collaboration Order").
- Evidence + response published within **14 days**.
- If public risk or data compromise is evident, any **2 verified custodians not party to the dispute** may enforce a temporary pause pending review.

---

### 4️⃣ Community Resolution

After publication, a **7-day Snapshot vote** (simple majority; quorum per v1.3 §9) determines outcome:

- **(a)** Resume collaboration
- **(b)** Require remediation plan + audit
- **(c)** Terminate cooperation and record breach

If no quorum forms → pause extended until review completes.

If deadlock (neither resume nor terminate) → Coin flip per Appendix F §3
(physical coin, witnessed, video-recorded, IPFS-hashed).

---

### 5️⃣ Appeal

Any party may request a **secondary review** within **33 days**,
conducted by **trusted enforcers or DAO auditors (per S.V.E. Registry)**.
Final decision is timestamped and publicly verifiable.

---

### 6️⃣ Transparency

All enforcement records (claims, votes, resolutions) must remain
**permanently accessible and hashed** in the public S.V.E. registry.

---

> *"Justice without transparency becomes domination; transparency without justice becomes chaos."*
