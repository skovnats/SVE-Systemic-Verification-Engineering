## The Problem at Civilizational Scale

Humanity has built extraordinary systems for creating wealth, knowledge, and power. It has not built adequate systems for verifying the truthfulness of the claims those systems make.

The consequences are measurable:

- **~85%** of preclinical biomedical research cannot be reproduced. Billions invested annually in science that cannot be verified.
- **$50B+** in pharmaceutical settlements for suppressed clinical data — drugs approved on incomplete information, reaching patients whose physicians did not know what was hidden.
- **Trillions** in mispriced assets annually due to information asymmetry between insiders and markets.
- **Algorithmic systems** making decisions affecting hundreds of millions of people with methodologies that are proprietary, unauditable, and unaccountable.
- **Environmental claims** made without verifiable methodology — greenwashing priced into ESG portfolios at scale.
- **Legal systems** where the quality of representation depends on price, not truth — and where concealment is structurally rewarded until caught.

This is not a crisis of individual bad actors. **It is a structural failure:** the systems designed to produce reliable truth have no enforceable mechanism to distinguish truth from performance. S.V.E. is that mechanism.

## The Admission Rights Mechanism

**This is the most unusual element of the Parents+ structure. Read it carefully.**

### How it works

When the first Parent+ joins, they pay one cent and accept the role. That is the founding stake.

When a second person wishes to become a Parent+, **they cannot simply apply to the Foundation. They must be accepted by the existing Parents+ — unanimously.** The existing Parents+ evaluate not only financial capacity but purity of motive, understanding of mission, willingness to accept the veto responsibility. They set the admission contribution for each new entrant through direct negotiation.

When a third person wishes to join, **the first two negotiate together.** When a fourth — the first three. And so on.

The admission contribution is split:
- **50% to the S.V.E. Foundation** (operational mission, distributed per Appendix B: 70% treasury, 20% contributors, 10% open grants)
- **50% distributed equally among all existing Parents+**

### The arithmetic

| Event | Admission contribution | Existing Parents+ receive |
|---|---|---|
| Parent+ #1 joins | $0.01 | — |
| Parent+ #2 joins | Negotiated (e.g. $100K) | #1 receives $50K |
| Parent+ #3 joins | Negotiated (e.g. $1M) | #1 and #2 each receive $500K |
| Parent+ #4 joins | Negotiated (e.g. $10M) | #1, #2, #3 each receive ~$1.67M |
| ... | ... | ... |

The earlier you join, the more admission events you benefit from. The ROI on $0.01 is, in this structure, theoretically bounded only by the number and scale of future admissions and the success of S.V.E. operations. **No ceiling has been set. The ceiling is the value of the infrastructure being built.**

### Why this is not a pyramid scheme — and what the real risk is

A pyramid scheme extracts value from new entrants and redistributes it upward, with no underlying value creation. The scheme collapses when new entrants stop arriving.

This structure is different in three critical ways:

**1. Underlying value exists independently.** S.V.E. generates revenue from pharma certification fees, attorney recovery shares, and corporate transformation tiers — regardless of admission events. Dividends come from operations, not from admission fees alone.

**2. Growth is finite and quality-controlled.** There is a maximum number of Parents+. The unanimous consent requirement means growth is slow and deliberate — the opposite of a pyramid's incentive to recruit as many as possible as fast as possible.

**3. Everyone joins as equals.** Parent+ #47 has the same veto right, the same governance weight, and the same access to dividends as Parent+ #1. The financial asymmetry is in admission fee economics; the governance asymmetry is zero.

**The real legal risk, stated honestly:** In some jurisdictions (US SEC, EU), the admission fee mechanism may be interpreted as an unregistered security offering or as a referral scheme. Independent legal counsel is mandatory before formalising this structure. The S.V.E. Interpretive Guidance (SVE-IG-EN-v1.0) addresses the jurisdiction framework. The structure may need to be adapted — but the principle is sound and legally achievable.

**The Fruits Test applies here too (§0):** If this mechanism produces bad fruits — enriches early entrants at the expense of the mission, attracts people motivated only by admission fee arbitrage, or creates perverse incentives to block worthy later entrants to protect financial position — it is reviewed and restructured. This clause is in the Foundation's constitution, not in an appendix. It cannot be removed.

### The anti-corruption safeguard

The gatekeeper role is powerful. Power corrupts. S.V.E. addresses this directly:

- All admission negotiations are **public** — logged in the S.V.E. Registry (GitHub/IPFS), timestamped, with stated reasoning.
- Any candidate rejected by existing Parents+ may appeal to the **41 AI Oracle** for a non-binding opinion on whether the rejection served the mission or the financial interest of existing Parents+.
- If the Oracle finds the rejection motivated by financial self-protection rather than mission integrity, the Veche (community assembly) may override by 2/3 vote.
- **Existing Parents+ cannot veto a candidate they have a direct financial conflict of interest with.** Recusal is mandatory and auditable.

The Coin (Proverbs 16:33) resolves deadlocks where existing Parents+ cannot reach unanimity on admission.
