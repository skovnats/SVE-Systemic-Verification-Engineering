## Purpose

Defines a transparent, ethical revenue-share model for all commercial uses of S.V.E.
Aligns compensation strictly with **verified benefit** — not speculation or monopoly.

Two paths:
- **§1–9:** Standard tiers (individuals, small orgs, transparent corps)

## PART I: STANDARD COMMERCIAL TIERS

### §1 — Core Principles

1. **Integrity before profit** — truth overrides gain
2. **Proportional reward** — pay grows with measurable value
3. **Open accountability** — every transaction auditable
4. **Human-first** — social or data value may substitute payment if funds are limited

### §2 — Base Compensation

Each contributor receives:
- Payment = **median market rate (≈99.9% regional average)**
- Reimbursement for verified infrastructure costs

Ensures parity with industry norms — no markup, no exploitation.

### §3 — Success Fee / Impact Share

Applied only on verified outcomes:

| Tier | Scope | Share of Verified Value |
|------|-------|-------------------------|
| **11%** | Minimal integration | 11% |
| **22%** | Standard deployment | 22% |
| **33%** | Multi-domain optimization | 33% |
| **44%** | Complex / mission-critical | 44% |

*Verified value = documented savings, efficiency gain, risk reduction, or new revenue (S.V.E.-audited).*

### §4 — Fund Distribution

| Allocation | Share | Purpose |
|------------|-------|---------|
| Community Treasury | 70% | Research, maintenance, grants |
| Active Contributors | 20% | Work-based reward |
| Open-Benefit Grants | 10% | Education & transparency |

Immutable without public amendment.

### §5 — Limited-Resource Clients

If liquidity is lacking:
- Offer **equity**, **tokenized stake**, or **public contribution**
- Open-publish verified results ("Verified by S.V.E.")

No financial barrier shall block projects serving the public good.

### §6 — Transparency & Audit

Each engagement must keep an **S.V.E. Compliance Log** with:
- Project & tier
- Proof of benefit
- Payment & distribution summary

Public by default; embargo ≤12 months.

### §7 — Ethical Flexibility

**WARNING:** Any violation (even one-bit dishonesty) triggers Appendix F immediate termination.
No "small violations" — trust is binary.

Percentages adjustable only by mutual written consent and public release.
Community Treasury may waive fees for hardship or force majeure.

### §8 — Guiding Maxim

> *"Money is secondary; impact and integrity come first."*

Any conflict between profit and truth is resolved in favor of truth.

### §9 — Governance

Updated by **distributed consensus** of:
- Exodus 3.0 custodians and/or S.V.E. DAO (once active)
- Verified Public Custodians (S.V.E. Registry)
- Recognized institutional or academic partners (open endorsement)

**Amendments require:**
- Public proposal + 90-day comment period
- ≥2/3 multi-group approval
- Publication with timestamp (GitHub + IPFS)

No single entity may unilaterally control framework evolution.

### §17 — Special Scenarios

- Parent transformation applies to all subsidiaries

### §18 — Why 44 Years?

**Practical foundation:**
- Average career = 40–45 years → transformation fits within one professional lifetime
- Long enough to require serious commitment and filter bad faith
- Short enough for founders to see completion

**Strategic foundation:**
- Competitors cannot wait 44 years → early adopters gain long-term advantage
- Investors rewarded with patience (lower tier after Year 45)
- Workers/users witness the transformation → trust builds over decades

> *Biblical note: 40 years = a generation (Exodus, Elijah); 44 = margin of grace beyond strict judgment.*

**Who:** Any licensed attorney or law firm globally. No prior permission required.

**How:** Find violations → File suit → Recover penalties → Keep your share.

### Attorney Compensation: Escalating Share Model

Attorney share **increases** with violator's resistance:

| Phase | Trigger | Attorney Share | To SVE |
|-------|---------|---------------|--------|
| **Pre-litigation** | Violator corrects after notice | 25% | 75% |
| **Filing** | Formal complaint filed | 40% | 60% |
| **Discovery** | Litigation proceeds past discovery | 55% | 45% |
| **Trial** | Case goes to trial | 70% | 30% |
| **Appeal** | Violator appeals | 90% | 10% |

**Rationale:** Truth-seekers settle fast (cheap). Resistors reveal guilt through obstruction (expensive).

**Example:** $10M penalty, settled pre-litigation → Attorney: $2.5M / SVE: $7.5M.
$10M penalty, goes to appeal → Attorney: $9M / SVE: $1M. **More they fight, more you earn.**

**Victim compensation (if victims exist):** 100% of penalty to victims; attorney share is separate and paid additionally by the violator. SVE Foundation receives nothing in victim cases.

- Academic papers using SVE logic without citation or reproducibility
- Corporate products "inspired by SVE" but proprietary/closed
- Pharma companies suppressing negative SVE-derived trial results
- Large corps claiming Tier 11–44% while qualifying for the 44-year path
- Any use of SVE in weapons, surveillance, or manipulation (per v1.3 §2)

**Evidence sources:**
- Public financial reports (discrepancies suggest hidden SVE use)
- Patent filings (SVE-derived algorithms claimed as proprietary)
- Academic publications (semantic fingerprints per Appendix A)
- Whistleblower reports (10% to informant; attorney share separate)
- AI forensic scans (automated detection of SVE logic patterns)

**If a company refuses even one document + uses SVE = proof of violation** (per v1.3 §3 — secrecy voids all rights).

### Special Rules: Science Cases (Witch Hunt Prevention)

*For academic/research violations only:*

- **If you win:** Standard attorney share (25–90% as above)
- **If you lose:** You pay all costs (court fees, defendant's legal fees, your own expenses)

**Why:** Prevents frivolous lawsuits against struggling researchers. Only pursue if evidence is solid.

**Exception:** If defendant is a pharma/biotech company (not an individual academic) → standard rules apply (no personal liability for attorney on loss).
