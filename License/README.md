## What S.V.E. Is

S.V.E. is infrastructure, not a product — in the way the internet, TCP/IP, the printing press, and double-entry bookkeeping were infrastructure. None looked like a business at the moment of creation. All of them restructured how a civilization verifies what it's told.

**Five VISION statements, ascending in scope:**

> **VISION-1:** SVE is TÜV for Reality.

> **VISION-2:** SVE is Airbnb and Uber for the Integrity of Reality. Airbnb solved trust between strangers sharing a room — SVE solves trust between humans sharing reality itself: data, science, law, contracts, word and thought. Where Uber dispatches drivers to your door, SVE dispatches attorneys to your defense — a distributed enforcement network with no gap left between thought, word, deed, and fruit.

> **VISION-3:** SVE is the TÜV stamp on the medicine bottle you give your child — a QR code to the full trial data, immutably logged. If any of it was falsified, the attorney network activates automatically: the partnership is voided, 101% of everything earned is returned, every dollar won in court goes to those harmed.

> **VISION-4 (FAKTEN-TÜV):** A verifiable quality mark, like TÜV, applied to facts instead of machines. Scannable, traceable, immutable.

> **VISION-5 (deepest):** Integrity-as-a-Service. Every claim verified. Every error acknowledged. Every correction public. Truth as a structural property of systems, not a virtue reserved for saints.

Also known, informally, as: *"Git for Reality," "Waze for Ontologies," "Fair Trade for Science," "ISO 9001 for Claims of Truth."*

## The Problem

- **~50–85%** of preclinical biomedical research cannot be reproduced.
- **$50B+** in pharmaceutical settlements tied to suppressed clinical trial data.
- **Trillions** mispriced annually from information asymmetry between insiders and markets.
- **Algorithmic systems** deciding outcomes for hundreds of millions of people, unauditable by design.
- **Legal representation quality tracks price, not truth** — concealment is structurally rewarded until caught.

Existing open licenses (GPL, Creative Commons) govern *distribution* — who may use what. None of them govern *veracity* — whether the claims made under them are true. That is the gap SVE is built to close. This is not a claim about individual bad actors; it is a claim about missing structure.

## The Core Hypothesis

> Human behaviour follows incentive structures more reliably than moral instruction. If the incentive structure rewards honesty over deception — in science, commerce, law, and governance — habits of honesty compound across generations, become what a society considers normal, and eventually shift its collective trajectory.

SVE does not preach honesty. It tries to make honesty the economically dominant strategy — markets that price honesty correctly, not the abolition of markets. Internally this is called **Capitalism v2.0**.

## How It Works — Three Simultaneous Tracks

### Track 1 — Enforcement (the Sword)

Any licensed attorney, globally, may pursue an SVE violation without prior authorization. Compensation escalates with resistance: 25–90% of recoveries; up to 95% in pharmaceutical cases where patient deaths are proven; a flat 90% share for the first nine cases filed. The network is self-financing — violators fund the enforcers, not a central budget. See *Call to Lawyers* / Appendix E for full terms.

### Track 2 — Certification (the Shield)

A company that voluntarily submits to SVE audit earns the SVE-Verified badge — a publicly auditable, cryptographically signed certification — and a Compliance Shield that protects it from the enforcement track while the certification holds. The market, not SVE, prices what verified trust is worth.

### Track 3 — Ownership Transformation (the Foundation): the 44-Year Corporate Path

This is the track worth stating in full, since it previously lived only as a cross-reference.

**Who it applies to.** A corporation whose use of SVE methodology crosses the Founder Mode size thresholds — revenue > $100M, staff > 1,000, or market capitalization > $500M (the same thresholds defined in License v5.5 §11) — leaves the lighter Founder Mode regime and enters the standard 44-Year Corporate Path. Below those thresholds, a qualifying founder instead operates under **SVE-Unicorns Founder Mode**: full operational freedom, with the right to *freeze* (defer publication of) methods until Year 44 without that freeze counting as enclosure, provided the six Founder-Mode Commandments hold and the work is hash-registered as SVE-bound from day one (License v5.5 §11).

**What the path does.** Over a 44-year horizon, a participating corporation transitions — gradually, consensually, and verified by independent audit at each stage — toward the four-quadrant **PEMY** ownership structure described below (25% Investors / 25% Founders–Management / 25% Workers–Contractors / 25% Users–Community). It is not an expropriation event and not a single transaction: it is a scheduled, publicly logged sequence of steps, each auditable, each reversible only through the same non-selective withdrawal logic that governs the rest of this framework (License v5.5 §6.3) — a company cannot enter the path to claim the SVE-Verified badge and then quietly stop advancing along it without that stall itself being auditable as a bad fruit under §8.

**Revenue-share tiers en route.** Independent of full ownership transformation, commercial use of SVE methodology is priced on an 11/22/33/44% verified-benefit tier schedule (Meta-License v4.0 Appendix B, incorporated by reference in License v5.5 §10) — commercial use requires no prior authorization, only compliance with the applicable tier. The 44-year path is the ownership-structure destination; the tier schedule is what funds the Foundation and the enforcement network along the way.

**Where the details live.** The full mechanics — audit cadence, tier qualification criteria, and the transformation schedule itself — are Appendix B's, not this document's; this README states the shape of the track and the thresholds that gate it, and defers the operational detail to that appendix so this file does not silently invent terms Appendix B hasn't actually specified.

## The Parents+ Role — Investor Vision

*A note on framing: the author's personal faith shaped this framework and this document sometimes quotes it, but no SVE decision runs through it — every mechanism below (Fruits Test, Veche vote, AI Oracle, public audit chain) is a verifiable, secular instrument. Two earlier drafts of this section existed, one with fuller scriptural framing and one secular; they are merged here, secular-first, on equal footing.*

**This is not a regular startup.** The horizon is decades, plausibly centuries. If your primary metric is quarterly ROI, this is honestly not for you — there is no contempt in that; there are thousands of excellent investments built for that horizon, and this isn't one of them.

**What Parents+ is not:** a startup equity play, a charity, a fund, a token, a security (pending confirmation — see *Legal Status* below), or a scheme. **What it is:** a founding governance role — closer to a foundation-board seat or a cooperative's founding member than to a shareholding — in the governance of an infrastructure layer for verified truth.

### The Entry

| Generation | Range | Entry contribution | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Founding (Genesis)** | 1–10 | $0.01 + optional voluntary contribution | Accepted the role before any proof of concept existed |
| **First** | 11–100 | $100K–$10M, negotiated | Joined at proof-of-concept stage |
| **Second** | 101–max | $10M+, negotiated | Joined at growth stage |

The $0.01 minimum is deliberate: SVE filters by comprehension and commitment, not capital. A Founding Parent+ may add a voluntary contribution above that minimum, but it changes nothing about governance, veto, or dividend rights — those are equal across the Founding generation regardless of contribution size. The role's real cost is accepting veto responsibility, reputational exposure, and a multi-generational horizon with no exit and no guarantee — not the entry price.

### Admission

Not a market — a family council. A candidate needs **unanimous** approval from existing Parents+, who weigh purity of motive and mission understanding before financial capacity. Deadlock past 6 months escalates to a Veche 2/3 vote, so no single veto can permanently block growth. All admission negotiations are logged publicly with stated reasoning; a rejected candidate may appeal to the **4+1 AI Oracle** for a non-binding opinion, and the Veche may override a rejection found to be self-protective rather than mission-driven. Existing Parents+ must recuse from any admission where they hold a direct conflict of interest.

### What Parents+ Receive

- **Dividends** — 25% of distributable surplus (net operating profit after the Foundation sets aside operational-continuity reserves, development reserves, and an open-grants pool — reserve sizing set by Veche vote, not management), split equally among active Parents+. Not guaranteed; depends entirely on operational profitability. If SVE reaches ≥$500M annual distributable surplus, the Veche may revisit the 25% allocation by 2/3 vote (downward) or Parents+ unanimous consent (upward) — the formula is a starting point, not dogma.
- **Chairmanship** — a permanent governance seat; the right to convene sessions.
- **A protective veto** — the right to block a decision that harms vulnerable populations or weakens a Core Principle. Not a right to direct strategy.
- **The Public Registry** — a permanent, cryptographically signed, timestamped record.
- **Optional Impact Designation** — up to 100% of a personal dividend share may be redirected, publicly and irrevocably for the declared period, to a public-benefit fund of the Parent+'s choosing.

**Participation is required**, not passive: no governance participation for 24 consecutive months triggers a grace-period review; beyond 36 months, dividend entitlement is suspended pending Veche review.

**What Parents+ do not control:** the Vision or Core Principles (both constitutionally fixed), the direction of the independent attorney network, reserve sizing, or admission of new Parents+ without unanimous consent (subject to the 6-month Veche override).

**Exit & succession:** the role is not a tradeable asset and has no secondary market. On death or formal withdrawal it passes to a named successor (subject to existing Parents+ approval) or returns to the Foundation pool; the historical record of who founded it stays permanent either way.

## Legal Status — Open Questions (Germany)

A formal request for an initial legal assessment (*Erstberatung*, § 34 RVG) has been prepared for German counsel, asking whether the Parents+ structure described above qualifies as a security, an asset investment (*Vermögensanlage*), or an investment fund under German law (WpHG, KWG, VermAnlG, KAGB) — and if so, what minimal changes would let it launch without a prospectus or BaFin authorization. **No opinion has been received yet.** Nothing in this README should be read as resolving that question either way; it is open, and treated as open.

For reference, the structural comparison put to counsel:

- **Closer to:** a foundation-board seat (oversight, not ownership); a cooperative member (voting + profit share + participation duty); a founding association member (governance rights, no tradeable interest).
- **Distinct from:** shares or GmbH interests (no ownership right, no capital-weighted vote, no trading); investment-fund units (no redemption, no NAV, no fund structure); profit-participation loans/*Genussrechte* (no subordinated capital, no loss participation beyond the $0.01 entry); tokens or crypto-assets (no digital asset, no blockchain-based trading).

The specific questions put to counsel: (1) is Parents+ a *Wertpapier* under §2 WpHG — specifically, a transferable security tradeable on a capital market; (2) is it a *Vermögensanlage* under §1 VermAnlG; (3) does the SVE Foundation itself constitute an *Investmentvermögen* under §1 KAGB; (4) if any of the above, does a prospectus requirement attach, and do the small-offer exemptions apply; (5) does accepting Parents+ contributions require BaFin authorization; (6) if the current structure is regulation-relevant, what is the minimal change that avoids a prospectus and BaFin authorization requirement.
