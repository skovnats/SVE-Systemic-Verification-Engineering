# S.V.E. PATCH C — GOVERNANCE & VOTING
## (Anti-Capture Core)

**Patch ID:** C  
**Applies To:** S.V.E. v3.0+  
**Type:** Operational  
**Status:** Mandatory

---

## C.1 Bodies

Recognized bodies MAY include:
- Veche (human collective)
- AI validators
- Custodians
- Temporary emergency roles

No body is permanent.

---

## C.2 Voting Thresholds (Default)

- Routine decisions: >50%  
- Patch adoption: ≥67%  
- Emergency escalation: ≥51% + audit  
- Spirit-related disputes: Patch A governs

Thresholds MAY NOT be lowered by Patch.

---

## C.3 Anti-Capture Mechanisms

Mandatory features:
- Term limits
- Randomized sub-juries
- Delayed execution for high-impact decisions
- Automatic quorum decay (prevents stagnation)

---

## C.4 Founder Limitation

The Founder:
- Has no veto
- Has no unilateral control
- Is subject to recall and audit

---

## C.5 Audit of Auditors

All oversight bodies are themselves auditable.

Recursive accountability is mandatory.
