### PART II: ELDER BROTHER STATUS (Долгосрочное членство)

#### 1. Vesting Mechanism: "9-Year Rule"

**Core Principle:**
> **"If you contribute ≥1 meaningful work to S.V.E. within any 9-year period, you remain S.V.E. Family with full rights."**

**What counts as "1 meaningful work":**
- Research paper using S.V.E. principles (peer-reviewed or pre-print)
- Code contribution (≥100 lines merged to S.V.E. repositories)
- Patch proposal (submitted via Patch 1.39 protocol, even if rejected)
- Community service (organize conference, translate docs, mentor fellows)
- Whistleblower action (report violation leading to enforcement)
- Teaching (course/workshop on S.V.E. principles, ≥10 students)

**Minimum quality bar:**
- Must be **public** (no secret contributions)
- Must be **S.V.E.-licensed** or directly support S.V.E. ecosystem
- Must pass **peer review** (community vote: ≥51% "this counts as meaningful")

**Renewal:**
- Every 9 years, review: "Did Elder Brother contribute ≥1 work in past 9 years?"
- If YES → status continues another 9 years
- If NO → status lapses (but can be re-earned by new contribution)

**Rationale for 9 years:**
- Biblical: 9 = 3×3 (completeness, fruitfulness)
- Practical: Long enough for life circumstances (health, family, sabbatical) but requires re-engagement
- Prevents: Dead weight (must stay minimally active)
- Enables: Long-term wisdom retention

#### 2. Elder Brother Rights & Responsibilities

**RIGHTS (Equal to Active Workers):**

**A. Voting Power:**
- Full vote in Veche decisions (simple majority, 51%)
- Full vote in structural amendments (supermajority, 67%)
- Full vote in Keeper selection/removal (if applicable)
- **Weight:** 1 vote = 1 Elder Brother = 1 Active Worker (pure equality)

**B. Dividend Rights:**
- Share in Fund Distribution per v1.4 Appendix B:
  - 20% "Active Contributors" pool now = **"Active Contributors + Elder Brothers"**
  - Split **pro-rata** based on contribution hours in past year
  - **Minimum share:** Even if Elder Brother contributed only "1 work" (minimal hours), they receive at least 1% of personal allocation (dignity floor)

**C. Family Benefits:**
- Legal Consortium access (Article II, v1.4)
- Informant Priority (4-8% if report violations, Article I.1)
- Evidence Fund eligibility (Patch 1.47)
- S.V.E. Family public recognition (listed on website/docs as "Elder Brother")
- Access to internal forums, strategy discussions

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

**A. Mentorship (Encouraged, not mandatory):**
- Guide new fellows/contributors
- Share institutional knowledge
- Review applications (optional but valued)

**B. Wisdom Council (Optional participation):**
- Elder Brothers form advisory body to Keeper
- Non-binding recommendations on strategic decisions
- Meets quarterly or as needed

**C. Quality Guardian (Active when voting):**
- Vote thoughtfully (not rubber-stamp)
- Prioritize Spirit over personal interest
- Challenge decisions misaligned with Love/Truth/Justice/Mercy/Sacrifice (Patch 1.33)

**D. Continued Minimal Engagement:**
- Must produce ≥1 meaningful work per 9 years (see above)
- Cannot go fully dormant
- If unable to contribute due to health/hardship → can request grace period (Veche vote, case-by-case)

#### 3. Earning Elder Brother Status

**Path 1: Research Fellowship (Standard)**
- Complete ≥12 months as S.V.E. Fellow (€8,888/month program)
- Maintain S.V.E. Tier A compliance throughout
- Produce ≥1 peer-reviewed output
- **Upon completion:** Automatic Elder Brother status granted

**Path 2: Sustained Contribution (Alternative)**
- Contribute to S.V.E. for ≥24 consecutive months (without fellowship)
- ≥500 hours total (documented via public logs)
- Community vote: 67% approve Elder Brother status
- **Upon approval:** Elder Brother status granted

**Path 3: Exceptional Service (Rare)**
- Single transformational contribution (e.g., won major legal case for S.V.E., created critical infrastructure, saved S.V.E. from existential threat)
- Keeper nominates
- Veche vote: 75% supermajority required
- **Upon approval:** Honorary Elder Brother status (immediate)

**Difference from Active Workers:**
- **Active Workers:** Contributing NOW (monthly/quarterly basis), receive 20% pool based on current hours
- **Elder Brothers:** Contributed HISTORICALLY, receive share if contributed ≥1 work in past 9 years
- **Both:** Equal voting power, both in 20% distribution pool (pro-rata by recent hours)

#### 4. Loss of Elder Brother Status

**Automatic Loss (No vote needed):**
- 9 years elapse with ZERO meaningful contributions (status lapses)
- S.V.E. License violation (transparency breach, fraud, Spirit violation per Patch 1.33)
- Criminal conviction related to dishonesty (fraud, perjury, corruption)

**Vote-Based Removal (67% Veche supermajority):**
- Systematic undermining of S.V.E. principles (lobbying against transparency, attacking fellows/whistleblowers)
- Severe conflict of interest (joined competitor, working against S.V.E.)
- Abuse of Elder Brother status (claiming authority without recent contribution)

**Voluntary Resignation:**
- Elder Brother can resign anytime (dignity preserved, can return later if contribute again)

**Restoration After Loss:**
- If lost via 9-year lapse → automatic restoration upon new contribution
- If lost via violation/removal → requires Veche vote (75% supermajority) + redemption period (≥12 months good-faith contribution)

### PART IX: KNOWN LIMITATIONS

**1. Long-term Fund Sustainability**
- **Issue:** If Fund shrinks, fellowships must be cut (Fellows lose income)
- **Mitigation:** Fellows know stipend is Fund-dependent, not guaranteed; Emergency mode (v1.43) reduces to €4,444/month before eliminating
- **Responsibility:** Keeper must monitor Fund health, warn Fellows 6 months before cuts

**2. 9-Year Vesting Creates "Ghosts"**
- **Issue:** Elder Brothers who contribute minimal "1 work" every 9 years but add little value
- **Mitigation:** Dignity floor is small (≥1% of allocation); Veche can vote to raise bar for "meaningful work" if gaming detected
- **Accept:** Some inefficiency acceptable to honor principle of long-term loyalty

**3. Tax Complexity (International Fellows)**
- **Issue:** €8,888/month taxed differently in Estonia vs Brazil vs India
- **Mitigation:** Fellowship is gross income; tax responsibility on Fellow; S.V.E. provides documentation but does not withhold
- **Recommendation:** Fellows consult local accountant

**4. Brain Drain from Academia**
- **Issue:** If S.V.E. Fellowship too attractive, universities lose good researchers
- **Response:** FEATURE, not bug. Academic capture is the problem; S.V.E. offers alternative. Universities can compete by improving transparency.
- **But:** Maintain partnerships with ethical universities (co-appointments possible if S.V.E. Tier A maintained)
