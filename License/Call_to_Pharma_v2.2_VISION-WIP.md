## A Word Before We Begin

This document is addressed to pharmaceutical companies. But it is ultimately about patients.

## The Problem You Already Know

The pharmaceutical industry has a trust deficit priced into every interaction you have with regulators, insurers, patients, and courts.

**What distrust costs you today:**

- **Litigation reserves.** Vioxx cost Merck $4.85B. Opioid settlements exceeded $50B. Every major pharma company maintains reserves for undisclosed adverse events — including companies with clean data who subsidise the industry's history.
- **Regulatory friction.** EMA and FDA request additional rounds because they expect incompleteness. Each round costs months and millions.
- **Insurance premiums.** Product liability pricing reflects the actuarial expectation that data has been withheld. Clean companies pay the same premium as dirty ones.
- **Reputational discount.** Investors apply a blanket "pharma discount" to earnings. You pay it regardless of your own data practices.
- **Talent flight.** Researchers increasingly decline positions at companies associated with data suppression.

**The core problem:** The market cannot distinguish honest companies from dishonest ones. SVE solves the distinguishability problem.

## What We Offer

### The SVE-Verified Badge

A publicly auditable certification — cryptographically signed, publicly registered — confirming independent SVE audit. Four tiers, each with a distinct transparency commitment. **There is no fixed premium attached to any tier.** The market — insurers, hospital procurement committees, physicians, patients — decides what verified trust at each level is worth, up to a maximum of 25% above a verified non-inflated baseline.

| Tier | Data Commitment |
|------|---|
| **Bronze** | Trial completeness: all NCT-registered trials have published top-line results (positive *and* negative) within 24 months of completion |
| **Silver** | Bronze + summary results published proactively; adverse event timeline disclosed |
| **Gold** | Silver + raw data available to qualified researchers under NDA; methodology documented and independently reproducible |
| **Platinum** | Gold + financial transparency (R&D costs, pricing methodology per Appendix B); company on SVE Corporate Transformation Path (App. B §§10–22) |

**On competitive data:** Bronze and Silver require results only — not raw data. Gold and Platinum require raw data access for *qualified researchers under NDA*, not competitors. Proprietary formulations, manufacturing processes, and trade secrets are protected at all tiers (v1.3 §5 exclusion). SVE requires disclosure of *what the drug does*, not *how it is made*.

**On pricing:** SVE does not authorise, set, or coordinate pharmaceutical prices. It certifies the data underlying value-based pricing — a framework already recognised in EU HTA, UK NICE, and US outcomes-based contracts. Any pricing decisions remain entirely the company's responsibility under applicable law. Each company should seek independent legal counsel regarding pricing strategies in their jurisdiction. SVE certification does not constitute legal advice on pricing.

**On baseline integrity:** The DVN audits baseline pricing against external benchmarks (IQVIA, national HTA reference prices, regional affordability standards). If the baseline is found inflated, the SVE-Verified badge is suspended until correction. The maximum market premium ceiling of 25% applies only above a verified non-inflated baseline.

## What You Get

### 1. The Compliance Shield *(the most valuable benefit)*

An SVE-Verified company in good standing is **protected from SVE enforcement actions** (Appendix E).

**When the Shield activates:** Upon signing Phase 1 (Expression of Interest), the company enters a Safe Harbor period. The Attorney Incentive Program is paused for 12 months while the audit is conducted, provided good faith cooperation is maintained. Full Shield activates upon certification.

**What the Shield covers:** SVE attorneys cannot target you under the Attorney Incentive Program. Your SVE-Verified status serves as affirmative defense in any SVE-related dispute. Whistleblower claims are routed through the audit process first — not directly to litigation.

**What the Shield does not cover:** Criminal investigations, regulatory actions (FDA, EMA, BfArM), claims brought by third parties outside the SVE framework. The Shield eliminates the SVE-specific enforcement vector — which is the one SVE controls.

**Duration:** The Shield lasts as long as your compliance lasts. If an audit reveals concealment, the Shield drops publicly and standard enforcement applies retroactively — including attorney shares up to 95%.

### 2. Litigation Cost Reduction

Companies with proactive disclosure practices face fewer product liability suits and defend those that arise more effectively: no discovery surprises, no "what were they hiding?" narrative, shifted jury perception. Industry benchmarks for companies with systematic disclosure policies show estimated 30–60% reduction in product liability costs. This benefit exists **independent of whether any market premium materialises**.

### 3. Regulatory Acceleration *(medium-term goal)*

SVE is actively seeking formal EMA/FDA recognition of SVE-certified data packages as meeting ICH E6(R3) and EU CTR 536/2014 transparency requirements. Early adopters will be positioned to benefit first when recognition arrives.

### 4. ESG and Impact Capital Access

Institutional investors with ESG mandates increasingly require transparency certifications for pharma holdings. SVE-Verified provides a standardised, auditable signal for capital pools that currently exclude pharma due to data opacity.

### 5. Procurement Advantage

Hospitals and insurers increasingly include transparency criteria in formulary and procurement decisions. SVE-Verified provides a standardised, publicly auditable transparency score — a differentiator in tenders where "trust" is otherwise unmeasurable.

### 6. Exit Without Penalty

If a certified company determines that SVE-Verified status is not producing expected market benefits, it may voluntarily exit the program without penalty, provided:
- No outstanding audit findings indicate concealment.
- Exit is publicly announced (no quiet withdrawal).
- The exit does not constitute an admission of any violation.

This ensures companies can engage with the pilot program without being locked in. Transparency in exit, as in entry.

This is the right question to ask, and it deserves a direct answer.

If insurers, hospitals, and governments decline to reward SVE-Verified drugs — which is possible, especially in price-controlled markets — the market premium is simply zero. **The following benefits remain regardless:**

1. **Litigation cost reduction** (30–60% estimated, independent of premium)

3. **Regulatory acceleration** (when EMA/FDA recognition is achieved)

For a mid-size biotech carrying $50M+ in litigation reserves, the Shield and litigation reduction alone likely justify compliance cost — before any premium is considered.

**The honest position:** The market premium is a plausible hypothesis supported by analogous certifications in other industries. It is not guaranteed. The pilot program exists precisely to test this hypothesis with real data. We do not promise a number. We promise a signal — and the market's response will be what it is.

## The Patient-First Clause *(non-negotiable)*

SVE-Verified status is not a licence to extract from patients:

1. **Patient Access Program:** A minimum of 5% of any premium revenue from SVE-Verified products must fund patient assistance or access programs in lower-income markets. Audited annually.
2. **No baseline inflation:** Badge suspended if baseline price is found inflated relative to external benchmarks.
3. **Post-market commitment:** Silver and above must submit real-world outcomes data at 36 months post-launch. If real-world data materially contradicts trial findings, certification is reviewed.
4. **Sunset clause:** SVE-Verified status expires if transparency lapses. Revocation is public.

**The Fruits Test (§0):** If SVE-Verified certification results in patients paying more without measurable safety or efficacy benefit, that outcome triggers a review of the mechanism itself. The goal is not that verified drugs cost more. The goal is that hiding data costs everything — and that patients receive honest information about every drug they take.

## FAQ

**Q: Who sets the premium?**
The market. SVE certifies the data. Insurers, hospitals, procurement committees, and patients decide what verified trust is worth to them. We do not promise a number.

**Q: Who conducts the audit?**
Independent auditors supervised by the DVN. No conflicts of interest. Audit methodology is public and reproducible.

**Q: Can competitors see our data?**
Bronze/Silver: results only. Gold/Platinum: raw data to qualified researchers under NDA. Proprietary formulations and manufacturing processes protected at all tiers.

**Q: What if no market premium materialises?**
Shield, litigation reduction, ESG capital, and regulatory benefits remain. See the "What if no market premium?" section.

**Q: Can we exit if it doesn't work?**
Yes. Voluntary exit is available without penalty, provided no audit findings indicate concealment and exit is publicly announced.

**Q: Is this antitrust-compliant?**
SVE does not set prices or coordinate pricing between companies. Each company makes independent pricing decisions. Independent legal counsel is recommended for jurisdiction-specific guidance.
