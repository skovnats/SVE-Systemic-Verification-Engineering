## Why nothing here can be enclosed — yet all of it stays open

The deliberate asymmetry of the SVE ecosystem: **closed in exactly one direction — against privatization of any kind — and open in every other.** Value flows *in* to the commons and out *to people*; it cannot flow *out* into private ownership. (A one-way valve, or a ratchet: it only turns toward openness.) Two locks hold it:

1. **Against patent enclosure — defensive publication (works against everyone).** A dated public disclosure is *prior art*: under the novelty bar (35 U.S.C. § 102; EPC Art. 54) it blocks third parties from later patenting the same invention. The DOI and Zenodo timestamp fix the date publicly and immutably. *(Standard, well-established mechanism — the ground for invalidation / opposition, not a claim that no patent office ever errs.)*
2. **Against copyright enclosure — copyleft + SVE (binds anyone who builds on the work).** Under **SVE Meta-License + CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**, copyright is turned *against* lock-up: every user must keep the work and its derivatives **open and share-alike**, **attribute** it, stay **non-commercial** except via SVE commercial tiers, and **never re-license it proprietary or patent SVE-derived ideas.** Concealment-on-doubt triggers audit (Appendix A / F).

**Closed to enclosure — patent or copyright. Open to all of humanity.**
In one line: `SVE ≡ FOSS + a taboo on closing ideas/derivatives (+ audit on doubt).`

> This "closed" is **not secrecy — it is the opposite.** *Closed-to-capture, not closed-to-view:* everything is public; only taking it private is forbidden.
