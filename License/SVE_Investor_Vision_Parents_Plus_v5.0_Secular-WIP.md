## The Problem at Civilizational Scale

Humanity has built extraordinary systems for creating wealth, knowledge, and power. It has not built adequate systems for verifying the truthfulness of the claims those systems make.

The consequences are measurable:

- **~85%** of preclinical biomedical research cannot be reproduced. Billions invested annually in science that cannot be verified.
- **$50B+** in pharmaceutical settlements for suppressed clinical data — drugs approved on incomplete information, reaching patients whose physicians did not know what was hidden.
- **Trillions** in mispriced assets annually due to information asymmetry between insiders and markets.
- **Algorithmic systems** making decisions affecting hundreds of millions of people with methodologies that are proprietary, unauditable, and unaccountable.
- **Legal systems** where the quality of representation depends on price, not truth — and where concealment is structurally rewarded until caught.

This is not a crisis of individual bad actors. **It is a structural failure.** S.V.E. is the structural correction.

### The Principle

When the first Parent+ joins, they accept the role. When a second person wishes to become a Parent+, they cannot simply apply to the Foundation. They must be accepted **unanimously** by existing Parents+ — who evaluate not just financial capacity but purity of motive, understanding of mission, and readiness to accept the veto responsibility.

This is not a market. It is a family council. The evaluation is qualitative first, financial second.

All admission contributions flow **100% to the SVE Foundation** — to fund operations, enforcement, and mission infrastructure. Existing Parents+ do not receive direct cash distributions from admission events.

## The "Parents+" Role — What You Are and Are Not

**What Parents+ receive:**

**1. Dividends** — **25% of distributable surplus**, defined as: net operating profit, *after* the Foundation has set aside:
- Operational continuity reserves (minimum 6 months of operating costs)
- Development reserves (allocated by Veche for roadmap priorities)
- Open grants pool (for independent verification research and legal aid)

The size and allocation of all reserves is determined by **Veche vote**, not by the Foundation's management unilaterally. This ensures that Parents+ dividends are never extracted at the expense of the Foundation's mission or resilience.

This 25% is distributed equally among all **active** Parents+. Each Parent+'s individual share = 25% ÷ total number of active Parents+. As the community grows, the per-person share decreases — but the absolute pool grows with SVE's revenues.

**Veche override on dividend percentage:** If SVE reaches an annual distributable surplus of **$500M or more**, the Veche may initiate a review of the 25% Parents+ allocation by 2/3 vote. The allocation may be adjusted downward (releasing more to mission) or, with unanimous Parents+ consent, upward. This clause exists because the structure is not dogma — it is a starting point. If the fruits of the structure are disproportionate to its mission, the structure is revised.

**Participation requirement:** A Parent+ who does not participate in governance for more than 24 consecutive months without declared reason enters a grace period. If absence continues beyond 36 months, dividend entitlement is suspended and reviewed by the Veche. Parents+ are guardians, not passive recipients.

**2. Compounding from Foundation growth** — each new admission strengthens the Foundation's operational base, growing the dividend pool for all existing Parents+.

**3. Chairmanship** — Permanent seat at the governance table. The right to initiate discussions and call extraordinary sessions.

**4. Veto Right** — The right to block any decision that harms vulnerable populations, weakens the Core Principles, or violates the Fruits Test. This is a *protective* veto: *"I do not dictate direction. I prevent destruction."*

**5. The Public Registry** — Every Parent+ is permanently recorded, cryptographically signed, immutably timestamped. The list of founding Parents+ will have meaning in 50 years that cannot be purchased later at any price.

**Optional: Impact Designation** — Each Parent+ may designate up to 100% of their personal dividend share to a public-benefit fund of their choosing. This designation is public, voluntary, and irrevocable for the period declared. It is not required. It is available.

**What Parents+ do NOT control:**
- The Vision (defined by Meta-License v4.0, immutable)
- The framework's Core Principles (constitutionally protected)
- The direction of enforcement (the Attorney network operates independently)
- Reserve allocations (determined by Veche, not by Parents+ alone)
- Admission of new Parents+ without unanimous consent (subject to Veche override after 6 months)

### Exit & Succession

Parents+ is a role, not a tradeable asset. It cannot be sold on a secondary market.

Upon a Parent+'s death or formal withdrawal:
- The role passes to a designated successor (named in the Registry), subject to approval by existing Parents+
- If no successor is designated, the dividend entitlement returns to the Foundation pool
- The historical record is permanent — the name and founding contribution remain forever

*Guardians are not owners. They are accountable for what they protect — precisely because they did not create it and cannot fully control it.*
