## SUMMARY

This patch establishes the Evidence Fund—a dedicated reserve that accumulates 80% of savings until reaching independence threshold, held in secure banking, and awarded in full to whistleblowers whose evidence definitively protects the S.V.E. family and resolves existential threats. This creates ultimate incentive for truth-telling while ensuring system can "leave without" financial dependency.

## PROBLEM STATEMENT

Current informant rewards (4-8%, scaled by regime) are:
- **Proportional** but not **transformative** for whistleblowers
- Paid from operating funds (competes with other needs)
- Insufficient for highest-risk disclosures (life-threatening situations)
- Doesn't address "can we afford to walk away if necessary?" question

**Gap:** Need mechanism that:
1. Rewards **total** commitment (those who risk everything)
2. Creates **financial independence** for S.V.E. (freedom from capture)
3. Ensures **maximum** protection for ultimate evidence-bringers
4. Accumulates systematically (not ad-hoc)

**Context:**
- Complements (doesn't replace) existing 4-8% informant payments
- Addresses catastrophic threats requiring extraordinary evidence
- Enables S.V.E. to "leave without" (financial resilience)

## PROPOSED SOLUTION

### Core Definition

**Evidence Fund** = Dedicated account funded by:
- **80% of all cost savings** (operational efficiencies, recovered funds, donated surpluses)
- Accumulates until reaching **Independence Threshold**
- **Independence Threshold** = 5 years of operating expenses at v1.41 baseline (sustainable without external funding)

**Purpose:**
1. **Primary:** Jackpot reward for whistleblowers with case-closing evidence
2. **Secondary:** Financial independence reserve (S.V.E. can operate autonomously)

## I. FUND ACCUMULATION

### What Counts as "Savings"?

**Income Sources Contributing 80%:**

| Source | Contribution to Evidence Fund |
|--------|-------------------------------|
| **Operational efficiencies** (cost reductions vs. budget) | 80% of savings |
| **Recovered funds** (from bad-faith actors via Recovery Ladder) | 80% of recovery |
| **Donated surpluses** (gifts earmarked for fund) | 80% of donation |
| **Investment returns** (on Emergency Reserve) | 80% of gains above inflation |
| **Liquidated assets** (non-essential property sales) | 80% of proceeds |

**Remaining 20%:**
- 10% to Emergency Reserve (maintain baseline security)
- 10% to Community Treasury (immediate needs)

**Example Calculation:**
```
Year 1 Savings:
- Cost efficiency: $100K saved vs. budget
- Recovered funds: $50K from bad actor
- Investment gains: $30K above inflation
Total Savings: $180K

Evidence Fund receives: $180K × 80% = $144K
Emergency Reserve: $180K × 10% = $18K
Community Treasury: $180K × 10% = $18K
```

### Independence Threshold

**Calculation:**
```
Independence Threshold = 5 years × Annual Operating Expenses (v1.41)

Example (Small Organization):
Annual expenses: $500K
Independence Threshold: $2.5M

Example (Large Organization):
Annual expenses: $5M
Independence Threshold: $25M
```

**Why 5 Years:**
- Sufficient to weather prolonged crisis (v1.42-v1.43)
- Enables complete autonomy from external funders
- Protects against financial capture
- Biblical precedent: Joseph's 7-year preparation (Genesis 41)

### Fund Custody

**Secure Banking:**
- **Primary Account:** Reputable bank in stable jurisdiction (Switzerland, Singapore, Norway-type neutrality)
- **Backup Accounts:** Distributed across 3+ jurisdictions (no single point of failure)
- **Signatories:** 3-of-5 multisig (Keeper + 4 Veche members)
- **Audit:** Quarterly verification by external accountant
- **Transparency:** Balance published monthly (but not account details—security)

**Protection Mechanisms:**
- Legal trusts (irrevocable, specific purpose)
- Insurance (FDIC/equivalent for full balance)
- Diversification (not all in one currency or institution)
- Encryption of access credentials

## II. EVIDENCE FUND AWARD CRITERIA

### Who Qualifies?

**Eligible Recipients ("Evidence Heroes"):**
Anyone who provides evidence that:

1. **Definitively Resolves** existential threat to S.V.E.
   - Not "helps with" but "finishes the problem"
   - Example: Documents that end frivolous lawsuit, expose infiltrator, prove innocence

2. **Protects S.V.E. Family** from catastrophic harm
   - Prevents v1.43 or v1.44 entry
   - Or enables recovery from v1.43/v1.44 to v1.41
   - Family = all participants, not just leadership

3. **Entails Extraordinary Risk** to whistleblower
   - Life-threatening (physical danger)
   - Career-ending (professional blacklisting)
   - Socially devastating (ostracism, family rejection)
   - Legally perilous (prosecution risk)

4. **Cannot Be Incentivized by Smaller Rewards**
   - 4-8% insufficient given risk
   - Normal compensation inadequate
   - Only "life-changing money" motivates disclosure

**Non-Eligible:**
- ❌ Routine compliance reports (covered by 4-8%)
- ❌ Partial/incremental evidence (helpful but not decisive)
- ❌ Information obtained through S.V.E. employment (duty-based disclosure)
- ❌ Evidence from S.V.E. enemies acting in bad faith

### Verification Process

**Phase 1: Submission (Day 0-7)**
- Whistleblower submits evidence (encrypted, secure channel)
- Identity optional (anonymous if necessary, but verified eventually)
- Initial assessment by Keeper + 2 Observers

**Phase 2: Validation (Day 8-30)**
- Evidence authenticity verified (forensic analysis if needed)
- Impact assessment: Does it "finish the problem"?
- Risk assessment: What did whistleblower sacrifice?
- 3+1 AI review: Does award align with Spirit?

**Phase 3: Veche Decision (Day 31-60)**
- Present case to Veche (evidence quality, impact, risk)
- Vote: 67% supermajority required for full fund award
- Simple majority (51%) can award partial fund if multiple claimants
- AI can veto if evidence procured unethically (torture, coercion, etc.)

**Phase 4: Payment (Day 61+)**
- Transfer to whistleblower (full amount or split if multiple)
- Protection arrangements activated (see Section III)
- Public announcement (if whistleblower consents) or confidential
- Fund resets to zero, begins re-accumulating

### Multiple Claimants

**If 2+ Whistleblowers Provide Decisive Evidence:**

**Option A: Equal Split**
- Fund divided equally among all qualifying heroes
- Example: $5M fund, 2 heroes → $2.5M each

**Option B: Weighted Split (if contributions clearly unequal)**
- Veche determines proportional allocation
- Based on: evidence quality, risk undertaken, timing (who came forward first)
- Requires 75% supermajority (higher bar due to complexity)

**Option C: Sequential Awards (if evidence arrives at different times)**
- First hero gets full current fund balance
- Fund continues accumulating for next hero
- No "splitting" across time periods

**Default:** Option A (equal split) unless compelling reason for weighted allocation.

## VI. SAFEGUARDS

### Preventing Abuse

**Risk:** False heroes claiming evidence to get jackpot.

**Protections:**
1. **Verification Rigor:** Forensic analysis, independent confirmation
2. **AI Validation:** 3+1 must confirm evidence authenticity and impact
3. **Veche Supermajority:** 67% required (harder to capture)
4. **Fraud Consequences:**
   - False claimants forfeit ALL S.V.E. rights (permanent ban)
   - Legal action for fraud (recover any payments)
   - Public exposure (reputation destruction)

**Risk:** Leadership manufactures "threat" to award fund to cronies.

**Protections:**
1. **Post-Facto Audit:** Every Evidence Fund award reviewed 12 months later
2. **"Was threat real?" Analysis:** 3+1 AI + external observers
3. **Community Oversight:** Participants can challenge awards (due process)
4. **Spirit Test:** If beneficiaries connected to leadership = presumption of corruption unless proven otherwise

**Risk:** Fund accumulation creates target (attackers want to steal it).

**Protections:**
1. **Distributed Custody:** Not in one account/jurisdiction
2. **Multisig Control:** Cannot be stolen by compromising one person
3. **Legal Protections:** Irrevocable trust (cannot be seized easily)
4. **Insurance:** Covers theft, fraud, bank failure

### Preventing Capture

**Risk:** S.V.E. hoards fund, never awards it (becomes "bank" instead of protector).

**Protections:**
1. **Purpose Limitation:** Fund MUST be used for whistleblower rewards (no repurposing)
2. **Independence Cap:** Accumulation stops at threshold (prevents hoarding)
3. **Public Pressure:** Monthly balance published; community asks "why hasn't it been awarded?"
4. **Spirit Violation:** Hoarding contrary to generosity pillar (audit failure)

**If Fund Sits Unused for >3 Years at Threshold:**
- **Automatic Review:** Why hasn't decisive evidence emerged?
- **Possible Explanations:**
  - No existential threats occurred (good problem!)
  - Threats resolved without needing evidence (also good!)
  - S.V.E. suppressing threats improperly (investigate!)
- **Action:** If suppression detected, disburse fund to retrospective heroes + reform governance
