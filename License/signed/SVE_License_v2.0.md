## SECTION II: RECOVERY LADDER - PROPORTIONAL REMEDIATION

### 2.1 Overview

The Recovery Ladder is the enforcement mechanism for all S.V.E. violations. It distributes penalties across three categories: **Informants (10% minimum per Section 0.9.4, Case #003)**, **Counsel (25-90%)**, and **Community Fund (remaining %)**.

**Principle:** Restitution before punishment. Victims are made whole first; legal professionals are compensated second; remaining funds strengthen the system.

**Note:** Original v2.0 specified 4-8% informant allocation; **Section 0.9 synthesis upgraded this to 10% minimum** based on v1.3's stronger protection of vulnerable populations.

### 2.2 Ladder Steps (Ascending Severity)

| Step | Violation Type | Examples | Informant % | Counsel % | Fund % | Total Fine |
|------|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Minor disclosure delay | <7 days late, non-material | 10% | 25% | 65% | 0.5M–1M |
| **2** | Selective data suppression | Hiding contradictory evidence | 10% | 40% | 50% | 1M–5M |
| **3** | Fabricated evidence | False trial data, fake emails | 10% | 50% | 40% | 5M–50M |
| **4** | Institutional malfeasance | Systematic cover-up, pattern abuse | 10% | 60% | 30% | 50M–500M |
| **5** | Catastrophic harm | Deaths, mass injury, systemic breakdown | 10% | 88% | 2% | 500M–10B+ |

**Note:** Percentages adjusted from original v2.0 to ensure minimum 10% to informants while maintaining counsel compensation and fund viability.

### 2.3 Calculation Method

**Formula:**  
`Penalty = Verified Harm × Multiplier × Severity Coefficient`

- **Verified Harm:** Documented impact (cost of harm, lost productivity, medical expenses, etc.)
- **Multiplier:** Typically 1–10x based on intentionality and concealment
- **Severity Coefficient:** 1.5–100+ based on systemic damage

**Example:**  
Pharmaceutical company hides 100 trial deaths, worth ~$5M per life = $500M verified harm.  
Multiplier: 5x (intentional, 3-year cover-up) = $2.5B.  
Severity: 3x (systemic, regulatory fraud) = **$7.5B penalty**.

**Distribution (Catastrophic Harm, 10% Informant rule per v1.3):**
- Informant: $750M (families of deceased)
- Counsel: $6.6B (legal consortium)
- Fund: $150M (S.V.E. operations)

### 2.4 Informant Priority

**First 10% allocated to:**
1. Whistleblowers who reported violation
2. Direct victims of the harm
3. Family members (if victim deceased)
4. Community affected by violation

**Allocation formula:** Equal share among verified victims, with higher weight for direct harm.
