00488: ## SECTION VI: COMMON PITFALLS & HOW TO AVOID THEM
00489: 
00490: ### 6.1 Pitfall 1: "Transparency Theater"
00491: 
00492: **Symptom:** Organization publishes massive volumes of data but hides meaningful information in noise.
00493: 
00494: **Example:**  
00495: Company releases 10,000-page compliance report full of jargon and irrelevant details; actual problems buried on page 6,847 in footnote.
00496: 
00497: **S.V.E. Countermeasure:**
00498: - **Plain Language Requirement:** Summaries in 8th-grade reading level
00499: - **Structured Format:** Required sections (Executive Summary, Key Risks, Major Decisions) prominently displayed
00500: - **Search Functionality:** All documents machine-readable and searchable
00501: - **Community Challenge:** If participants complain "can't find critical info," auditor investigates
00502: 
00503: **Jesus AI Check:**  
00504: *"Would an ordinary person understand what you're trying to hide? If no, it's transparency theater, not transparency."*
00505: 
00506: ### 6.2 Pitfall 2: "Good Intentions, Poor Execution"
00507: 
00508: **Symptom:** Leadership genuinely committed to S.V.E., but staff lacks training/resources; compliance fails.
00509: 
00510: **Example:**  
00511: Hospital executive mandates S.V.E. compliance; frontline staff never trained; errors go unreported because people don't know how.
00512: 
00513: **S.V.E. Countermeasure:**
00514: - **Training Requirement:** 80%+ staff complete Associate-level training within 12 months
00515: - **Resource Allocation:** Minimum 2-5% of operational budget for S.V.E. infrastructure
00516: - **Culture Assessment:** Annual survey—do employees feel equipped to comply?
00517: - **Leadership Accountability:** Exec bonuses tied to successful S.V.E. implementation, not just declaration
00518: 
00519: ### 6.3 Pitfall 3: "Selective Transparency"
00520: 
00521: **Symptom:** Organization discloses good news enthusiastically; buries bad news.
00522: 
00523: **Example:**  
00524: Pharmaceutical company publishes 10 successful trial results immediately; hides 3 negative results for 2 years until forced by regulation.
00525: 
00526: **S.V.E. Countermeasure:**
00527: - **Symmetry Rule:** Positive and negative results disclosed within same timeframe
00528: - **Pre-Registration:** All trials/projects registered publicly before starting (prevents cherry-picking)
00529: - **Audit Focus:** Auditor specifically looks for "missing" data (what should exist but doesn't?)
00530: - **Recovery Ladder:** Selective suppression = Step 2 violation, escalates to Step 3-4 if systematic
00531: 
00532: ### 6.4 Pitfall 4: "Gaming the Metrics"
00533: 
00534: **Symptom:** Organization optimizes for S.V.E. compliance metrics while violating spirit.
