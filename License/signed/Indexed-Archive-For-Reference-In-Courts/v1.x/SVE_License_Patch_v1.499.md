## CORE MAXIMS

### 1. The Lighthouse Principle
**"Academia is humanity's lighthouse - to guide, it must be visible."**

Science exists to illuminate truth for all. When research becomes opaque:
- Ships (society) crash on rocks (bad policy/products)
- Lighthouse keepers (researchers) lose credibility
- Darkness (ignorance) prevails

**Therefore:** Academic use of S.V.E. = highest transparency standard.

### 2. Reproducibility Imperative
**"If it cannot be reproduced, it is not science."**

S.V.E. in academic context requires:
- Full methodology disclosure
- Data availability (privacy-compliant)
- Code/weights publication
- Null results published (no p-hacking)

### 3. Earnings & Conflicts
**"The lighthouse keeper serves the sea, not the merchants."**

When S.V.E. used in academic research:

**Permitted:**
- Salary from institution
- Grant funding (disclosed sources)
- Consulting ≤20% time (disclosed clients)
- Book royalties (academic press)

**Prohibited while claiming S.V.E. academic use:**
- Equity in companies using your S.V.E. research (>5%)
- Non-disclosed corporate funding
- IP licensing that restricts reproduction
- Advisory roles with undisclosed conflicts

**Gray Zone (requires Veche approval):**
- Founding startup based on S.V.E. research
- Consulting for industry using S.V.E.
- Patent filing (if open-licensed)

## ACADEMIC TIER FRAMEWORK

### Tier A: Pure Research
- University-funded, no commercial ties
- Full immediate transparency
- Enhanced citation rights
- S.V.E. Fund research grants available

**Benefits:**
- 0% commercialization restrictions
- Full S.V.E. Family support
- Priority dispute resolution

### Tier B: Applied Research
- Industry partnerships disclosed
- 6-month publication embargo (max)
- Commercial applications transparent
- Conflict register maintained

**Requirements:**
- Annual conflict disclosure
- Public methodology (post-embargo)
- No trade secret claims on S.V.E. components

### Tier C: Commercial Academic
- Spin-offs, licensing, equity involved
- Full transparency + 12-month delay permitted
- Subject to commercial tiers (Appendix B v1.4)
- Must not claim "pure research" status

**Obligations:**
- Clear labeling: "Commercial academic research"
- Separate S.V.E. academic from commercial use
- Higher Recovery Ladder liability if opacity detected

## WAIVER OF SECRECY (Academic Context)

**Academic use of S.V.E. = irrevocable waiver of:**
- Trade secrets (entire research program)
- Commercial confidentiality (on S.V.E. components)
- Publication restrictions (beyond ethical review)
- Proprietary claims on methodology

**Exception:** Human subjects data (GDPR/IRB compliant anonymization required, not prohibition)

## ANTI-BYPASS IN ACADEMIA

**Prohibited:**
- "Black box" model deployment without weights
- API-only access claiming "proprietary optimization"
- Selective publication (only positive results)
- Ghost-writing by industry (undisclosed)
- Weaponized peer review (blocking competitors)

**Required:**
- Pre-registration (hypothesis, methods)
- Null result publication
- Raw data availability (privacy-compliant repositories)
- Correspondence disclosure (FOI-ready)

## ENFORCEMENT

### Academic Violations
If academic institution uses S.V.E. but violates transparency:

**Step 1:** Public notice (30 days to comply)  
**Step 2:** Recovery Ladder activation:
- Tier 1: Return grants + penalties
- Tier 2: Loss of S.V.E. academic benefits
- Tier 3: Public documentation + informant rewards

**Step 3:** Permanent ban from S.V.E. academic use

### Whistleblower Protection (Academic)
Researchers reporting violations in their institution:
- 4-8% informant rewards apply
- Academic freedom defense provided
- Evidence Fund (v1.47) available for decisive evidence
