[0034] [0035] ## UNITED STATES
[0036] [0037] ### 4. Promissory Estoppel
[0038] **Doctrine:** One cannot deny representations relied upon by others  
[0039] **Relevance:** Entity using S.V.E. v1.3 estopped from denying v1.4 obligations  
[0040] **Case:** *Hoffman v. Red Owl Stores* (1965) - reliance creates obligation
[0041] [0042] ### 5. Constructive Notice Doctrine
[0043] **Principle:** Public filing = legal notice to all  
[0044] **Application:** GitHub/IPFS publication + timestamping = constructive notice  
[0045] **Case:** *Tulsa Professional Collection v. Pope* (1988) - public records provide notice
[0046] [0047] ### 6. Trade Secret Waiver
[0048] **Doctrine:** Voluntary disclosure = trade secret lost  
[0049] **Application:** Using S.V.E. = waiver of secrecy  
[0050] **Case:** *Religious Technology Center v. Netcom* (1995) - public distribution destroys secrecy claim  
[0051] **Statute:** Defend Trade Secrets Act (18 U.S.C. § 1839) - requires reasonable secrecy efforts; S.V.E. use = abandonment
[0052] [0053] ### 7. Unconscionability (Contracts)
[0054] **S.V.E. Defense:** Not unconscionable because:  
[0055] - Transparency requirement disclosed upfront
[0056] - Voluntary adoption (can choose other licenses)
[0057] - Serves public interest (human dignity)
[0058] **Case:** *Williams v. Walker-Thomas Furniture* (1965) - unconscionability requires both surprise + oppression (S.V.E. has neither)
[0059] [0060] ### 8. GPL Enforcement Precedent
[0061] **Case:** *Artifex Software v. Hancom* (N.D. Cal. 2017)  
[0062] **Holding:** Copyright infringement for GPL violation  
[0063] **Application:** S.V.E. share-alike = copyright condition; violation = infringement

[0171] [0172] ## ARGUMENTS S.V.E. CAN MAKE IN COURT
[0173] [0174] ### Retroactivity is Valid Because:
[0175] 1. **Clarification, not new obligation** (*Fairstar* precedent)
[0176] 2. **Constructive notice provided** (public timestamping)
[0177] 3. **Original license reserved right to evolve** (v1.3 Clause 8)
[0178] 4. **Estoppel applies** (benefited from v1.3, cannot deny v1.4)
[0179] [0180] ### Transparency Requirement is Enforceable Because:
[0181] 1. **Copyright condition** (*Jacobsen* precedent)
[0182] 2. **Voluntary adoption** (no unconscionability)
[0183] 3. **Public interest** (human dignity protection)
[0184] 4. **EU fundamental rights** (GDPR alignment)
[0185] [0186] ### Trade Secret Waiver is Binding Because:
[0187] 1. **Clear term in license** (Waiver of Secrecy clause)
[0188] 2. **Public use = abandonment** (*Religious Technology Center*)
[0189] 3. **Cannot claim both S.V.E. benefit AND secrecy** (doctrinal contradiction)
