[0015] [0016] ## CORE MAXIMS
[0017] [0018] ### 1. The Lighthouse Principle
[0019] **"Academia is humanity's lighthouse - to guide, it must be visible."**
[0020] [0021] Science exists to illuminate truth for all. When research becomes opaque:
[0022] - Ships (society) crash on rocks (bad policy/products)
[0023] - Lighthouse keepers (researchers) lose credibility
[0024] - Darkness (ignorance) prevails
[0025] [0026] **Therefore:** Academic use of S.V.E. = highest transparency standard.
[0027] [0028] ### 2. Reproducibility Imperative
[0029] **"If it cannot be reproduced, it is not science."**
[0030] [0031] S.V.E. in academic context requires:
[0032] - Full methodology disclosure
[0033] - Data availability (privacy-compliant)
[0034] - Code/weights publication
[0035] - Null results published (no p-hacking)
[0036] [0037] ### 3. Earnings & Conflicts
[0038] **"The lighthouse keeper serves the sea, not the merchants."**
[0039] [0040] When S.V.E. used in academic research:
[0041] [0042] **Permitted:**
[0043] - Salary from institution
[0044] - Grant funding (disclosed sources)
[0045] - Consulting ≤20% time (disclosed clients)
[0046] - Book royalties (academic press)
[0047] [0048] **Prohibited while claiming S.V.E. academic use:**
[0049] - Equity in companies using your S.V.E. research (>5%)
[0050] - Non-disclosed corporate funding
[0051] - IP licensing that restricts reproduction
[0052] - Advisory roles with undisclosed conflicts
[0053] [0054] **Gray Zone (requires Veche approval):**
[0055] - Founding startup based on S.V.E. research
[0056] - Consulting for industry using S.V.E.
[0057] - Patent filing (if open-licensed)
