### 1. Context (The Threat)

SVE enforcement depends on legal representation. Without clear incentives, attorneys may avoid SVE cases (novel, untested) or — worse — represent violators while claiming SVE expertise. The framework needs attorneys as immune cells, not parasites.

### 3. Interpretation (The Precedent)

**3.1 · Attorney Compensation Model (Escalating Shares)**

Attorneys representing SVE interests (victims, community, whistleblowers) receive a share of recovered funds that *increases* with the violator's resistance. This creates a structural incentive for violators to settle early and comply — the longer they fight, the more expensive it becomes.

| Phase | Trigger | Attorney Share of Recovery |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-litigation | Violator corrects after notice | 10% |
| Filing | Formal complaint filed | 15% |
| Discovery | Litigation proceeds past discovery | 20% |
| Trial | Case goes to trial | 25% |
| Appeal | Violator appeals | 30% |

The remaining recovery is distributed per standard allocation (whistleblower → victims → foundation → public good), with percentages recalculated on the reduced pool.

**3.2 · Conflict of Interest (The Bright Line)**

An attorney who has represented SVE enforcement interests may **never** subsequently represent an entity accused of SVE violation, and vice versa. This is absolute. "Chinese walls" within a firm are insufficient — the entire firm is bound.

Violation of this rule = independent SVE breach (Tier 2), regardless of the underlying case outcome.

**3.3 · Pro Bono Priority**

Attorneys who represent SVE victims or whistleblowers pro bono (zero upfront fee) receive 1.5x multiplier on their share of recovery. This ensures the most vulnerable have access to the strongest advocates.

**3.4 · The "Escalating Commitment" Deterrent**

The escalating share model is not a punishment for litigation — it is a *mirror*. A violator who genuinely believes they are compliant will settle early (low cost). A violator who fights to delay, exhaust, or outlast the community reveals — through their own behavior — the depth of their violation. The escalation is proportional to demonstrated bad faith.

This aligns with Core Principle 5 (Truth & Love): truth-seeking should be rewarded; obstruction should be costly.

### 4. Verdict

- **Finding:** This case establishes the SVE Legal Representation Model as binding.
- **Scope:** Applies to all SVE enforcement actions from this date forward.
- **Override:** Attorney shares may be adjusted by Veche vote (2/3 majority) for specific cases involving extreme hardship or public interest.
