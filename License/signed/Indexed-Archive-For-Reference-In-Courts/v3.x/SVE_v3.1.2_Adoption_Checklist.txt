[0001] # S.V.E. v3.0 Adoption Checklist
[0002] **Pre-Adoption Assessment Tool • Phase 0-1 • Allow 2-3 hours**
[0003] [0004] ---
[0005] [0006] ## Instructions
[0007] [0008] This checklist helps you determine:
[0009] 1. **Compatibility** — Is S.V.E. right for your organization?
[0010] 2. **Readiness** — Can you adopt successfully now, or do you need preparation?
[0011] 3. **Scope** — Which Commercial Tier (if any) fits your use case?
[0012] [0013] **Scoring:**
[0014] - ✅ **Green** = Ready to proceed
[0015] - ⚠️ **Yellow** = Caution — address concerns before adopting
[0016] - ❌ **Red** = Not compatible — do not adopt yet
[0017] [0018] ---
[0019] [0020] ## Part 1: Compatibility Assessment
[0021] [0022] ### 1.1 Transparency Capacity
[0023] [0024] **Can your organization operate with default transparency?**
[0025] [0026] - [ ] ✅ Most operations are public or could be with minimal effort
[0027] - [ ] ⚠️ Significant confidentiality needs (IP, legal, safety) but can document justifications
[0028] - [ ] ❌ Core business model requires permanent secrecy (intelligence, certain defense contractors)
[0029] [0030] **Do you currently publish any transparency reports?**
[0031] [0032] - [ ] ✅ Yes, regularly (annual reports, audits, etc.)
[0033] - [ ] ⚠️ Occasionally or only when required by law
[0034] - [ ] ❌ No, and we actively conceal information
[0035] [0036] **Score:** If any ❌, STOP — S.V.E. not compatible. If ⚠️, see Section 6 (Remediation).
[0037] [0038] ---
[0039] [0040] ### 1.2 Leadership Commitment
[0041] [0042] **Are senior leaders genuinely willing to be held accountable?**
[0043] [0044] - [ ] ✅ Yes, leadership models vulnerability and welcomes scrutiny
[0045] - [ ] ⚠️ Leadership is skeptical but willing to learn
[0046] - [ ] ❌ Leadership demands exemption from accountability
[0047] [0048] **If a serious mistake were discovered tomorrow, would leadership:**
[0049] [0050] - [ ] ✅ Disclose immediately, make restitution, fix systems
[0051] - [ ] ⚠️ Delay disclosure for legal review but ultimately publish
[0052] - [ ] ❌ Suppress information to protect reputation/stock price
[0053] [0054] **Has leadership read at least the S.V.E. Core Summary (30 min)?**
[0055] [0056] - [ ] ✅ Yes, multiple executives have read it
[0057] - [ ] ⚠️ One person read it and briefed others
[0058] - [ ] ❌ No one has read it yet
[0059] [0060] **Score:** If any ❌, STOP. If 2+ ⚠️, leadership training required before Phase 1.
[0061] [0062] ---
[0063] [0064] ### 1.3 Financial Stability
[0065] [0066] **Current organizational reserves:**
[0067] [0068] - [ ] ✅ $50M+ (can absorb Step 2-3 penalties if needed)
[0069] - [ ] ⚠️ $10M-$50M (Step 1-2 penalties manageable, Step 3+ existential)
[0070] - [ ] ❌ <$10M (any penalty could threaten survival)
[0071] [0072] **Can you budget 2-5% of operations for S.V.E. infrastructure?**
[0073] [0074] - [ ] ✅ Yes, already budgeted or easily allocated
[0075] - [ ] ⚠️ Tight but feasible with reallocation
[0076] - [ ] ❌ No, budget is fully committed
[0077] [0078] **Do you have D&O (Directors & Officers) insurance ≥$50M?**
[0079] [0080] - [ ] ✅ Yes, $50M+ coverage
[0081] - [ ] ⚠️ Yes, but <$50M (consider increasing)
[0082] - [ ] ❌ No D&O insurance
[0083] [0084] **Score:** If ❌ on reserves + ❌ on budget, STOP. Consider Phase 0 fundraising first.
[0085] [0086] ---
[0087] [0088] ### 1.4 Cultural Fit
[0089] [0090] **How are whistleblowers/truth-tellers currently treated?**
[0091] [0092] - [ ] ✅ Celebrated and protected (recent examples exist)
[0093] - [ ] ⚠️ Tolerated but not rewarded
[0094] - [ ] ❌ Punished, marginalized, or fired
[0095] [0096] **When mistakes happen, does your organization:**
[0097] [0098] - [ ] ✅ Conduct transparent post-mortems and publish learnings
[0099] - [ ] ⚠️ Internal review only, selective external communication
[0100] - [ ] ❌ Hide errors, blame individuals, move on
[0101] [0102] **Anonymous staff survey: "I can speak truth to power without fear"**
[0103] [0104] - [ ] ✅ 70%+ agree
[0105] - [ ] ⚠️ 40-70% agree
[0106] - [ ] ❌ <40% agree (or no data)
[0107] [0108] **Score:** If 2+ ❌, culture change required before S.V.E. adoption (6-12 months minimum).

[0232] [0233] ## Part 4: Risk Assessment
[0234] [0235] ### 4.1 Adversarial Threats
[0236] [0237] **Could S.V.E. adoption expose you to:**
[0238] [0239] - [ ] Competitive disadvantage (revealing trade secrets)
[0240] - [ ] Legal liability (disclosing past harms)
[0241] - [ ] Physical threats (authoritarian government persecution)
[0242] - [ ] Reputational damage (skeletons in closet)
[0243] [0244] **If any checked, mitigation strategies needed:**
[0245] [0246] 1. **Trade secrets:** Disclose *impact* and *purpose*, not *mechanism*
[0247] 2. **Legal liability:** Consult counsel; restorative justice often cheaper than litigation
[0248] 3. **Physical threats:** Consider underground S.V.E. implementation (encrypted, exiled governance)
[0249] 4. **Reputation:** Truth revealed on your terms > truth leaked by adversaries
[0250] [0251] ---
[0252] [0253] ### 4.2 Recovery Ladder Exposure
[0254] [0255] **Honestly assess: In past 5 years, have you:**
[0256] [0257] - [ ] Delayed disclosing material information beyond legal requirements (Step 1)
[0258] - [ ] Suppressed evidence of harm or failure (Step 2)
[0259] - [ ] Covered up institutional mistakes that harmed stakeholders (Step 3)
[0260] - [ ] Concealed catastrophic risks or outcomes (Step 4)
[0261] - [ ] Engaged in systemic fraud or corruption (Step 5)
[0262] [0263] **If any checked:**
[0264] [0265] 1. **Option A (Recommended):** Disclose past harms proactively *before* adopting S.V.E. (restorative justice)
[0266] 2. **Option B:** Adopt S.V.E. and immediately trigger Recovery Ladder (pay penalties, make restitution)
[0267] 3. **Option C:** Do not adopt S.V.E. until past issues resolved
[0268] [0269] **Warning:** False S.V.E. adoption (claiming compliance without fixing past harms) = Step 2-3 violation.

[0298] [0299] ## Part 6: Remediation Pathways
[0300] [0301] ### If Transparency ❌
[0302] - **Action:** Hire transparency consultant, implement basic disclosure policies
[0303] - **Timeline:** 6-12 months
[0304] - **Cost:** $50K-$150K
[0305] - **Outcome:** Move to ⚠️, reassess
[0306] [0307] ### If Leadership ❌
[0308] - **Action:** Executive coaching on accountability, board governance training
[0309] - **Timeline:** 3-6 months
[0310] - **Cost:** $25K-$75K
[0311] - **Outcome:** Leadership commitment or resignation
[0312] [0313] ### If Financial ❌
[0314] - **Action:** Fundraising, reserve-building, insurance acquisition
[0315] - **Timeline:** 6-12 months
[0316] - **Cost:** Depends on gap
[0317] - **Outcome:** $10M+ reserves minimum
[0318] [0319] ### If Culture ❌
[0320] - **Action:** Culture transformation program (psychological safety, truth-telling norms)
[0321] - **Timeline:** 12-24 months
[0322] - **Cost:** $100K-$500K (external facilitation)
[0323] - **Outcome:** Staff survey >70% "can speak truth to power"
