**PART I: CORE PRINCIPLES (IMMUTABLE)**

**1.1 Transparency Principle**

**Material decisions affecting others must be disclosed within defined timeframes.**

* **Organizational decisions** (strategy, hiring, major expenditures, policy changes) disclosed within 30â€“365 days depending on regime
* **Rationale published** explaining the reasoning, alternatives considered, and expected outcomes

* **Exception disclosure deadline** maximum 365 days after harm resolved; full transparency thereafter

**Application:**

* Government agencies: Budget decisions, policy rationales, regulatory changes
* Healthcare systems: Treatment outcome data, medication errors, billing structures
* Technology companies: Algorithmic optimization targets, content moderation rules, data practices
* Nonprofits: Board decisions, grant allocations, strategic pivots

**Prohibited:**
