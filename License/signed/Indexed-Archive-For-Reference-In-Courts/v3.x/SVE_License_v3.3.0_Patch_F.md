# S.V.E. PATCH F — FOUNDERS, FAMILY, & SUCCESSION
## (Stability Without Control)

**Patch ID:** F  
**Applies To:** S.V.E. v3.0+  
**Type:** Structural  
**Status:** Mandatory

---

## F.1 Financial Security

Founders and designated family members SHALL receive:
- Fixed endowment
- Indexed periodic support
- Survivorship guarantees

---

## F.2 Non-Domination Clause

Financial security:
- Confers no governance power
- Confers no interpretive authority
- Confers no enforcement immunity

---

## F.3 Succession Automation

In case of death or incapacity:
- Trust execution is automatic
- Governance proceeds without interruption
- No discretionary override allowed

---

## F.4 Ceiling & Transparency

Upper bounds MAY exist but must be:
- Explicit
- Auditable
- Non-arbitrary

---
