# S.V.E. PATCH B — VERSION SYNTHESIS & ARMORY
## (Retroactivity + Historical Protection)

**Patch ID:** B  
**Applies To:** S.V.E. v3.0+  
**Type:** Interpretive / Structural  
**Status:** Mandatory

---

## B.1 Living Archive Principle

All prior S.V.E. materials (signed, unsigned, draft, abandoned) constitute a **Protective Armory**.

They are:
- Non-operative by default
- Admissible when stronger

---

## B.2 Maximum Protection Rule

In any conflict, the system SHALL apply the provision that maximizes:

1. Spirit alignment  
2. Protection of vulnerable parties  
3. Transparency and accountability  

regardless of version number or signature status.

---

## B.3 Hash Registry

All historical documents MAY be:
- Hashed
- Timestamped
- Listed in the public registry

Hash inclusion implies **admissibility**, not activation.

---

## B.4 Retroactive Binding

Any claim of S.V.E. usage or compliance implies consent to:
- This Patch
- Retroactive synthesis
- Strongest-available interpretation

---

## B.5 No Selective Downgrading

Entities MAY NOT choose weaker historical clauses when stronger ones exist.
