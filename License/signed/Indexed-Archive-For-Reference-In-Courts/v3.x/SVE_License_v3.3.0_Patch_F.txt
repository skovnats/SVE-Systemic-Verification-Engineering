[0001] # S.V.E. PATCH F — FOUNDERS, FAMILY, & SUCCESSION
[0002] ## (Stability Without Control)
[0003] [0004] **Patch ID:** F  
[0005] **Applies To:** S.V.E. v3.0+  
[0006] **Type:** Structural  
[0007] **Status:** Mandatory
[0008] [0009] ---
[0010] [0011] ## F.1 Financial Security
[0012] [0013] Founders and designated family members SHALL receive:
[0014] - Fixed endowment
[0015] - Indexed periodic support
[0016] - Survivorship guarantees
[0017] [0018] ---
[0019] [0020] ## F.2 Non-Domination Clause
[0021] [0022] Financial security:
[0023] - Confers no governance power
[0024] - Confers no interpretive authority
[0025] - Confers no enforcement immunity
[0026] [0027] ---
[0028] [0029] ## F.3 Succession Automation
[0030] [0031] In case of death or incapacity:
[0032] - Trust execution is automatic
[0033] - Governance proceeds without interruption
[0034] - No discretionary override allowed
[0035] [0036] ---
[0037] [0038] ## F.4 Ceiling & Transparency
[0039] [0040] Upper bounds MAY exist but must be:
[0041] - Explicit
[0042] - Auditable
[0043] - Non-arbitrary
[0044] [0045] ---
