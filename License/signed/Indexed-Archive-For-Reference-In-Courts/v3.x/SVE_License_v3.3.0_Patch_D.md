# S.V.E. PATCH D — OPERATIONAL REGIMES
## (Compressed Regime System)

**Patch ID:** D  
**Applies To:** S.V.E. v3.0+  
**Type:** Operational  
**Status:** Mandatory

---

## D.1 Regimes

Four regimes exist:

1. **Normal** — full transparency  
2. **Defense** — limited delay/redaction  
3. **Survival** — temporary secrecy for life safety  
4. **War** — existential threat only  

---

## D.2 Activation

Regime escalation requires:
- Objective trigger criteria
- Logged declaration
- Automatic expiry timer

---

## D.3 De-escalation

All regimes auto-degrade unless re-justified.

Failure to de-escalate = breach.

---

## D.4 Post-Audit Requirement

Every non-Normal regime mandates:
- Full retrospective audit
- Public justification
- Nullification of illegitimate acts
