[0001] # S.V.E. PATCH D — OPERATIONAL REGIMES
[0002] ## (Compressed Regime System)
[0003] [0004] **Patch ID:** D  
[0005] **Applies To:** S.V.E. v3.0+  
[0006] **Type:** Operational  
[0007] **Status:** Mandatory
[0008] [0009] ---
[0010] [0011] ## D.1 Regimes
[0012] [0013] Four regimes exist:
[0014] [0015] 1. **Normal** — full transparency  
[0016] 2. **Defense** — limited delay/redaction  
[0017] 3. **Survival** — temporary secrecy for life safety  
[0018] 4. **War** — existential threat only  
[0019] [0020] ---
[0021] [0022] ## D.2 Activation
[0023] [0024] Regime escalation requires:
[0025] - Objective trigger criteria
[0026] - Logged declaration
[0027] - Automatic expiry timer
[0028] [0029] ---
[0030] [0031] ## D.3 De-escalation
[0032] [0033] All regimes auto-degrade unless re-justified.
[0034] [0035] Failure to de-escalate = breach.
[0036] [0037] ---
[0038] [0039] ## D.4 Post-Audit Requirement
[0040] [0041] Every non-Normal regime mandates:
[0042] - Full retrospective audit
[0043] - Public justification
[0044] - Nullification of illegitimate acts
[0045] [0046] ---
