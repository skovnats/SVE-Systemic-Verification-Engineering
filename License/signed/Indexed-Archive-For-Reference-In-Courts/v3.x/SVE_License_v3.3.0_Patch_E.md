# S.V.E. PATCH E — ENFORCEMENT & ECONOMICS
## (Game-Theoretic Engine)

**Patch ID:** E  
**Applies To:** S.V.E. v3.0+  
**Type:** Operational  
**Status:** Mandatory

---

## E.1 Recovery Ladder

Violations are addressed in escalating steps:
1. Disclosure
2. Restitution
3. Penalty
4. Structural exclusion

Restoration path MUST exist.

---

## E.2 Whistleblower Incentives

The system SHALL guarantee:
- Positive expected value for truthful reporting
- Protection against retaliation

Exact percentages MAY vary but cannot reach zero.

---

## E.3 Anti-Judas Rule

False accusations:
- Carry higher cost than silence
- Yield negative expected value
- Trigger sanctions proportionate to harm

---

## E.4 Life-Critical Zero Tolerance

In domains affecting life or safety:
- Concealment = maximum severity
- Negligence ≠ excuse
- Profit from harm prohibited
