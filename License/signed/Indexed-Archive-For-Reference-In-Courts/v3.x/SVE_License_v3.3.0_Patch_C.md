[0010] [0011] ## C.1 Bodies
[0012] [0013] Recognized bodies MAY include:
[0014] - Veche (human collective)
[0015] - AI validators
[0016] - Custodians
[0017] - Temporary emergency roles
[0018] [0019] No body is permanent.
[0020] [0021] ---
[0022] [0023] ## C.2 Voting Thresholds (Default)
[0024] [0025] - Routine decisions: >50%  
[0026] - Patch adoption: ≥67%  
[0027] - Emergency escalation: ≥51% + audit  
[0028] - Spirit-related disputes: Patch A governs
[0029] [0030] Thresholds MAY NOT be lowered by Patch.
[0031] [0032] ---
[0033] [0034] ## C.3 Anti-Capture Mechanisms
[0035] [0036] Mandatory features:
[0037] - Term limits
[0038] - Randomized sub-juries
[0039] - Delayed execution for high-impact decisions
[0040] - Automatic quorum decay (prevents stagnation)
[0041] [0042] ---
[0043] [0044] ## C.4 Founder Limitation
[0045] [0046] The Founder:
[0047] - Has no veto
[0048] - Has no unilateral control
[0049] - Is subject to recall and audit
[0050] [0051] ---
