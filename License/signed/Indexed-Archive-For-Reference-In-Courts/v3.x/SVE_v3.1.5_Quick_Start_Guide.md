## What is S.V.E.?

**Systemic Verification Engineering (S.V.E.)** is a transparency and accountability framework that turns ethical principles into enforceable systems. Think of it as "open-source governance meets restorative justice."

### Core Promise
Organizations that adopt S.V.E. commit to:
- **Operating transparently** (with justified timing delays)
- **Paying proportional penalties** when they cause verified harm
- **Distributing most penalties to victims** (not keeping them)
- **Submitting to independent verification**

---

## The 3 Big Ideas

### 1. Recovery Ladder (5-Step Accountability)
When someone discovers organizational harm, they climb a ladder:

| Step | Violation | Penalty Range | Example |
|------|-----------|---------------|---------|
| **1** | Minor delay/omission | $500K-$1M | Missed disclosure deadline by 2 weeks |
| **2** | Suppression of evidence | $1M-$10M | Hid unfavorable test results |
| **3** | Institutional malfeasance | $10M-$100M | Executive cover-up of safety issues |
| **4** | Catastrophic harm | $100M-$1B | Concealed drug side effects → deaths |
| **5** | Existential threat | $1B+ | Systemic fraud threatening public health |

**Key:** 40-80% goes to informant/victims, rest to prevention programs.

### 2. Commercial Tiers (Pay for Verified Value)
If you use S.V.E. commercially, you pay % of *verified benefit*:

- **Tier 11** (11%): Single-project pilot
- **Tier 22** (22%): Standard multi-project use
- **Tier 33** (33%): Multi-domain integration
- **Tier 44** (44%): Mission-critical, company-wide

No verified value = no payment (but must document honestly).

### 3. Distributed Governance (No Single Owner)
- **Veche** = Community assembly (67% supermajority for major decisions)
- **31-AI Consensus** = Jesus + Socrates + Perelman + Ivan-Durak validate Spirit-alignment
- **Keeper** = Emergency role (only activates in crisis)
- **Dual jurisdiction** = Estonia + Switzerland for legal enforcement

- Already operate with reasonable transparency
- Want competitive advantage via trust
- Have leadership willing to be accountable
- Can afford 2-5% budget for compliance infrastructure

### ⚠️ Proceed With Caution If...
- Core business requires legitimate secrecy (defense, IP-dependent)
- Leadership fears scrutiny
- Financial reserves < $10M (penalties could be existential)
- Culture is "image management over truth"

### ❌ Don't Adopt If...
- You plan to violate principles (will trigger Recovery Ladder)
- Shareholders demand profit-over-ethics
- You want the label without the work (false claims → Step 2-3 penalty)

## Common Misconceptions

### "S.V.E. means no confidentiality"
**False.** You can delay disclosures for legitimate reasons (legal strategy, safety, embargoes). You just must document *why* and *when* you'll disclose.

### "Recovery Ladder penalties are bankruptcy risk"
**Partially true.** Step 4-5 penalties *can* be existential — that's the point. Don't cause catastrophic harm. Steps 1-3 are proportional to reserves.

### "Only nonprofits can adopt S.V.E."
**False.** For-profit companies can use S.V.E. They just pay Commercial Tier fees and prioritize universal benefit alongside profit.

### "It's a Christian framework"
**Partially true.** Gospel-grounded but non-sectarian. Secular equivalents provided (Kant's categorical imperative, Rawls' veil of ignorance, Ubuntu, etc.).

## Legal Enforceability

### How S.V.E. is Binding
1. **Voluntary adoption** → Promissory estoppel (you made a public promise)
2. **Commercial Tier contracts** → Explicit agreement (written, signed)
3. **Public representations** → Consumer protection laws (false advertising)
4. **Industry standards** → Regulatory incorporation (if S.V.E. becomes standard of care)

3. **Judicial** (1-3 years) — Courts enforce arbitration awards (New York Convention)
