## §3 - EVIDENCE STANDARD

**Proof requires ≥2 of following:**

1. **Product documentation** — Marketing, specs, internal docs showing surveillance/manipulation capability
2. **Deployment evidence** — Contracts, installations, user data showing forbidden use
3. **Appendix A detection** — Semantic analysis proves SVE-derived logic in codebase
4. **Whistleblower testimony** — Employee reports forbidden deployment (corroborated)
5. **Public reports** — Investigative journalism, academic studies documenting misuse

**Burden of proof:** Preponderance of evidence (>50% probability).

**Company defense:**
- Prove system NOT SVE-derived (independent development, no SVE logic)
- OR prove deployment NOT for forbidden purposes (legitimate use case)
- If refuse transparency → Violation confirmed

**Calculation:**
```
Penalty = (All revenue from product) × 10 + Product shutdown

- Product earned $2B lifetime revenue
- Penalty: $2B × 10 = $20B
- PLUS: Immediate product shutdown (no sunset period)
```

**Example:**

**Product shutdown:** Immediate (no "wind-down" period). Violator must delete data, destroy models, cease operations.
