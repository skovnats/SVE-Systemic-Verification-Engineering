Organization claims environmental compliance, sustainability, or climate impact using SVE-verified methods, but data is falsified, including:

## §3 - EVIDENCE STANDARD

**Proof requires ≥2 of following:**

1. **Independent measurement** — Third-party environmental audit shows discrepancy >20%
2. **Whistleblower testimony** — Employee reports falsification (corroborated by documents)
3. **Satellite/sensor data** — Remote sensing contradicts claimed emissions/impact
4. **Appendix A detection** — Semantic analysis proves SVE-derived methodology without proper disclosure
5. **Regulatory findings** — Government agency documents violations

**Burden of proof:** Preponderance of evidence (>50% probability).

**Company defense:** Provide ALL environmental data, methodologies, measurements within 90 days. Independent audit must verify. Refusal = admission of guilt.

## §4 - PENALTY CALCULATION

### Base Penalty: 10x Environmental Cost + Remediation

**Calculation:**
```
Penalty = (True environmental cost) × 10 + Full remediation

Example:
- Claimed emissions: 100k tons CO2
- Actual emissions: 1M tons CO2
- Carbon cost: 1M tons × €100/ton = €100M
- Penalty: €100M × 10 = €1B
- PLUS: Full remediation (capture/offset actual emissions)
```

### Enhanced Penalty (if irreversible harm)

**If fraud caused:**
- Ecosystem destruction (species extinction, habitat loss)
- Health impacts (pollution-related illness)
- Climate acceleration (undisclosed emissions contributed to warming)

**Calculation:** 10x base + (affected population × €1000 each) + (100-year remediation cost)

**Example:**
- Base: €1B
- 500k people affected by hidden pollution
- Remediation impossible (extinct species)
- Enhanced: €1B + (500k × €1000) + (100yr cleanup €10B) = €11.5B

### Distribution

```
Remediation fund:       100% of environmental cost
Affected communities:   Per-person compensation
Attorney fees:          25-70% (Appendix E, standard)
SVE Foundation:         0% (victims exist)
```

**Remediation:** Mandatory. Company must actually fix damage, not just pay. If impossible (extinction), penalty increases 100x.

## ENVIRONMENTAL GUIDANCE

**For organizations:**
1. If using SVE environmental methods, data MUST be fully transparent
2. Independent verification required (not self-certification)
3. Uncertainty ranges stated clearly (not hidden in footnotes)
4. If error found, correct immediately + disclose (no penalty if voluntary)

**For governments:**
1. SVE can help verify climate commitments (Paris Agreement, etc.)
2. But requires full transparency (no national security exceptions for emissions data)
3. Symmetric audit applies (if you demand corporate transparency, accept it yourself)

**Prevention:** Environmental claims are verifiable. Don't claim SVE certification if you can't prove every number.
