# S.V.E. v3.0 Adoption Checklist
**Pre-Adoption Assessment Tool • Phase 0-1 • Allow 2-3 hours**

---

## Instructions

This checklist helps you determine:
1. **Compatibility** — Is S.V.E. right for your organization?
2. **Readiness** — Can you adopt successfully now, or do you need preparation?
3. **Scope** — Which Commercial Tier (if any) fits your use case?

**Scoring:**
- ✅ **Green** = Ready to proceed
- ⚠️ **Yellow** = Caution — address concerns before adopting
- ❌ **Red** = Not compatible — do not adopt yet

---

## Part 1: Compatibility Assessment

### 1.1 Transparency Capacity

**Can your organization operate with default transparency?**

- [ ] ✅ Most operations are public or could be with minimal effort
- [ ] ⚠️ Significant confidentiality needs (IP, legal, safety) but can document justifications
- [ ] ❌ Core business model requires permanent secrecy (intelligence, certain defense contractors)

**Do you currently publish any transparency reports?**

- [ ] ✅ Yes, regularly (annual reports, audits, etc.)
- [ ] ⚠️ Occasionally or only when required by law
- [ ] ❌ No, and we actively conceal information

**Score:** If any ❌, STOP — S.V.E. not compatible. If ⚠️, see Section 6 (Remediation).

---

### 1.2 Leadership Commitment

**Are senior leaders genuinely willing to be held accountable?**

- [ ] ✅ Yes, leadership models vulnerability and welcomes scrutiny
- [ ] ⚠️ Leadership is skeptical but willing to learn
- [ ] ❌ Leadership demands exemption from accountability

**If a serious mistake were discovered tomorrow, would leadership:**

- [ ] ✅ Disclose immediately, make restitution, fix systems
- [ ] ⚠️ Delay disclosure for legal review but ultimately publish
- [ ] ❌ Suppress information to protect reputation/stock price

**Has leadership read at least the S.V.E. Core Summary (30 min)?**

- [ ] ✅ Yes, multiple executives have read it
- [ ] ⚠️ One person read it and briefed others
- [ ] ❌ No one has read it yet

**Score:** If any ❌, STOP. If 2+ ⚠️, leadership training required before Phase 1.

---

### 1.3 Financial Stability

**Current organizational reserves:**

- [ ] ✅ $50M+ (can absorb Step 2-3 penalties if needed)
- [ ] ⚠️ $10M-$50M (Step 1-2 penalties manageable, Step 3+ existential)
- [ ] ❌ <$10M (any penalty could threaten survival)

**Can you budget 2-5% of operations for S.V.E. infrastructure?**

- [ ] ✅ Yes, already budgeted or easily allocated
- [ ] ⚠️ Tight but feasible with reallocation
- [ ] ❌ No, budget is fully committed

**Do you have D&O (Directors & Officers) insurance ≥$50M?**

- [ ] ✅ Yes, $50M+ coverage
- [ ] ⚠️ Yes, but <$50M (consider increasing)
- [ ] ❌ No D&O insurance

**Score:** If ❌ on reserves + ❌ on budget, STOP. Consider Phase 0 fundraising first.

---

### 1.4 Cultural Fit

**How are whistleblowers/truth-tellers currently treated?**

- [ ] ✅ Celebrated and protected (recent examples exist)
- [ ] ⚠️ Tolerated but not rewarded
- [ ] ❌ Punished, marginalized, or fired

**When mistakes happen, does your organization:**

- [ ] ✅ Conduct transparent post-mortems and publish learnings
- [ ] ⚠️ Internal review only, selective external communication
- [ ] ❌ Hide errors, blame individuals, move on

**Anonymous staff survey: "I can speak truth to power without fear"**

- [ ] ✅ 70%+ agree
- [ ] ⚠️ 40-70% agree
- [ ] ❌ <40% agree (or no data)

**Score:** If 2+ ❌, culture change required before S.V.E. adoption (6-12 months minimum).

### 3.2 Commercial Tier Selection

**Based on scope above, recommended tier:**

| Your Scope | Recommended Tier | Success Fee | Verification Level |
|------------|------------------|-------------|-------------------|
| 1 pilot project | **Tier 11** | 11% verified value | Self-verified (quarterly) |

## Part 4: Risk Assessment

### 4.1 Adversarial Threats

**Could S.V.E. adoption expose you to:**

- [ ] Competitive disadvantage (revealing trade secrets)
- [ ] Legal liability (disclosing past harms)
- [ ] Physical threats (authoritarian government persecution)
- [ ] Reputational damage (skeletons in closet)

**If any checked, mitigation strategies needed:**

1. **Trade secrets:** Disclose *impact* and *purpose*, not *mechanism*
2. **Legal liability:** Consult counsel; restorative justice often cheaper than litigation
3. **Physical threats:** Consider underground S.V.E. implementation (encrypted, exiled governance)
4. **Reputation:** Truth revealed on your terms > truth leaked by adversaries

---

### 4.2 Recovery Ladder Exposure

**Honestly assess: In past 5 years, have you:**

- [ ] Delayed disclosing material information beyond legal requirements (Step 1)
- [ ] Suppressed evidence of harm or failure (Step 2)
- [ ] Covered up institutional mistakes that harmed stakeholders (Step 3)
- [ ] Concealed catastrophic risks or outcomes (Step 4)
- [ ] Engaged in systemic fraud or corruption (Step 5)

**If any checked:**

1. **Option A (Recommended):** Disclose past harms proactively *before* adopting S.V.E. (restorative justice)
2. **Option B:** Adopt S.V.E. and immediately trigger Recovery Ladder (pay penalties, make restitution)
3. **Option C:** Do not adopt S.V.E. until past issues resolved

**Warning:** False S.V.E. adoption (claiming compliance without fixing past harms) = Step 2-3 violation.

## Part 6: Remediation Pathways

### If Transparency ❌
- **Action:** Hire transparency consultant, implement basic disclosure policies
- **Timeline:** 6-12 months
- **Cost:** $50K-$150K
- **Outcome:** Move to ⚠️, reassess

### If Leadership ❌
- **Action:** Executive coaching on accountability, board governance training
- **Timeline:** 3-6 months
- **Cost:** $25K-$75K
- **Outcome:** Leadership commitment or resignation

### If Financial ❌
- **Action:** Fundraising, reserve-building, insurance acquisition
- **Timeline:** 6-12 months
- **Cost:** Depends on gap
- **Outcome:** $10M+ reserves minimum

### If Culture ❌
- **Action:** Culture transformation program (psychological safety, truth-telling norms)
- **Timeline:** 12-24 months
- **Cost:** $100K-$500K (external facilitation)
- **Outcome:** Staff survey >70% "can speak truth to power"

## Next Steps

### If Proceeding to Phase 1

1. **Schedule kickoff meeting** — S.V.E. coordinator, leadership, legal
2. **Download templates:**
   - Adoption Declaration
   - Compliance Log Template
   - Initial Audit RFP (Request for Proposal)
3. **Hire initial auditor** — Independent third-party or community-verified peer
4. **Set timeline:** 
   - Month 1-2: Audit
   - Month 3-4: Remediation
   - Month 5-6: Public Adoption Declaration + First Compliance Log

### If Not Ready

1. **Document gaps** — Use this checklist as roadmap
2. **Create remediation plan** — Timeline, budget, responsibilities
3. **Reassess in 6-12 months** — Repeat this checklist
